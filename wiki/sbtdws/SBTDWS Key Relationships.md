## 1. PG J7 — Defining Linkage

**Why defining:** SBTDWS provides the exercise execution capability. J7 provides the exercise programme. Without this relationship, SBTDWS has no pathway to FE@R contributions.

**What SBTDWS provides to J7:**
- EXCON teams
- Observer Trainer cadre
- Scenario design
- Validation serials
- J-function support to J7 exercise programme

**What J7 provides to SBTDWS:**
- Exercise calendar and tasking
- LVC architecture coordination
- Shared assets agreement

**Required formalisation:**
- Joint J3/J7/SBTDWS lane-definition engagement (ongoing, PREP phase)
- Force Generation Pipeline ToR (J3/J7/SBTDWS) — signed **Q2 26**
- MoU on LVC architecture, shared assets, and OT/HICON/EXCON design split

---

## 2. J3 WDC — Most Important Linkage

**Why most important:** ADWC is positioned as the field execution arm of J3 WDC's experimentation mandate. Without J3 WDC governance, SBTDWS has no authority to run FDC 5 Tactical Experimentation. The risk: parallel or competing experimentation efforts.

**What SBTDWS provides to J3 WDC:**
- Operational environment for experimentation
- Trained operators and facilities
- Executed sprint events (Makhlab 1 model)
- TTP and doctrine outputs

**What J3 WDC provides to SBTDWS:**
- FDC 5 governance framework
- Experimentation mandate
- Kill/continue decision authority
- Linkage to force design pipeline

**Required formalisation:**
- J3 WDC establishment and governance (dependency — SBTDWS cannot lead this)
- Makhlab 1 Memorandum of Agreement (J3 WDC / SBTDWS) — target Q1 27

---

## 3. PG Innovation Command (PGIC) — Handover Planning

**Status:** PGIC not yet established (concept approved; timing TBD).

**Risk:** If CAPDEV Cell hands over to PGIC too early, before SBTDWS has completed the Makhlab 1 PoC, SBTDWS loses its tactical experimentation capability mid-transition.

**Recommendation:** Define handover timeline early. CAPDEV Cell should formally refocus toward FDC 5 (Q1 27) and operate under J3 WDC governance until PGIC stands up and a handover protocol is agreed.

**What PGIC eventually takes:** CAPDEV portfolio management, strategic experimentation governance, industry engagement (the innovation enterprise level).

**What SBTDWS retains:** Tactical experimentation execution (Makhlab 1 model), field trials, EXCON/TEX function, TTP and doctrine outputs.

---

## 4. PGI / IWTC — IW Training Pipeline

**Relationship type:** Sequential pipeline (PGI teaches → SBTDWS validates).

**What PGI/IWTC provides:**
- IW qualification teaching (individual level)
- OPS contribution to standards
- Pipeline criteria inputs

**What SBTDWS provides:**
- Collective IW/UW training and validation
- SAG 9 validation framework
- Hybrid threat scenario integration

**Required formalisation:**
- IW pipeline handover protocol (individual-to-collective transition criteria)
- Disagreement arbitration mechanism (SBTDWS vs PGI on competency calls)

---

## 5. Falcons Brigade / SOF — Makhlab 1 PoC and SAG 9/10

**Why critical:** Falcons Bde is the natural partner unit for the first Makhlab 1 experimentation sprint. They provide the operational unit perspective, the SAG 9 partner for validation, and the UxS talent pipeline. See [[Falcons Brigade]] for the full entity profile, including their own J3-tabled Action Plan.

**Makhlab 1 PoC:**
- First FPV/UxS tactical experimentation sprint
- Runs under J3 WDC governance
- Falcons Bde provides operators and problem statements
- SBTDWS runs EXCON and evaluation
- Output: First TTP publication under FDC 5

**SAG 9/10:**
- Falcons Bde / SOF are the validation customers for SAG 9 (Combined UW) and SAG 10 (R&CB)
- SBTDWS OT cadre validates against these standards

---

## 6. PG S&T R&D Hub — Joint Experimentation

**Relationship type:** Complementary — SBTDWS provides operational environment; R&D Hub provides technology and test infrastructure.

**Target:** Joint experimentation events at Hameem from 2027 (post-capital endorsement Q3 27).

**What R&D Hub provides:** Technology prototypes, AI/ML compute, engineering support, lab-to-field integration.

**What SBTDWS provides:** Operational scenarios, realistic desert environment, trained operators, TTPs.

---

## 7. Operational Units — Validation Customers

**Why formalise:** Units must understand that SBTDWS validation carries command consequence. The "authority to fail" is only credible if the relationship between SBTDWS and its validation customers is clearly defined.

**Required:** Compulsion-to-attend validation writ. Reporting authority confirmed. Disagreement arbitration process.

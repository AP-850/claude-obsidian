# TEIW Standing Charter

**Status:** DRAFT — for Commandant review and FDC 5 endorsement  
**Authority:** Commandant SBTDWS, under FDC 5 mandate (J3 WDC)  
**Review cycle:** Quarterly, at FDC 5

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ADWC Three Wing Structure]] | [[SBTDWS CAPDEV Projects]]

---

## 1. Purpose

The Tactical Experimentation and Innovation Wing (TEIW) converts observed operational problems into tested TTPs and capability recommendations. It runs governed experiments — not R&D, not procurement, not capability development at scale.

TEIW is the field-level execution node. Its outputs feed two recipients:
- **CTVMR Wing** — validated TTPs inform the next exercise design and collective serial
- **PGIC / ATI-Hub** — experiment results provide the evidence base for capability investment decisions

TEIW does not deliver capability to units. It delivers *validated knowledge* that enables others to do so.

---

## 2. What TEIW Does

Five functions only:

**1. Receive problem statements from CTVMR AARs and FDC 5 direction.**
TEIW does not self-generate research questions. Every experiment traces back to a named exercise observation or a directed task from FDC 5/J3 WDC.

**2. Design and run bounded experiments.**
Each experiment has: a hypothesis, a measure of effectiveness (MOE), a time boundary (30–90 days), and a resource ceiling (within existing authority, no new budget). Experiments that cannot be scoped within these constraints are classified Tier 2 and submitted to PGIC.

**3. Produce doctrine outputs.**
Every completed experiment produces at minimum: a TTP recommendation, an equipment specification update (if applicable), and a training requirement delta. Experiments with no usable output are reviewed at the next FDC 5 cycle and either redesigned or closed.

**4. Feed CTVMR exercise design.**
TEIW outputs inform the next collective exercise design. TEIW does not deliver capability — it delivers validated TTPs that CTVMR exercises against. The loop is: CTVMR observes a gap → TEIW tests a solution → CTVMR validates the TTP in the next serial.

**5. Prepare Transition Gate submissions for Tier 2 projects.**
When an experiment result warrants capital investment or scale, TEIW prepares a submission for PGIC (or J3 WDC until PGIC exists). TEIW does not own the project beyond that gate.

---

## 3. What TEIW Does Not Do

These are hard exclusions. Any tasking in these categories is redirected.

**Not R&D.** TEIW tests and evaluates existing or prototype technology in operational conditions. It does not develop new technology. That is ATI-Hub / RDW function.

**Not procurement.** TEIW produces the evidence base for procurement decisions. It does not contract. Procurement authority sits elsewhere.

**Not the ATI-Hub.** Capital projects, DLOD/FIC development, and production runs (0–100 units) are ATI-Hub functions. Until ATI-Hub is established, Tier 2 results go to PGIC or J3 WDC.

**Not speculative.** TEIW does not experiment on technologies without an operational sponsor and a named gap. "Explore the potential of [technology]" is not a valid problem statement.

**Not a CTVMR substitute.** Participation in a TEIW experiment does not constitute unit validation. CTVMR and TEIW are parallel functions. A unit that runs an FPV sprint with TEIW is not validated — it is contributing to experiment data.

**Not the permanent owner of OPFOR.**
The OPFOR / Role Player Programme (information domain) sits primarily under CTVMR Wing — OPFOR is a CTVMR non-negotiable, not an experimentation output. TEIW may request OPFOR support for specific experiment design, but does not own or resource OPFOR.

---

## 4. Experiment Entry Criteria

Three questions. All three must be answered before an experiment is approved.

**Q1 — What is the gap?**
Name the specific exercise serial, AAR finding, or directed task that generated this problem statement. A gap without a named observation is not ready for experimentation. It is a hypothesis — return it to the problem statement backlog.

**Q2 — What is the test?**
One controlled change. One observable outcome. Defined pass/fail criteria against the MOE. Resource ceiling confirmed (Tier 1: within existing authority; Tier 2: flag for PGIC). "We will test several variations" is two experiments, not one.

**Q3 — What will we do with the result?**
Name the downstream action for both a positive and a negative result.
- Positive: which TTP is produced, which CTVMR exercise will validate it, or which PGIC submission it triggers
- Negative: what the failure rules out, and whether the problem statement is revised or closed

"We'll see what happens" is not an acceptable answer to Q3.

## 5. Authority — Two Tiers

| Tier | What it covers | Approval (current) | Approval (target state) | Timeline |
|------|---------------|-------------------|------------------------|---------|
| **Tier 1 — Rapid Experiment** | Within existing resources; no new budget; no external contract | Commandant | Commandant (unchanged) | 30–90 days |
| **Tier 2 — Development Project** | Requires new budget, capital, external contract, or facility | Commandant → higher command resource decision | PGIC / J3 WDC (when established) | 90+ days |

TEIW runs Tier 1 only. It prepares Tier 2 submissions but does not own or execute Tier 2.

**Current state (PGIC and ATI-Hub not yet established):** Tier 2 submissions go to the Commandant with a recommendation to escalate to higher command for a resource decision. They do not wait indefinitely for a governance structure that does not yet exist — they are flagged, packaged, and escalated through the current chain.

**Commandant approval for Tier 1** is granted on a rolling basis at the monthly TEIW-CTVMR coordination meeting, not case-by-case. The TEIW Commander presents proposed experiments against the three entry criteria; the Commandant approves the agenda for the next 30–90 day window.

## 6. Outputs

Every completed TEIW experiment produces entries in the following format:

| Output | Format | Recipient |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **TTP Recommendation** | 1–2 pages: hypothesis / method / finding / recommendation | SAT Cell (doctrine integration) |
| **Equipment Specification Delta** | What changed in the specification and why; includes failure modes found | J4 / procurement authority |
| **Training Requirement Delta** | What the exercise pipeline must add, remove, or change | DWW / CTVMR exercise design cell |
| **Transition Gate Submission** | Tier 2 case with experiment evidence and DLOD/FIC gap analysis | PGIC / J3 WDC (when warranted) |
| **GAP-X Case Entry** | Digital record in TEIW experiment log; searchable by domain and outcome | TEIW archive / Lessons Library |

Outputs must be filed within 14 days of experiment close. Unfiled outputs are treated as incomplete experiments at the next FDC 5 review.

## 7. Governance

**TEIW does not self-authorise its agenda.**

### Current State (Interim)

FDC 5, J3 WDC, PGIC, and ATI-Hub are not yet operational governance structures. The charter must function under current authority:

- **Experiment agenda:** Set by the Commandant, informed by CTVMR AAR findings and the CAPDEV portfolio review.
- **Approval authority:** Commandant SBTDWS for all Tier 1 experiments. Tier 2 escalated to higher command through normal chain.
- **Doctrine integration:** SAT Cell is the current routing point for TTP outputs. Outputs that require J3 WDC endorsement are flagged and held pending the J3 WDC relationship being formalised (Force Generation Pipeline ToR — target Q2 26).
- **Review cycle:** Quarterly, chaired by Commandant. TEIW Commander briefs completed experiments, in-progress status, and next-cycle agenda.

### Target State (When Governance Structures Are Established)

- **FDC 5 / J3 WDC** sets the experiment mandate. TEIW executes within it. Out-of-mandate experiments require a Commandant directive with J3 WDC notification.
- **PGIC** receives Tier 2 submissions. Reviews at its own cycle.
- **ATI-Hub** executes Tier 2 projects. TEIW hands off at the Transition Gate.

The charter does not wait for these structures to become operational. It runs under interim governance now and transitions when they are ready.

### Makhlab 1 — Supported PoC (Not the TEIW Template)

SBTDWS has a supporting role in Makhlab 1 (Tactical Drones PoC). TEIW is not the lead. Makhlab 1 is not a TEIW-owned experiment and does not set the operating template for TEIW. SBTDWS delivers its support contribution as directed; TEIW observes and extracts any applicable lessons for its own programme.

TEIW establishes its operating template through its **first owned experiment** — drawn from the CAPDEV portfolio and governed under this charter's entry criteria and approval process.

## 8. CTVMR Handoff — The Information Circuit

TEIW and CTVMR are on the same information circuit. They share the same operational knowledge base; they do not compete for it.

**From CTVMR to TEIW:**
AAR findings flag observed gaps → problem statements enter the TEIW backlog → TEIW prioritises by MOE clarity and resource fit.

**From TEIW to CTVMR:**
Validated TTPs → CTVMR exercise design cell → embedded into next collective serial as a trained standard.

**Coordination mechanism:** Monthly TEIW-CTVMR standing meeting (TEIW Commander + CTVMR exercise design lead + one OT cadre representative). Agenda: review open experiment status, review AAR findings from most recent CTVMR serial, confirm next cycle priorities.

**Priority rule:** TEIW experiments that can be designed into a CTVMR exercise serial are prioritised over those that cannot. Experiments that have no CTVMR integration path are deprioritised unless directed by FDC 5.

## 10. Staffing

TEIW is not a large formation. The charter does not require new billets; it requires the right people in the right roles.

**Minimum viable TEIW (Makhlab 1):**

| Role | Source | Function |
|------|--------|---------|
| TEIW Commander | OF-4 or senior OF-3 | Runs FDC 5 relationship; approves experiment designs; briefs Commandant |
| Experiment Cell (×2–3) | CAPDEV Cell staff (existing) | Designs and executes individual experiments; files outputs |
| Doctrine Liaison (×1) | SAT Cell link | Routes TTP outputs into doctrine process; prevents outputs from sitting in a drawer |
| CTVMR Liaison (×1, part-time) | OT cadre rotation | Closes the AAR → experiment → TTP loop; attends monthly coordination meeting |

**Full TEIW (FOC):** Scales with ATI-Hub establishment. When ATI-Hub is active, TEIW transitions to pure Tier 1 function; Tier 2 execution moves to RDW/TEX-Wing. Until then, TEIW holds both roles within the Tier 1 ceiling.

---

## 11. Charter Review

This charter is reviewed at each quarterly FDC 5 cycle. Amendment requires Commandant endorsement and J3 WDC notification.

**The charter narrows over time, not widens.** As ATI-Hub and PGIC are established, Tier 2 functions are handed off. TEIW becomes a leaner, faster, better-defined function — not a growing one.

First review: Q4 2026 (following Makhlab 1 sprint completion).

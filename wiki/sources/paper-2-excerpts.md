## 3.15 SBTDWS — Indicative Tasks

Intent: SBTDWS will extend its scope from training PG force elements to operate in the desert environment to enabling them to prosecute All Domain Warfare. Focus on training and validating PG units' ability to conduct effective operations which synchronise effects in all 6 domains — to ensure they are capable of successfully waging Sixth-generation Warfare (6GW). SBTDWS will be referred to as the PG 'All Domain Warfare Centre' (ADWC) by 2030.

1. Develop the capacity and expertise to train and validate PG FEs to conduct All Domain operations which deliver synchronised effect across air, sea, land, cyber, electromagnetic and space domains.
2. Hand off the existing SBTDWS capability development cell to the new PG Innovation Command.
3. Triggered by the new J3-led PG Warfare Development Centre, act as the PG's lead for tactical experimentation, testing operating concepts and developing effective TTPs prior to transferring them to PG units.
4. Integrate emerging operational trends and lessons learned from PG mission exploitation into training and course design, to ensure PG FEs maintain a decisive battlefield advantage.
5. Starting with MAKHLAB 1, be prepared to support PG 2030 / AI EMAD proof of concept events to showcase key elements of the PG's transformation agenda.
6. Enhance the existing Hameem site to provide a world class mission rehearsal facility, to enable PG FEs to conduct discrete, hyper realistic, pre-deployment training activity.
7. Develop the capacity, knowledge and expertise to exercise PG SRO responsibility for validating PG FEs' ability to deliver SAG 9 (Combined Unconventional Warfare) by 2030.
8. Develop the capacity, knowledge and expertise to exercise PG SRO responsibility for validating PG FEs' ability to deliver SAG 10 (Resilience and Capacity Building) by 2030.
9. Ensure appropriate personnel attend the IW training courses delivered by PGI; to enable them to be force generated to deploy on partnered/combined special forces operations.

## 5. Tactical Experimentation Concept

Tagline: "Driving innovation through experimentation"

- SBTDWS to become the All Domain Warfare Centre.
- Taking the knowledge gained from training and operations to drive innovation in capability and force development.
- Employing experienced operators to experiment with new and existing technology to inform TTP and doctrine development.
- Working closely with the PG Science and Technology R&D Hub and its Testing Range.
- Combining, testing, and validating new warfighting TTPs with technology in one centralised location.
- Exploit technology, data and combat experience.

Capability Development Cycle depicted: Force Development → Capability and Doctrine Development → Training → Exercises → Force Generation → Readiness → Deployment → Operations → Knowledge → (back to Capability and Doctrine Development).

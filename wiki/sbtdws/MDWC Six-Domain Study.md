# MDWC Six-Domain Study

J7-authored study scoping which of the six PG 2030 operational domains (land, air, maritime, space, cyber, EMS) sit within MDWC's remit, which require PG HQ decisions, and which sit with other organisations. DRAFT v0.1, for HQ socialisation. Full context: [[sources/mdwc-six-domain-study-vault-context]].

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[sources/mdwc-six-domain-study-vault-context]] | [[Commander Decisions Register]]

> [!contradiction] Uses "MDWC," not "ADWC"
> This document treats "MDWC — Multi Domain Warfare Centre" as the successor name to SBTDWS, stating ADWC is superseded. This is not a confirmed PG HQ decision — see [[ADWC Naming Analysis]] and [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-10. ## Strategic context

PG 2030's SBT 2030 transformation initiative drives SBTDWS's evolution into MDWC. J7 identified six operational domains the future institution must develop training and validation capability across. [[Force Design Concept Five|FDC5]] (Force Design Concept Five) is the operative design reference — it introduces cross-domain experimentation and UXS testing requirements that shape which domains MDWC must integrate, particularly maritime UXS T&E and EMS threat emulation for UXS validation.

## Domain analysis

### Land — core domain

**Current state:** Active live training at [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept|Hameem]]. Established collective training at sub-unit and unit level.

**Key gaps:** No formal collective training validation framework · No structured lesson capture / TTP codification pipeline (see [[Tactical Adaptation Platform]]) · Limited synthetic/constructive training capability · No UGV/ground UXS training infrastructure · Limited OPFOR capability.

**What's needed:** Formalised validation framework (standards, metrics, assessor cadre) · Tactical Adaptation Platform concept → implementation (ORABATV cycle) · LVC integration, initial increment · UGV training lanes and SOPs · OPFOR doctrine and trained element.

**Position:** All investments within MDWC authority. Immediate priority. Gaps are in formalisation, not foundational capability — land is the anchor domain.

### Air — core domain / major growth requirement

**Current state:** Nascent UAS training activity only. No dedicated infrastructure, programme, qualification framework, or T&E framework.

**Key gaps:** No UAS operator training programme or qualification framework · No UAS training infrastructure at Hameem (airspace, RF management, safety) · No C-UAS training capability · No UAS T&E framework aligned to [[Makhlab 1 Project|MAKHLAB]] projects · JTAC training not institutionalised or accredited · No synthetic air domain representation.

**What's needed:** UAS training range at Hameem (restricted airspace, RF spectrum management, safety protocols) · UAS operator qualification programme · C-UAS training programme · JTAC training and accreditation pathway · UAS T&E SOPs aligned to FDC5 and MAKHLAB project offices · Synthetic air domain representation in collective exercises.

**Position:** Highest urgency development area. MDWC authority. Immediate priority — UAS and C-UAS represent the most significant gap between current capability and future requirement, and MAKHLAB project T&E demands make this the top investment priority.

### Maritime (C/MAR) — conditional

**Current state:** No maritime capability exists within MDWC. Hameem has no maritime infrastructure.

**Position:** MDWC does not assess a standing maritime training requirement. FDC5 *may* create a maritime UXS T&E requirement — this cannot be determined at MDWC level.

> [!important] HQ action required
> Determine whether MDWC has a maritime UXS T&E role under FDC5 and assign authority and resourcing accordingly. This is [[MDWC Six-Domain Study#HQ decisions required|HQ Decision #1]] below, and directly informs the "is maritime in scope?" open item flagged on [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)]] and [[Commander Decisions Register]].

### Space — external

**Current state:** No capability. Not an MDWC requirement. Space domain training sits with a TBC organisation within the UAE defence establishment.

**Position:** MDWC should acknowledge space as an enabling domain in exercise design (e.g. GPS-denied scenario injects) without assuming institutional ownership.

> [!important] HQ action required
> Confirm which organisation carries space domain training authority. Establish a coordination mechanism with MDWC.

### Cyber — external

**Current state:** No capability. Not an MDWC requirement. Sits with a TBC organisation.

**Position:** Basic cyber awareness should be embedded in MDWC staff preparation; formal cyber training is not an MDWC responsibility.

> [!important] HQ action required
> Confirm which organisation carries cyber domain training authority. Establish a coordination mechanism with MDWC.

### EMS — future integration requirement

**Current state:** No EMS training capability. No EW systems or threat emitters at Hameem. No spectrum management framework. UXS platforms currently tested without EMS threat representation.

**Key gaps:** No EMS threat emulation capability (jammers, GPS spoofers) for UXS T&E · No EMS training programme or awareness curriculum · No spectrum management plan for Hameem · No EW planning integration into exercise design · MAKHLAB UXS platforms not tested against EMS threats · No EW/EMS SME cadre within MDWC.

**What's needed:** Portable EMS threat emulation systems (jammers, GPS spoofers, safety-assured) · Spectrum management plan and frequency deconfliction process for Hameem · EMS awareness training module in all MDWC programmes · EW/EMS SME cadre (organic or via support agreement) · UXS T&E SOPs incorporating EMS threat scenarios · EW standard injects in collective exercise design · Coordination with MAKHLAB project offices.

**Position:** Near-term planning priority, cannot be deferred. UXS T&E without realistic EMS threats is operationally unassured — this directly undermines the credibility of all MAKHLAB project validation outcomes.

## Findings & recommendations

1. **Land — formalise what exists.** Gaps are in formalisation, not foundational capability. Advance the Tactical Adaptation Platform concept to implementation as the institutional standard.
2. **Air — most urgent growth area.** Develop a dedicated UAS training and T&E programme; establish restricted airspace, RF spectrum management, and safety protocols at Hameem immediately; align T&E framework with MAKHLAB project offices.
3. **EMS — cannot be deferred.** Initiate EMS capability planning for Hameem now; acquire portable threat emulation systems as priority; embed EMS into all UXS T&E SOPs.
4. **Maritime — requires HQ decision.** No standing requirement assessed; refer to PG HQ; MDWC to prepare an options brief if directed.
5. **Space & Cyber — sit outside MDWC.** Both are TBC-organisation responsibilities; MDWC should acknowledge them in exercise design without assuming ownership; PG HQ to confirm institutional authority and establish coordination mechanisms.

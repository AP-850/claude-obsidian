# Al Forsan (AF)

PG manoeuvre unit responsible for Support to Civil Unrest (SAG 6), Large Scale Direct Action (SAG 14), and Medium Scale Maritime Forcible Entry (SAG 16). Building toward a deployable 1-star JTF HQ under the new PG Component C2 Model.

## Develop by 2030

1. Forcible Theatre entry capability (alongside Joint Enablers)
2. Large-scale Direct-Action capability
3. Deployable 1st JTF HQ
4. Tactical UxS — **MAKHLAB 2**, shared serial with [[Hamid Bin Essa]], designed to be copyable by SOC and HBE
5. IW as a baseline skill for selected personnel

---

## Tasks (Aug 2026 register)

- Develop a deployable, 1-star JTF HQ capable of integrating AI and joint enablers
- Act as Training Requirements Authority (TRA) for all Al Forsan training courses delivered by [[PGI]]
- Be prepared to prosecute targets allocated by PGI's Multi-Domain Targeting Cell
- Share Air Assault knowledge and expertise with [[Hamid Bin Essa]] to support HBE's conversion transition
- Ensure appropriate personnel attend IW training courses delivered by PGI

Each SAG (6, 14, 16) follows the standard OPS pass-off chain: get Operational Performance Standards approval from J3, pass off to J7 for training implementation.

Gap and Issue Triage Register

Consolidated, ranked triage of gaps, blockers, and issues — SBTDWS transformation

Prepared by SBTDWS | 2026-06-19 | Pre-decisional planning document

Consolidated, ranked triage of every gap, blocker, and issue identified across the SBTDWS transformation analysis (FIC analysis, institutional barriers review, CTC architecture model, 9 directed tasks mapping, command decisions log, and the J3 working group back-brief of 2026-06-10). Built to feed the J3/Commander engagement: what can be fixed at SBTDWS level, what cannot be resolved below Higher HQ / PG HQ, and what needs to change before the OPORD is released to units.

How to read this register. Items are triaged by who can act and how urgent, not by which source document they came from. Tier 0 is the OPORD timing risk — act before the OPORD is issued, not after. Tiers 1–4 mirror escalation level.

Two live discrepancies exist between the written planning guidance and the verbal direction given at the most recent J3 working group session (2026-06-10). Tier 3 — Institutional / Cultural (Slow-Burn, No Single Decision Fixes It)

These barriers are documented separately and translated into command-safe language for use in formal briefs. They are not blocked by a single pending decision — they require sustained command tone-setting, and the 2030 timeline assumes they are fixed faster than institutional culture typically changes.

BarrierCommand-safe framing used in briefsRisk if unaddressed

Contractor dependency"Consolidate military ownership of core skills"Unsustainable as a validation institution; military-ownership target at risk
Resource shortages"Strengthen training system foundations"Training continuity disrupted independent of any higher-level decision
Bureaucratic delays"Improve training continuity"Schedule risk compounds on top of decision delays
Hiding deficiencies"Enhance readiness assurance"Directly contradicts the "truth before excellence" principle the transformation is built on
Timeline tension worth stating plainly: initial operating capability is targeted within a few years. These are cultural/institutional barriers, which historically take longer to fix than capability or infrastructure gaps. There is a real risk the transformation timeline does not allow enough time for cultural change to precede the capability build it depends on.

Suggested Brief Structure

  1. Open with Tier 0 — the OPORD timing window. This is the one part of the brief with a hard expiry: once the OPORD is cut, the six items above become much more expensive to fix.
  2. Tier 1 as the "decisions we cannot make for you" section — the four standing decisions, plus the single-point-of-failure risk stated explicitly.
  3. Tier 2 as proof of momentum — show the command-level actions are already in motion or about to be actioned, so the brief isn't purely a list of asks.
  4. Tier 3 mentioned, not dwelt on — the institutional barriers are already documented in command-safe language; reference rather than re-litigate.
  5. Tier 4 flagged as "on the radar, not today's ask."

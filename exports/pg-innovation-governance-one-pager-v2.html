Explaining these tiers is the point of this brief. Stage 3 (Tier 1) is where SBTDWS's TEIW charter already operates — offered below as a ready-made, PG-wide template. Stage 4 (Tier 2) is the gate CDD now chairs. Stage 2 (intake) still has no owner or mechanism, regardless of who chairs the gate.

Still Worth Flagging in the Room

act · shape
ACT

Now that CDD chairs, the separation from day-to-day CAPDEV procurement/fielding matters more, not less — worth landing explicitly, not assuming it's understood.

ACT

Stage 2 (intake) still has no owner or mechanism — a live gap regardless of who chairs governance.

SHAPE

A department running CAPDEV well today isn't automatically suited to running innovation — different culture, and possibly not yet the institutional weight to make a ring-fenced cell stick.

SHAPE

No budget/bridge-funding mechanism exists yet between a proven experiment and a resourced programme.

calibrated, not a governance claim

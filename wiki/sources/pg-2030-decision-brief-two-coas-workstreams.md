## Part 1 — Decision Brief Revision

Full content moved to [[SBTDWS Core Focus — Decision Brief (Two COAs, Revised)]] — the authoritative current version of D-12. The original three-COA brief ([[SBTDWS Core Focus — COA Decision]]) is marked superseded but retained for the historical record.

**Headline change:** the decision is reframed from "how much of the course programme survives" to "authority to transition SBTDWS to the MDTC, plus the extent of that change" — full transition (desert warfare role ends by FOC Q4 30) versus transition with a permanently protected desert warfare core. Both COAs now require change; the status quo is deliberately not offered as an option.

> [!contradiction] COA numbering reused with an inverted meaning — read carefully
> In the original three-COA brief, **COA 1 ("Retain and Add")** meant *keep everything, add MDTC alongside it* — the closest thing to status quo. In this revision, **COA 1 ("Full Transition")** means the opposite: the desert warfare role **ends entirely** by FOC Q4 30. The label "COA 1" now points to what was previously the most disruptive end of the spectrum, not the least. Any older note, slide, or conversation referencing "COA 1" from before 2026-08-08 is describing the retired option — check the date before citing a COA number.

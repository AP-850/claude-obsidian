# Practical constraints you’ll have to account for
- **Endurance vs. payload (battery)**: Personal e-multicopters are 15-25 min class-fine for short hops, not patrol. Hybrid or turbine options extend range but raise acoustic/thermal signatures. ([eVTOL News](https://evtol.news/lift-hexa/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

# J7 LVC Architecture

The PG-wide Live, Virtual, Constructive (LVC) training architecture owned by J7. SBTDWS's OT, HICON, EXCON and [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept|Hameem]] mission-rehearsal capabilities must nest inside this architecture rather than being built as standalone systems.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept]] | [[SBTDWS CTC Architecture]]

---

## Why it matters

Listed as a top dependency in both [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] §4.5 and the [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)|5-LOE implementation slide]]: without J7 LVC architecture, "OT, HICON, EXCON and Hameem mission-rehearsal builds cannot nest coherently" — risking duplicative or non-interoperable infrastructure built in isolation by SBTDWS.

- Hameem's EXCON building and secure network are explicitly designed to link into the J7 network (LOE 5, Phase 2 of the 5-LOE slide).
- Hameem IOC (Q4 28) requires EXCON operational and integrated with J7 LVC architecture per [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] end-state.
- J7 also approves the Hameem development concept and design (5-LOE slide, LOE 5 Phase 1).

## Open items

No dedicated decision ID exists yet in [[Commander Decisions Register]] specifically for J7 LVC architecture alignment — it is folded into D-08 (Hameem concept of use and capital commitment), which names "LVC integration with J7" as part of what must be approved.

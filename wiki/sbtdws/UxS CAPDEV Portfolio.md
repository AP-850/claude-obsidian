## Autonomous Combat Platoon / Company (Research 2025)

Research into defence companies and militaries developing autonomous combat systems at company scale (3 combat platoons + 1 support platoon).

### Key systems identified

| System / Company | Type | Status |
|-----------------|------|--------|
| Milrem THeMIS (Estonia) | UGV — modular combat/support | Combat-proven in Ukraine |
| Overland AI ULTRA | Autonomous multi-mission vehicle | US military trials |
| Kratos MOSA | Leader-follower UGV platooning | USMC demonstration |
| Textron RIPSAW M5 | Robotic combat vehicle | US Army programme |

### Key finding
Full autonomous company-level formations remain pre-IOC. The feasible near-term option is **manned-unmanned teaming** — a manned command vehicle leading 2-3 autonomous wingman UGVs. Full autonomous combat platoons by 2028-2030 are achievable only with human oversight.

## VTOL / eVTOL Research (November 2025)

Research into personal and larger VTOL platforms for military use.

### Personal / individual craft

| Platform | Capability | Military use case |
|---------|-----------|------------------|
| JetPack Aviation JB12 | Personal jet propulsion | Individual SOCOM insertion |
| Gravity Industries suit | Wearable propulsion | Short-range assault / recon |
| Opener BlackFly | Single-seat eVTOL | SOCOM Agility Prime candidate |
| Joby / Archer / Beta | Passenger-scale eVTOL | Tactical mobility, medevac |

### SOCOM Agility Prime
US SOCOM programme evaluating commercial eVTOL for small unit tactical mobility. Primary use cases: medical evacuation, small team insertion, resupply in denied terrain.

### Relevance to SBTDWS
VTOL research supports the ATI-Hub UxS portfolio. Near-term relevance: tactical resupply and personnel recovery in deep desert. Longer-term: force insertion at Hameem mission rehearsal.

## Logistical Resupply Drone (June 2025)

Concept of Use for a tactical resupply UAS capable of 50kg payload in desert environments.

### Capability need statement summary

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Title | Tactical Resupply UAS (TR-UAS) |
| Payload | Up to 50kg |
| Environment | Desert (extreme heat, dust, low humidity) |
| Range | Forward line resupply (10-30km typical) |
| Key use cases | Ammunition resupply, water, medical supplies, battery replacement |
| Non-attributable | Preferred — no national markings |
| Autonomy level | Semi-autonomous / waypoint navigation with human override |

### Operational employment concept
TR-UAS operates from a Logistics Support Company or combat support node, delivering to forward line units without vehicle exposure. Reduces the resupply truck signature in contested desert environments.

## Desert Warfare Capability Ideas for 2028

Five CAPDEV ideas identified for a desert warfare organisation starting in 2028:

1. **AI-enabled desert navigation** — autonomous route optimisation through complex terrain; degraded GPS environment
2. **Distributed energy systems** — solar-battery hybrid field power for forward positions; reduces fuel resupply dependency
3. **Micro-mesh communications** — decentralised tactical mesh networks resilient to jamming and node destruction
4. **Counter-UAS training system** — dedicated C-UAS training range with FPV drone threat simulators
5. **Synthetic terrain simulation** — high-fidelity virtual desert environment for pre-deployment rehearsal and scenario design

## FPV Reserve Brigade Program

Designed to raise 10 x 10-man FPV drone teams through the reserve brigade:
- 3-week intensive operator pipeline
- Instructor cadre from ATI-Hub + external SMEs
- Organic FPV production: 50-100 units per batch
- Standardised spare parts, battery, and antenna pool
- Integration into DWS collective exercises and platoon attacks

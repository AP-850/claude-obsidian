# OPFOR/EXCON/HICON Estimate — J7 Discussion Primer

An internal SBTDWS estimate of OPFOR, EXCON, and HICON requirements, built to prime discussion with the J7 team ahead of **G1** (EXCON/Instructor/OT Establishment Requirement, target Oct-26, jointly owned COMD PG/J7/COMD SBT — see [[J7 Readiness Flow and Deliverables Register]]). Applies the same internal-efficiency-first methodology already built for the wider MDTC staffing transition — see [[Workstream 1 — OOB and Staffing Transition Framework]] Priority-Position Methodology.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Workstream 1 — OOB and Staffing Transition Framework]] | [[J7 Readiness Flow and Deliverables Register]]

> [!key-insight] This is a primer, not a position
> Built explicitly as a discussion starting point for the J7 team, not a final ask. Numbers below are illustrative ranges sized against SBTDWS's *current* exercise echelon (company level), not a validated requirement — every figure should be treated as a hypothesis to test with J7, not a number to defend.

## What Each Function Needs to Do

Drawn from existing wiki analysis, not invented for this page:

- **OPFOR** — per [[ADWC Three Wing Structure]]: "dedicated OPFOR capability — not borrowed from training units, not role-players with insufficient authority... must be credible, persistent, and resourced to win... must include FPV/UxS operators, EW capability, IW/deception actors, hybrid threat behaviours." [[SBTDWS CAPDEV Projects]] lists an "OPFOR / Role Player Program" with the same composition — currently an aspirational CAPDEV concept, not a staffed function.
- **EXCON (Exercise Control)** — per [[J7 Readiness Flow and Deliverables Register]] C8: "EXCON and HICON structures, appointments, battle rhythm, and adjudication rules." Per [[SBTDWS Key Relationships]], SBTDWS already provides "EXCON teams" to J7 as part of its defining linkage — meaning EXCON is an existing *commitment*, just not yet a resourced establishment line.
- **HICON (Higher Control)** — role-plays the next higher headquarters during an exercise, issuing realistic orders/injects so the exercising unit responds to a credible chain of command rather than a vacuum. Needs staff-officer-calibre people, not just any body — a smaller but more senior requirement than EXCON or OPFOR.

---

## Design Principles, Not Headcount Benchmarks — [[National Training Center (NTC)]] / [[JRTC]]

The wiki already holds comparative CTC research on OPFOR/OC-T design. **Their headcounts are not usable here** — NTC's OPFOR is a full permanently-stationed armoured cavalry regiment (11th ACR); JRTC's is a dedicated brigade-scale formation. SBTDWS is at company-level collective training today, several orders of magnitude smaller. Scaling a headcount down from a regiment would be false precision, not an estimate.

What *does* transfer, as design principles rather than numbers:
1. **Dedicated and persistent, not borrowed or ad hoc.** Both NTC and JRTC exist specifically because informally-sourced OPFOR produces weaker training outcomes.
2. **Credible authority.** OPFOR/OC-T staff need real standing to contest the exercising unit, not token participation.
3. **MOS/specialty-matched control staff** (JRTC's model) — EXCON/HICON people should match the function they're controlling, not be generalists assigned by availability.

## Scale Anchor — This Estimate Is IOC-Scale, Not FOC-Scale

SBTDWS's current mission is company-level collective training. The [[J7 Readiness Flow and Deliverables Register]] readiness flow scales up progressively — Unit Collective → Formation and CPX → Validation vs SAG → JTF C2 Certified — meaning formation-level OPFOR/EXCON/HICON (needed for CPX and JTF certification) is a **later, larger requirement**, not something G1 needs to size for now. This estimate covers what's needed to run credible company-level serials at IOC; formation-level growth is flagged separately below, not folded in.

## Function-by-Function Estimate

| Function | Internal source candidate | Residual gap | Illustrative range (IOC, company-level) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EXCON** | Strongest internal case. Collective Training Wing already runs company exercises and holds an informal Observer Trainer/exercise-support role per [[SBTDWS Overview]] — natural extension of the same wing's Case 1 transition in [[Workstream 1 — OOB and Staffing Transition Framework]], not a separate net-new ask. | Formalising the role (director, scenario designer, adjudicators) as a chartered function rather than an informal add-on to training delivery | ~5–8 (exercise director, 1 scenario designer, 3–6 controllers/adjudicators for a company-level serial) | Low-medium — direction is sound, headcount is a first-order guess |
| **HICON** | Partial internal case. Needs staff-officer-calibre people. Candidates: senior instructor cadre, or select individuals among the ~90-100 not-yet-itemised staff with prior staff experience (see [[Workstream 1 — OOB and Staffing Transition Framework]] Methodology Step 3). The 6 Western instructors may plausibly bridge this in the interim, given likely staff-college/HQ backgrounds — **not a permanent solution**, advisor billets aren't a stable establishment line. | Sustainable, PG-military-staffed HICON capacity beyond the interim bridge | ~2–4 (small, senior, deliberately not padded — HICON needs fewer people than EXCON but higher seniority each) | Low — genuinely a first guess, most in need of J7 input |
| **OPFOR** | **Weakest internal case, honestly.** Nothing in current establishment resembles a dedicated adversary/threat-emulation cell. The existing CAPDEV "OPFOR / Role Player Program" is an aspiration, not a staffed function. Worth exploring [[Specialist Reserve Brigade (SRB)]]'s UxS/SQEP pivot as a partial source for the FPV/UxS-operator slice specifically — distinct from, and not overlapping with, the *friendly*-side FPV Schoolhouse's own 10x10-man team commitment. | The largest genuine external-gap item of the three | ~15–25 role-players/operators + 2–3 leadership, composed per [[ADWC Three Wing Structure]]'s stated mix (FPV/UxS, EW, IW/deception, general role-players) — sized to roughly match the scale of the friendly company being exercised, not smaller | Low — most speculative figure on this page, deliberately not padded to look internally solved when it isn't |

> [!note] Naming OPFOR as the weak point is deliberate
> Claiming every function is internally coverable would read as padding to anyone who knows the school's actual establishment. Flagging OPFOR honestly as the function with the least internal coverage is what makes the EXCON and HICON internal-sourcing claims credible.

## Assumptions to Validate With J7

This is the actual point of the primer — a list to put in front of the J7 team, not a set of conclusions:

1. Is company-level the right sizing anchor for G1, or does J7 want the initial establishment case to already provision for formation-level growth?
2. Does J7 have an existing view on OPFOR sourcing model (organic to SBTDWS vs. a cross-unit pool, e.g. via SRB) that this estimate should be built around instead of guessing?
3. Is the Western-instructor HICON bridge acceptable as an interim measure, or does J7 want a PG-military-only HICON solution from the outset?
4. Does G4's Q1-27 cadre generation plan assume G1's Oct-26 positions are already approved, or can generation planning start in parallel with the establishment case?
5. Are there existing PG-wide EXCON/OPFOR resources (e.g., from another unit's exercise programme) SBTDWS should be aware of before proposing a fully organic build?

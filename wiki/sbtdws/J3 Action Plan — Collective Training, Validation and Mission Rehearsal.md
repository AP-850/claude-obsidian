## 1. Phases of Implementation

| Phase | Period | Capability Gate |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| **Phase One** | Now — Q3 2027 | Foundations: OT cadre stood up, first validation serial conducted, structural reorganisation endorsed |
| **Phase Two** | Q4 2027 — Q1 2029 | Initial Operating Capability (IOC): Hameem mission rehearsal IOC, validation at scale, OT cadre at FOC |
| **Phase Three** | Q2 2029 — Q3 2030 | Full Operating Capability (FOC): ADWC validation and mission rehearsal function fully operational |

*Phase One combines the PREP and Phase 1 stages of the detailed plan ([[SBTDWS Action Plan]] §4.3) — both are foundational and precede the first capability gate.*

## 2. Defining Phased Objectives

### Phase One – 2026/2027

1. **Stand up an accredited OT cadre to Initial Operating Capability.** Selection board convened Q3 26; OT Phase 1 (Foundation Training) and Phase 2 (Supervised Practice) complete; minimum 8 OTs accredited at Level 2 by **Q2 27**.
2. **Conduct the first validation serial.** Company-level serial against SAG 9 or ADW focus, OTs in role, by **Q2 27**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-03 (PENDING)
   > This objective assumes the Commandant has pre-committed to backing the OT cadre's first adverse assessment. Without D-03 resolved, OTs will self-censor and the serial will not produce a credible result. See [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-03.

3. **Endorse the structural reorganisation.** Function-based SBTDWS structure (training delivery / validation / experimentation / mission rehearsal / enabling support) endorsed by **Q4 26**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-01 and D-07 (PENDING)
   > Structural endorsement requires the three-wing design to be accepted (D-01) and the J-Staff support model confirmed (D-07). Both are PENDING.

4. **Endorse the Hameem development concept.** Use cases, capital scope, and J7/J6/J8 dependencies agreed by **Q3 27**.

### Phase Two – 2028

5. **Bring Hameem to mission rehearsal IOC.** EXCON operational, instrumentation live, first mission rehearsal serial complete by **Q4 28**.
6. **Grow the OT cadre to Full Operating Capability.** 20+ OTs across Levels 1–3, including 4+ Senior OTs, self-sustaining accreditation, by **Q2 28**.
7. **Deliver the thematic exercise year.** Three themed validation exercises (Experimentation to Adaptation, UxS Tactical Employment, IW/UW) across 2028.

### Phase Three – 2029/2030

8. **Declare ADWC/MDWC Full Operating Capability.** Full validation capability (SAG 9 at FOC, SAG 10 operational), Hameem at FOC with full LVC integration, by **2030**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-05 and D-06 (PENDING)
   > Validation authority (can SBTDWS certify / fail / recommend remediation?) and SAG 9/10 standards ownership are both PENDING at Higher HQ / PG HQ level. FOC cannot be meaningfully declared while validation remains advisory only.

9. **Deliver routine FE@R contributions to MOD via the J7 programme**, 2030.

## 3. Identifying Requirements for Achieving Objectives

- Endorse the three-wing structural design and the SBTDWS J-Staff support model (organic / allocated / liaison).
- Publish ADW competency profiles and an ADW validation framework (how SBTDWS validates, reports, and escalates).
- Recruit, train, and accredit the OT cadre (selection board, Foundation Training, Supervised Practice, formal accreditation boards).
- Secure command backing for the OT cadre's authority to fail, formalised in a signed writ.
- Commission and fund the Hameem mission rehearsal concept (capital sequence, EXCON infrastructure, instrumentation, J7 LVC integration, J6 digital backbone connectivity).
- Secure Higher HQ endorsement of SAG 9 and SAG 10 standards and SBTDWS's validation authority.
- Resource the additional personnel requirement (OTs, EXCON cell, HICON, OPFOR cell, scenario design cell — estimated 30–45 above current establishment at FOC).

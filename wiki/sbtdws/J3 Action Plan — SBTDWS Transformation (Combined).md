# J3 Action Plan — SBTDWS Transformation (Combined)

**Format:** J3 Action Plan (see [[J3 Action Plan Template]]) — single combined document
**Status:** DRAFT — for Commandant review before tabling to J3
**Built from:** [[J3 Action Plan — Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal]], [[J3 Action Plan — Tactical Experimentation]], [[J3 Action Plan — Training and Course Management]]

> [!note] One document or three?
> This combines the three separate J3-format Action Plans into a single document, objectives as sub-sections under each of the three required headings. Use this version where J3 wants one document; use the three separate versions where J3 wants to review or approve each objective independently. Content is identical either way — this page does not supersede the three separate documents.

> [!gap] Assumptions flagged, not resolved
> Objectives below are marked wherever they depend on a Commander decision that is still **PENDING** ([[Commander Decisions Register]]): D-01, D-03, D-04, D-05, D-06, D-07, D-08, D-09, D-11. This plan is written as if SBTDWS's preferred resolution of each holds — if a decision lands differently, the affected objective and its timeline must be revisited.

## 1. Phases of Implementation

All three objectives run on the same SBTDWS transformation calendar — the phases below apply across the whole plan, not per objective.

| Phase | Period | Capability Gate |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| **Phase One** | Now — Q3 2027 | Foundations: OT cadre to IOC, first validation serial, structural reorganisation endorsed, experimentation governance operational, course consolidation implemented |
| **Phase Two** | Q4 2027 — Q1 2029 | Initial Operating Capability (IOC): Hameem mission rehearsal IOC, OT cadre to FOC, thematic exercise year, PGI/IWTC pipeline formalised |
| **Phase Three** | Q2 2029 — Q3 2030 | Full Operating Capability (FOC): ADWC validation and mission rehearsal at FOC, experimentation programme self-sustaining, course portfolio sustained as steady state |

**Effort allocation across the three objectives:**

| Objective | Effort Type | Work Groupings |
|-----------|------------|----------------|
| 1. Collective Training, Validation, and Mission Rehearsal | Main Effort | WG1, WG3, WG5, WG6 |
| 2. Tactical Experimentation | Main Effort | WG4, WG7 |
| 3. Training and Course Management | Supporting Effort | WG3, WG6, WG7 |

*Phase One combines the PREP and Phase 1 stages of the detailed plan ([[SBTDWS Action Plan]]) for all three objectives — both are foundational and precede the first capability gate.*

## 2. Defining Phased Objectives

### Phase One – 2026/2027

#### Objective 1: Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal (Main Effort)

1.1. **Stand up an accredited OT cadre to Initial Operating Capability.** Selection board convened Q3 26; OT Phase 1 (Foundation Training) and Phase 2 (Supervised Practice) complete; minimum 8 OTs accredited at Level 2 by **Q2 27**.

1.2. **Conduct the first validation serial.** Company-level serial against SAG 9 or ADW focus, OTs in role, by **Q2 27**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-03 (PENDING) — Commandant pre-commitment to backing the OT cadre's first adverse assessment.

1.3. **Endorse the structural reorganisation.** Function-based SBTDWS structure (training delivery / validation / experimentation / mission rehearsal / enabling support) endorsed by **Q4 26**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-01 and D-07 (PENDING) — three-wing design acceptance and J-Staff support model confirmation.

1.4. **Endorse the Hameem development concept.** Use cases, capital scope, and J7/J6/J8 dependencies agreed by **Q3 27**.

#### Objective 2: Tactical Experimentation (Main Effort)

2.1. **Establish the J3 WDC experimentation governance framework.** Trigger, approval, reporting, and TTP transfer process defined by **Q3 26**; SBTDWS executes, J3 WDC owns priorities and outputs.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-11 (PENDING) — formal J3 WDC governance for tactical experimentation not yet decided.

2.2. **Establish the SBTDWS experimentation cell.** 3–5 personnel in role and operational by **Q4 26**.

2.3. **Deliver the first governed Makhlab 1 PoC serial.** UxS focus, data captured, AAR conducted, TTP recommendations drafted, by **Q1 27**, in partnership with [[Falcons Brigade]].

2.4. **Complete the first TTP transfer test.** Makhlab 1 recommendations reviewed, refined, and formally handed to J3 WDC for unit adoption by **Q2 27**.

2.5. **Finalise the TEIW Standing Charter** (v0.2, FPV as Priority 1) and field the UxS portfolio (land and air) by **Q2 27**.

#### Objective 3: Training and Course Management (Supporting Effort)

3.1. **Complete the 6GW training review.** Full audit of the course portfolio — retain, consolidate, or close — mapped against ADW requirements, by **Q3 26**.

3.2. **Implement the course consolidation plan.** Courses restructured or closed per the PREP-phase review; freed capacity redirected to Objective 1, by **Q4 26**.

3.3. **Launch the instructor development programme.** Structured programme running in low-tempo (Ramadan/summer) windows, military instructors improving in priority skill areas, from **Q4 26**.

3.4. **Initiate PGI/IWTC engagement.** Define the split — PGI delivers individual IW training, SBTDWS delivers collective application and validation — from **Q1 27**.

### Phase Two – 2028

#### Objective 1: Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal (Main Effort)

1.5. **Bring Hameem to mission rehearsal IOC.** EXCON operational, instrumentation live, first mission rehearsal serial complete by **Q4 28**.

1.6. **Grow the OT cadre to Full Operating Capability.** 20+ OTs across Levels 1–3, including 4+ Senior OTs, self-sustaining accreditation, by **Q2 28**.

1.7. **Deliver the thematic exercise year.** Three themed validation exercises (Experimentation to Adaptation, UxS Tactical Employment, IW/UW) across 2028.

#### Objective 2: Tactical Experimentation (Main Effort)

2.6. **Deliver the thematic UxS Tactical Employment exercise.** Large-scale exercise integrating experimentation and validation, across 2028.

2.7. **Stand up the formal lessons exploitation mechanism.** Quarterly cycle: exercise observations and operational lessons → course review → TTP refinement → validation criteria update, operational by **2028**.

2.8. **Establish the Makhlab follow-on serial cadence.** Annual or bi-annual governed experimentation serials, expanding UxS scope, from **FY28**.

2.9. **Assess maritime UxS scope and feasibility**, 2028.

#### Objective 3: Training and Course Management (Supporting Effort)

3.5. **Formalise the PGI/IWTC pipeline.** MoU or equivalent covering attendance tracking, unit release, collective consolidation at SBTDWS, and return-to-unit utilisation, by **2028**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-04 (PENDING) — IW/UW boundary must be defined by Higher HQ before the PGI/SBTDWS split can be finalised.

3.6. **Achieve military instructor ownership** of all core military skills across priority courses, by **2028**.

3.7. **Operationalise the lessons integration cycle.** Quarterly review — exercise AARs and operational lessons feeding course updates and validation criteria review, by **2028**.

> [!key-insight] Cross-objective duplication risk
> Objective 2's "lessons exploitation mechanism" (2.7) and Objective 3's "lessons integration cycle" (3.7) describe overlapping machinery — both are quarterly cycles feeding exercise/operational lessons into course design, TTP refinement, and validation criteria review. Combining the two objectives into one document surfaces this: build **one** lessons mechanism shared across both, not two. Recommend J7/J2 own a single lessons cycle that both Objective 2 and Objective 3 draw from.

### Phase Three – 2029/2030

#### Objective 1: Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal (Main Effort)

1.8. **Declare ADWC/MDWC Full Operating Capability.** Full validation capability (SAG 9 at FOC, SAG 10 operational), Hameem at FOC with full LVC integration, by **2030**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-05 and D-06 (PENDING) — validation authority and SAG 9/10 standards ownership both PENDING at Higher HQ / PG HQ level.

1.9. **Deliver routine FE@R contributions to MOD via the J7 programme**, 2030.

#### Objective 2: Tactical Experimentation (Main Effort)

2.10. **Declare the experimentation programme routine and self-sustaining.** TEIW operating at scale across land, air, and (if assessed viable) maritime; TTP transfer to units embedded as standard cycle, by **2030**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-09 (PENDING) — CAPDEV → PGIC transition plan not yet decided; TEIW's long-term scope cannot be finalised until this boundary is confirmed.

#### Objective 3: Training and Course Management (Supporting Effort)

3.8. **Sustain the consolidated, credible course portfolio as steady state**, feeding instructor and capacity gains directly into Objective 1, through **2030**.

3.9. **Embed honest assessment culture.** Pass rates reflect genuine standards, not social pressure — sustained as a standing Commandant directive.

## 3. Identifying Requirements for Achieving Objectives

### Cross-cutting (applies to all three objectives)

- Endorse the three-wing structural design and the SBTDWS J-Staff support model (organic / allocated / liaison) — gates Objective 1's structural reorganisation (D-01, D-07).
- Confirm validation authority and SAG 9/10 standards ownership with Higher HQ / PG HQ (D-05, D-06) — gates Objective 1's FOC declaration.
- Define the IW/UW boundary with Higher HQ (D-04) — gates Objective 3's PGI/IWTC pipeline and Objective 1's SAG 9 curriculum design.
- Resource the consolidated personnel ask across all three objectives (OTs, EXCON cell, HICON, OPFOR cell, scenario design cell, experimentation cell — estimated 30–45 above current establishment at FOC).
- Note the single point of failure: SAG 9/10 SRO, UW raising mission, FDC 5 tactical experimentation lead, and Hameem LVC delivery all sit on SBTDWS without dedicated additional resource — see [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] §6.

### Objective 1 — Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal

- Publish ADW competency profiles and an ADW validation framework (how SBTDWS validates, reports, and escalates).
- Recruit, train, and accredit the OT cadre (selection board, Foundation Training, Supervised Practice, formal accreditation boards).
- Secure command backing for the OT cadre's authority to fail, formalised in a signed writ.
- Commission and fund the Hameem mission rehearsal concept (capital sequence, EXCON infrastructure, instrumentation, J7 LVC integration, J6 digital backbone connectivity).

### Objective 2 — Tactical Experimentation

- Formalise the J3 WDC experimentation governance framework (trigger, approval, reporting, TTP transfer) in writing.
- Confirm SBTDWS's mandate as the PG's field execution arm for tactical experimentation, in writing.
- Stand up and resource the experimentation cell (3–5 personnel with serial design, data capture, and TTP writing capacity).
- Field the UxS portfolio: FPV drones (Priority 1 per TEIW charter), VTOL UxS, land UxS, data capture systems, simulation capacity.
- Negotiate and sign the Makhlab 1 Memorandum of Agreement between J3 WDC and SBTDWS.
- Define and confirm the CAPDEV → PG Innovation Command (PGIC) transition boundary.

### Objective 3 — Training and Course Management

- Complete the 6GW training review and course consolidation plan (retain / consolidate / close decisions per course).
- Map the current military vs. contractor instructor delivery ratio; identify priority skills for military ownership.
- Design and run the instructor development programme using low-tempo (Ramadan/summer) windows.
- Negotiate and formalise the PGI/IWTC pipeline split (individual IW training vs. collective application and validation).
- Issue a Commandant directive establishing honest assessment culture — pass rates reflecting standards, not pressure.

*Shared lessons mechanism (Objective 2 §2.7 / Objective 3 §3.7) — design and operationalise as one cycle, not two. See callout under Phase Two above.*

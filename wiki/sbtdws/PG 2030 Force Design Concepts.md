# PG 2030 Force Design Concepts

A PG-wide (not SBTDWS-specific) framework of numbered "Force Design Concepts" mapping every PG unit and staff branch to a revised 2030 role and an associated concept paper. First seen in two exhibit slides embedded in [[sources/sbtdws-emad-fg-review-2026-05-04]], sourced from a separate 68-page Force Design document authored by [[Foxglove]]. This is the first wiki evidence that SBTDWS/ADWC transformation sits inside a wider, PG-wide force design programme rather than a school-level initiative.

> [!key-insight] IW baseline skill convergence
> Five of six units list "IW as a baseline skill for selected personnel" as a 2030 development line — directly relevant to the [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-04 (IW/UW boundary clarification). This figure confirms IW baseline training is being grown force-wide across manoeuvre/special units in parallel with the PGI/IWTC individual training pipeline SBTDWS partners with — reinforcing why D-04 resolution is urgent: multiple units are designing IW training programmes against an as-yet-undefined IW/UW boundary.

## Relevance to SBTDWS

- Confirms Makhlab 1 sits under a named PG programme ("Programme MAKHLAB"), not an ad hoc SBTDWS/J3 WDC arrangement — see [[Makhlab 1 Project]] and [[UxS CAPDEV Portfolio]].
- SBTDWS's revised role/focus row matches the existing 2030 Intent language verbatim — no contradiction with [[ADWC Transformation Concept]].
- [[Falcons Brigade]]'s own J3 Action Plan (Specialized Technical Skills qualification, transition to unmanned systems) is the real-world worked example behind [[J3 Action Plan Template]] — confirming Falcons Bde's UxS/FPV development line is already formally tabled with J3.
- Five other units (Al Forsan, Falcons Bde, Hamad Bin Easa, SOC, PG Special Guard) are independently growing tactical UxS and IW capability that SBTDWS's collective training, validation, and SAG 9/10 functions will need to absorb as customer demand — reinforces the [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] dependency on "PG Units" as a demand signal.

# PG 2030 Mission Response Categories (MRC)

The four-category readiness benchmark the 21 SAG Mission Sets are validated against. First explicitly defined in [[sources/pg-2030-comd-briefing-pack]] (page 2) — "MRC" had appeared in three earlier wiki pages ([[Specialist Reserve Brigade (SRB)]], [[Programme MAKHLAB]], [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]]) referencing "MRC 2," "MRC 3," and "MRC 4" without any definition being available at the time.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[PG 2030 Warfighting Capability Pillars]] | [[Programme MAKHLAB]]

> [!gap] "MRC" acronym itself not spelled out in any source seen so far
> Neither [[sources/pg-2030-comd-briefing-pack]] nor any earlier source expands "MRC" to full words — the source diagram only labels the box "21 SAG Mission Sets / Readiness Benchmark" with MRC 1–4 beneath it. [[Specialist Reserve Brigade (SRB)]] previously guessed "Military Response Contingencies"; this is an unconfirmed inference, not a sourced expansion. The four category names themselves (Counter Terrorism, Support to Civil Authorities in Crisis, External Operations, Homeland Defense) are directly sourced and high-confidence; the acronym expansion is not. This page's title uses a placeholder expansion for the same reason — treat the parenthetical as descriptive, not confirmed.

---

## The 4 MRCs

| MRC | Name |
|---|---|
| **MRC 1** | Counter Terrorism |
| **MRC 2** | Support to Civil Authorities in Crisis |
| **MRC 3** | External Operations |
| **MRC 4** | Homeland Defense |

These sit alongside the 21 SAG Mission Sets in the [[PG 2030 Warfighting Capability Pillars]] logic chain as the "Readiness Benchmark" — the 21 SAGs are the mission-set structure; the 4 MRCs are the categories of national-level response those mission sets are readied against.

## Retrofitting Earlier References

Now that MRC is defined, the earlier bare references resolve as follows:

- [[Specialist Reserve Brigade (SRB)]]: "Wolf teams at readiness to reinforce Regular PG Bdes for **MRC 2 & 4**" → Support to Civil Authorities in Crisis and Homeland Defense.
- [[Specialist Reserve Brigade (SRB)]] / [[Programme MAKHLAB]] (MAKHLAB 4 task): "reinforcement to Falcons Brigade for **MRC 3**" → External Operations.

> [!gap] MRC-to-SAG mapping not yet captured
> This source names the 4 MRCs but does not show which of the 21 SAGs maps to which MRC(s) — only the SRB references above give partial, indirect evidence (SRB reinforces Falcons Bde for MRC 3; SRB itself readies for MRC 2 & 4). A full SAG-to-MRC crosswalk would need a dedicated source (likely the same PG-wide task register or a J3/J7 readiness product) — flag for a future ingest if one surfaces.

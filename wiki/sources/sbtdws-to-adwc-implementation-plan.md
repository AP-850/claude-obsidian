## What this source adds

**A new organizing taxonomy.** The 9 tasks are regrouped into **5 LOEs** — Authority & Agreements, People & Teams, Standards & Validation, Experiment & Innovate, Facilities & Rehearsal — explicitly built to supersede the EMAD deck's 4 LOEs (Foundations / Structure / Validate & Raise / Experiment & Rehearse). This is a *third* taxonomy for the same 9 tasks alongside the EMAD 4-LOE view (superseded) and the [[SBTDWS Action Plan]]'s 3-Objective structure (still current, not superseded by this document). See [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)]] for the full table and the taxonomy-proliferation note.

**Three decision items not yet in the formal register.** The slide's "Decisions needed from PG HQ by Q4 26" box surfaces *who owns cyber, EMS and space*, *who receives reports*, and *who settles PGI disputes* — none of which currently has a dedicated entry in [[Commander Decisions Register]]. See that page for the cross-reference note.

**Translation discipline.** English military idiom ("authority to fail," "stand up the cadres," "lock the relationships") was systematically replaced with literal phrasing for Arabic translation, preserving all dates, acronyms, gates and milestones. The riskiest phrase — "authority to fail" — is called out explicitly: literal translation inverts the meaning (reads as permission to be unsuccessful). Rendered as "Power to pass or fail a unit."

**MLOC date flagged as an assumption.** Q4 27 is used as the MLOC gate but marked ⚠️ working assumption — the EMAD deck implies the first below-Brigade serial (Q2 27) might be the actual milestone. See the open-question cross-reference on [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]].

## Decisions taken while building the brief (not all endorsed)

1. UW unit raising removed from the slide — the `UW UE concept` and `UW unit decision point` milestones deleted; LOE 3 renamed from "Validate & Raise" to "Standards & Validation."
2. Single vocabulary adopted — EMAD deck's time-band language (2026 Foundations / 2027 Build / 2028 Demonstrate / 2029–30 FOC) dropped in favour of the transformation brief's 4 phases.
3. 5 LOEs, not 4 — Authorities separated out as its own LOE; Experiment split from Rehearse.
4. Owner/resource column removed from the slide at J3's preference, replaced with task-mapping chips.
5. Language simplified for Arabic translation throughout.
6. Format fixed: black and white, plain bordered boxes, italic+underline as the sole convention for undecided/conditional items.

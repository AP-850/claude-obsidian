# PG 2030 Three Themes

The three overarching themes of the PG 2030 / Al Emad transformation vision that frame the entire SBTDWS development programme. Everything SBTDWS builds must serve at least one of these themes.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[PG 2030 Directive]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]]

---

## Theme 1: Adaptive by Nature

PG must evolve faster than adversaries. This means moving beyond a narrow counter-terrorism focus to prepare for the full spectrum:
- Conventional operations
- Irregular warfare
- Grey-zone operations
- Covert and clandestine operations
- Deniable operations

**For SBTDWS:** Training design must prepare forces for ambiguity and adaptation, not just rehearsed task completion. Assessment must challenge adaptability, not just compliance.

---

## Theme 2: Innovative by Design

Innovation is a cultural imperative, not merely a technical function. The PG must:
- Encourage experimentation
- Accept failure in training environments as learning
- Challenge old methods deliberately
- Rapidly adopt emerging capabilities
- Integrate uncrewed and autonomous systems across domains

**For SBTDWS:** The ATI-Hub / TEIW supports this theme. But the culture of innovation — accepting failure, challenging instructors, experimenting — must be embedded in the institution, not just in a dedicated cell. Contrast with [[SBTDWS Barriers and Blockers]] where the current culture does the opposite.

---

## Theme 3: Constantly Ready

Readiness must be continuous and digitally enabled. The PG must:
- Embed secure communications across the force
- Enable data-driven decision-making
- Build cyber awareness at individual and collective level
- Develop EMS awareness
- Integrate combat support and service support into all training ORBATs
- Implement FE@R-style (Force Elements at Readiness) readiness models

**For SBTDWS:** SAG 9 and SAG 10 validation are the accountability mechanism for this theme. See [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]]. Hameem mission rehearsal is where Constantly Ready is tested in a realistic pre-deployment environment.

---

## How the Three Themes Frame SBTDWS Work

| Theme | SBTDWS mechanism | Key dependency |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| Adaptive by Nature | IW/UW training, lessons-to-training loop, EXCON scenario complexity | J3 WDC governance; PGI/IWTC pipeline |
| Innovative by Design | ATI-Hub, TEIW, FPV program, tactical experimentation | PGIC transition; J3 WDC sanction |
| Constantly Ready | SAG 9/10 validation, Hameem, OT cadre, digital training backbone | J6 digital backbone; J7 LVC architecture; HQ endorsement |

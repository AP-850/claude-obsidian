# Force Generation Pipeline — Terms of Reference

**Document type:** Terms of Reference (ToR)
**Status:** DRAFT v0.1 — for Commandant review prior to J3/J7 engagement
**Parties:** Presidential Guard J7 Training Authority | SBTDWS / ADWC | Presidential Guard J3 Operations
**Target signature:** Q2 2026 (OVERDUE — see [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-02)

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Commander Decisions Register]] | [[SBTDWS 9 Directed Tasks]]

> [!key-insight] Why this document exists
> SBTDWS has no formal standing in the PG force generation system. J7 has no obligation to provide resources. This ToR creates that standing and defines the lane split so both institutions can plan against a shared architecture.

---

## 1. Background and Authority

### 1.1 Directing Authority

This Terms of Reference is issued under direction from the Presidential Guard Command (PGC) and in accordance with the PG 2030 Force Design Review (FDR 2030). It implements Task 6 of the nine directed tasks assigned to SBTDWS: *Support the J7 Exercise Programme as the collective training and validation node for the Presidential Guard.*

Reference: [[PG 2030 Directive]] Task 6; [[SBTDWS 9 Directed Tasks]] Task 6.

### 1.2 Context

SBTDWS is transforming from a desert warfare school into the PG All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC). A core function of ADWC is collective training and validation — assessing PG units against the 19 Directed Military Tasks (DMTs) spanning the four Military Readiness Capabilities (MRCs). This function requires integration with the J7 exercise programme, access to J7 exercise resources, and formal recognition of SBTDWS as a validation node within the PG training architecture.

Without this ToR, SBTDWS operates outside the formal force generation system. J7 has no obligation to support SBTDWS-led validation events. SBTDWS cannot integrate its EXCON and HICON functions into J7 LVC architecture. Validation events cannot be incorporated into the PG exercise programme.

### 1.3 Parties to This Agreement

| Party | Role |
|-------|------|
| **J7 Training Authority** (PG HQ) | Training system owner; exercise programme authority; LVC architecture; doctrine custodian |
| **SBTDWS / ADWC** (Commandant) | Collective training and validation executor; CTC operator; EXCON/HICON/OT cadre; SRO for SAG 9 and SAG 10 |
| **J3 Operations** (PG HQ) | Operational demand signal; readiness requirements; exercise programme tasking authority |

J3 is a signatory as the operational authority that generates the readiness requirement SBTDWS and J7 are jointly serving.

---

## 2. Purpose

This ToR establishes:

1. The **lane split** between J7 and SBTDWS in the PG force generation pipeline — what each institution owns, executes, and is accountable for
2. The **integration architecture** governing how SBTDWS collective training and validation events nest within the J7 exercise programme
3. The **resource provisions** J7 will extend to SBTDWS for collective validation events
4. The **reporting chain** for validation outcomes and readiness assessments
5. The **governance mechanism** for deconfliction, planning, and dispute resolution

---

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In Scope

- Collective training events at company level and above delivered or facilitated by SBTDWS
- Validation and readiness assessment against the 19 DMTs / 4 MRCs
- Mission rehearsal exercises (MRX) conducted at SBTDWS and Hameem training areas
- SAG 9 (Combined Unconventional Warfare) and SAG 10 (Resilience and Capacity Building) validation events for which SBTDWS holds SRO accountability
- EXCON and HICON functions operated by SBTDWS in support of collective training events
- Observer Trainer (OT) cadre operations under J7 standards and OT accreditation framework

### 3.2 Out of Scope

- Individual skills training (delivered by SBTDWS under existing course programme authority — no J7 ToR required)
- J7-directed exercises where SBTDWS provides only logistic or range support (covered under separate support arrangements)
- CAPDEV and tactical experimentation activities (governed separately under the J3 WDC — see [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-11)
- National-level or multi-force exercises directed by Higher HQ above PG level

## 4. Lane Split — J7 and SBTDWS

### 4.1 J7 Owns

| Function | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Exercise programme direction | Annual and quarterly exercise programme; priorities; scheduling; deconfliction |
| LVC architecture | Live, Virtual, Constructive integration standards; instrumentation protocols; simulation systems |
| OT accreditation standard | The standard against which SBTDWS OTs are trained and certified |
| Doctrine custodianship | OT handbook; exercise design doctrine; assessment standards |
| J7 exercise programme resources | J7-controlled OPFOR, instrumentation, range assets, simulation nodes available on request |
| Lessons learned system | Formal capture and dissemination of exercise observations across the PG |
| SOP and standards publication | Publication authority for updated tactics, techniques, and procedures emerging from exercises |

### 4.2 SBTDWS Owns

| Function | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Exercise execution | EXCON, HICON, OPFOR, and OT functions for SBTDWS-led collective training and validation events |
| Scenario design | Scenario development and scenario library for SBTDWS validation events, based on J7-endorsed design templates |
| OT cadre management | Recruitment, training, accreditation, and deployment of the SBTDWS OT cadre against the J7 standard |
| Validation assessment and reporting | Assessment of units against the 19 DMTs; formal readiness report to SRO and Division HQ |
| SAG 9 and SAG 10 SRO accountability | Execution authority and assessment reporting for PG SAG 9 and SAG 10 validation, under endorsed standards |
| Hameem mission rehearsal (Phase 2+) | Concept of use, site design, and execution of MRX at Hameem; J7 integration architecture applied |
| Asset and range scheduling | SBTDWS training areas, equipment, and support resources for events under SBTDWS execution authority |

## 5. Resource Provisions

J7 agrees to make the following resources available to SBTDWS for collective validation events, subject to the advance request process defined in Section 4.3:

| Resource | Provision |
|---------|-----------|
| OPFOR elements | J7-controlled OPFOR or coordination to source from J3 when J7 OPFOR is unavailable |
| Instrumentation | J7 instrumentation nodes (battle tracking, data capture, scoring systems) available for SBTDWS-executed events |
| LVC simulation access | J7 simulation nodes and constructive environment available for integration into SBTDWS validation events |
| J7 liaison | A designated J7 liaison officer assigned to SBTDWS collective training events (pre-event planning and event execution) |
| Standards materials | J7 exercise design templates, OT handbook, DMT assessment criteria, and AAR frameworks provided to SBTDWS |
| Training programme slot | One SBTDWS-led validation event per quarter incorporated into the J7 exercise programme at IOC; scaling to exercise programme integration as SBTDWS matures |

## 6. Validation Reporting Chain

Assessment reports from SBTDWS-led validation events will follow this chain:

```
SBTDWS OT Cadre (assessment)
        │
        ▼
SBTDWS Commandant (endorses report)
        │
        ├──► SRO / Unit Commander (formal readiness report)
        │
        ├──► J7 Training Authority (lessons and standards update)
        │
        └──► J3 Operations (readiness status input to force generation)
```

**Adverse assessments:** Where SBTDWS assesses a unit as not meeting the required standard, the report is endorsed by the Commandant and submitted simultaneously to the SRO and J3. J7 receives a copy for lessons purposes. The assessment stands unless formally reviewed under the dispute resolution mechanism in Section 7.

## 7. Governance

### 7.1 Joint Planning Cell

A Force Generation Planning Cell (FGPC) comprising J7, SBTDWS, and J3 representatives will convene [quarterly / bi-annually — TBD] to:

- Review the exercise programme and deconflict scheduling
- Review resource allocation against planned events
- Escalate unresolved resource or standards disputes to the relevant command authority
- Review and update this ToR on an annual basis

### 7.2 Dispute Resolution

1. Working-level: J7 and SBTDWS representatives resolve within 5 working days
2. Commandant level: If unresolved, Commandant SBTDWS and J7 Director engage directly within 10 working days
3. J3 arbitration: If still unresolved, J3 makes the determination within 15 working days
4. Escalation: Any matter touching validation authority (whether a unit can be failed) escalates to PG HQ — not resolved at this level

### 7.3 Review and Amendment

This ToR is reviewed annually by the FGPC. Amendments require signature from all three parties. Major amendments (affecting lane split, resource provisions, or reporting chain) require PG HQ notification.

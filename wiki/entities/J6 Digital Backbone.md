# J6 Digital Backbone

The J6-owned secure comms, C4I and data-systems infrastructure that all cyber, EMS and space training overlays depend on, plus LVC connectivity at [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept|Hameem]]. Cannot be self-generated by SBTDWS.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept]] | [[J7 LVC Architecture]]

---

## Why it matters

Listed as a direct dependency in both [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] §4.5 ("secure comms, C4I, data systems at Hameem; connectivity to SBTDWS") and the [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)|5-LOE implementation slide]]: without it, "all cyber, EMS and space training overlays; C4I integration; LVC connectivity at Hameem" cannot be delivered by SBTDWS alone.

- [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] treats J6 digital backbone as a Phase 2 (Q4 27 – Q1 29) requirement, with manual data capture available as a Phase 1 fallback if delayed — flagged as a Medium-likelihood, Medium-impact risk in that plan's risk register.
- Directly tied to the unresolved domain-scope decision: J6 ownership of cyber, EMS and space training overlays is one of the explicit open items in the 5-LOE slide's "Decisions needed from PG HQ by Q4 26" — *who owns cyber, EMS and space?* — not yet tracked as a formal decision ID in [[Commander Decisions Register]].

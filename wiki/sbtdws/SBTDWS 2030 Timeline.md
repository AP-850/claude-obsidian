## Calendar Opportunity

Ramadan and summer training gaps (approx. 4 months annually) are a structural opportunity for:
- Instructor development programme delivery
- WG planning sessions
- Experimentation preparation
- Staff course attendance

Plan deliberately around these windows rather than treating them as dead time.

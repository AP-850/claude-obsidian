## The 9 Directed Tasks

Detailed mapping against current capability (from SBTDWS_Mission_Analysis.pptx and bilingual brief):

### Task 1: Deliver Collective Training to Company Level
- **Status: READY**
- CTW already delivers company collective training. The core readiness function is in place.
- Action: Expand to formation level; add multi-domain integration layer to exercises.

---

### Task 2: Provide Validation and Assessment
- **Status: PARTIAL**
- E&E Wing delivers FST (squad-level evaluation only).
- Gap: No trained OT cadre for SAG 9 (Combined UW) or SAG 10 (R&CB) validation. No authority to fail.
- Action: Retrain E&E Wing as OT cadre; establish validation authority; phase out FST.

---

### Task 3: Conduct Mission Rehearsal
- **Status: GAP**
- No EXCON capability, instrumentation, or scenario design infrastructure.
- Hameem is the target location but requires capital development (FY27–FY30).
- Action: Hameem capital plan; J7 MoU on LVC architecture; HICON/EXCON SOP development.

---

### Task 4: Deliver Tactical Experimentation (FDC 5)
- **Status: DEVELOPING**
- CAPDEV Cell has mature projects but no experimentation governance.
- No formal link to J3 WDC. No governed methodology.
- Action: Makhlab 1 PoC framework; J3 WDC MoA; refocus CAPDEV Cell toward FDC 5.

---

### Task 5: Develop Doctrine and Standards
- **Status: PARTIAL**
- SAT Cell performs course design and QC — the foundation exists.
- Gap: No ADW validation framework, no OT accreditation standard, no ADW competency profiles by FE type.
- Action: SAT Cell evolves to Doctrine and Training Authority; ADW validation framework v0.1 by Q4 26.

---

### Task 6: Support the J7 Exercise Programme
- **Status: GAP**
- No established EXCON contribution to J7.
- No Force Generation Pipeline ToR defining the SBTDWS / J7 lane split.
- Action: Force Generation Pipeline ToR (J3/J7/SBTDWS) — target Q2 26 signature.

---

### Task 7: Integrate with PG J-Staff Functions
- **Status: GAP**
- No J2, J3, J5, J6, or J8 departments at SBTDWS.
- Action: PG HQ J-Staff support decision required before Q4 26 structural reorganisation.

---

### Task 8: Enable All-Domain Training
- **Status: GAP**
- Land and Air domains accessible. Maritime, Cyber, Space, EM not.
- Action: J6 digital backbone required; coalition and J6 support for cyber/EM; Hameem beach for maritime node.

---

### Task 9: Drive PG Capability Adaptation
- **Status: GAP**
- No mechanism to feed experimentation outputs into doctrine, acquisition, or force design.
- Action: FDC 5 pipeline under J3 WDC; Transition Gate model; DLOD/FIC fielding packages.

---

## Mapping Logic (from Initial Assessment Brief)

The 9-task mapping slide (Slide 3) used a PARTIAL / READY / GAP framework:

- **READY**: Capability exists and is being delivered.
- **PARTIAL**: Foundation exists but significant gaps in scope, authority, or scale.
- **DEVELOPING**: Work in progress; concept defined, not yet operational.
- **GAP**: Capability does not exist; requires new structure, authority, or investment.

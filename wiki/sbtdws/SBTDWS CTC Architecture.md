## Purpose and Objectives

SBTDWS as CTC must:
- Assess each unit's readiness against the 19 Directed Military Tasks spanning the 4 MRCs
- Provide a central node for exercise design, scenario development, OT management, and assessment facilitation
- Support the command chain from Division HQ through SROs/Commanders to SBTDWS

## Exercise Control Architecture

### EXCON Cell
- Led by dedicated EXCON Director (under J3 oversight)
- Manages scenario flow, event injects, participant response monitoring
- Communicates real-time scenario changes: weather, threat updates, terrain

### OPFOR Cell
- Skilled staff replicating realistic adversary TTPs
- Coordinates with J2 for intelligence-driven scenario complexity
- Adapts tactics in real-time to test Blue Force readiness

### Observer-Trainer (OT) Teams
- Assigned to monitor unit performance and provide real-time feedback
- Must be trained in: desert warfare doctrine, all 19 Directed Military Tasks, exercise scenario
- Capture performance data on: command and control, logistical solutions, readiness indicators
- Feed directly into AAR process

### Higher Control (HICON)
- Strategic-level control element above EXCON
- Sets conditions for the exercise; approves major injects; manages overall exercise coherence

## Exercise Design Principles

1. **Task-mapped design** — all 19 DMTs across 4 MRCs must be testable within the exercise
2. **Environmental integration** — extreme heat, water scarcity, desert terrain, dust and visibility constraints
3. **Scalable complexity** — multi-unit → joint → combined operations as the programme matures
4. **Multi-domain injects** — land, air, cyber, C2 coordination, external agency integration
5. **Sustainment embedded** — logistics, medical, maintenance, CASEVAC included from scenario start (not added late)

## Administrative and Resource Support

- **Asset booking system** — central registry for vehicles, aircraft, simulators, equipment; managed by SBTDWS
- **Range booking** — deconflicted scheduling for all training areas and facilities
- **Medical support** — forward medical element with CASEVAC plan
- **Safety management** — exercise safety officer; safety limits for live fire, aviation, pyrotechnics
- **Communications plan** — exercise net structure, frequency management, secure radio allocation

---

## Assessment and AAR Framework

1. **Pre-exercise**: set standards against each DMT; brief SRO on evaluation criteria
2. **During exercise**: OT observations recorded continuously; EXCON data capture
3. **Immediate debrief**: hot wash within 2 hours of exercise close
4. **Formal AAR**: structured review against standards; scored against each DMT
5. **Report to SRO / Division**: formal readiness assessment; pass/partial/gap against each MRC
6. **Remediation pathway**: units that do not meet standard receive a remediation recommendation — not just an observation

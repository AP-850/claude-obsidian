## 3. Common failure points / issues others have hit

### 3.1 The “Valley of Death” - stuck between prototype and real fielding

- Even DIU has been criticised for not always getting technologies across the line into sustained programs, partly due to **unclear metrics, limited buy-in from the traditional acquisition system, and fragmented collaboration** with other innovation orgs. ([MITRE](https://www.mitre.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/PR-25-0960-Mission-Focused-Industry-Driven-Rapid-Acquisition.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

**Mitigation for you:**

- Make each project start with **a named “sponsor unit” and “adoption pathway”** (e.g. “CTW will own this system if it works”).  
- Build a **formal “Acceptance to Service” gate** with DLOD/FIC criteria signed by higher command.

---

### 3.2 Culture clash: operators vs tech vs bureaucracy

- Innovation hubs repeatedly report that **cultural issues** (suspicion of outsiders, fear of failure, rigid risk aversion) slow things more than technology does. This is visible in broader DoD innovation commentary and MITRE studies on rapid acquisition barriers. ([MITRE](https://www.mitre.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/PR-25-0960-Mission-Focused-Industry-Driven-Rapid-Acquisition.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

**Mitigation:**

- Choose staff who are **bilingual in both worlds** (ex-operators with engineering interest, and engineers who can live in the field).  
- Make “fail fast, learn fast” a command-endorsed principle with **low-stakes experimentation** where early failures are expected.

---

### 3.3 Metrics & performance management

- GAO observations on DIU highlight **inconsistent performance metrics** and difficulty proving impact to Congress/leadership. ([Government Accountability Office](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-25-106856.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

**Mitigation:**

- From day one, define a small, ruthless set of **key metrics**, e.g.:
  - # of projects reaching field trials per year.
  - Time from idea → first prototype → operational trial.
  - # of systems transitioned to a unit with full DLOD package.
  - Operator satisfaction and adoption rate.
  - Cost per capability vs imported alternatives.

---

### 3.4 IP, attribution & export controls

- Dual-use accelerators like DIANA and DIU frequently wrestle with IP, export control, and dual-use complications - especially for autonomy, UxS, and EW. ([Security and Defence](https://securityanddefence.pl/Collaboration-among-NATO-s-defence-innovators-Lessons-from-Poland%2C205139%2C0%2C2.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com))  

**Your angle:**

- You want **non-attributable systems** and ideally no obvious national signature. That’s achievable, but you’ll need:
  - Early **legal and compliance advice** baked into the process.  
  - Clear agreements on whether IP sits with the state, the hub, or partners.  
  - Controls around what tech can/can’t be exported, or given to proxies.

# Presidential Special Guard (PSG)

PG unit responsible for National Special Protection Systems, Routine Protective Security, and VIP Exfiltration during conflict.

Navigation: [[entities/_index]] | [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Programme MAKHLAB]] | [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]]

> [!key-insight] Confirmed (2026-08-05): same entity as "SG" in Force Design Concepts
> [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]] lists a unit "SG" ("VVIP & Critical Infrastructure protection" → "Special Protection Operations," Force Design Concept 2). Confirmed by the user: this is the same unit as PSG — just a different naming convention between sources, the same way SBTDWS is sometimes written SBTS. "PSG" (Presidential Special Guard) is the more descriptive, current name.

## Develop by 2030

1. Covert UxS
2. Special Protection Ops — enhancing ability to investigate and counter emerging threats; increasing PSG's contingent response capability — **MAKHLAB 3**, shared serial with [[Special Operations Command (SOC)]]
3. Investigation as a baseline skill for selected personnel

---

## Tasks (Aug 2026 register)

- Responsible PG unit for National Special Protection Systems (UxS)
- MAKHLAB 3 — develop, test, and refine Special Protection operating concept to increase PG's contingent response capability
- Responsible PG unit for Routine Protective Security (SAG 5)
- Responsible PG unit for Exfiltration of VIPs due to conflict (SAG 21)
- Act as Training Requirements Authority (TRA) for all PSG training

Each SAG (4, 5, 21) follows the standard OPS pass-off chain: get approval from J3, pass off to J7 for training.

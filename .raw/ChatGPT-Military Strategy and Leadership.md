## **2. Five traits of leaders who successfully transform organisations**

### **(1) They define a clear strategic “North Star”**

Great reformers create a **compelling destination** that is:

- Simple to understand  
- Hard to argue with  
- Ambitious but credible  
- Operational, not rhetorical  

Examples in military history:

- “AirLand Battle”
- “Network-centric warfare”
- “Mission Command”
- “Special operations as a strategic capability, not just tactical force”

**For DWS → ADWC**, this might be framed as:

> “From a Desert Warfare School → to the region’s premier All-Domain Warfare Centre for experimentation, training, and operational innovation.”

---

### **(2) They separate identity from function**

Many organisations get stuck because they say:

> “We are a school, therefore we must only train.”

Great strategists ask instead:

> “What are we trying to achieve? What role should this institution play in the force?”

This is critical for your case.

DWS today is seen as:
- A training school  
- A course provider  
- A tactical training institution  

A strategist would ask:

- What does the Presidential Guard actually need from this institution in 2030?
- Should it still be “just a school”?
- Or should it be a strategic enabler for warfare development?

This is the mindset shift that unlocks ADWC.

---

### **(3) They reshape incentives before values**

Classic mistake:  
Leaders try to “change mindset” through speeches and workshops.

Smart leaders instead:

- Change promotions  
- Change reporting lines  
- Change budgets  
- Change who gets rewarded  
- Change what success looks like  

Then behaviour changes automatically.

For example:

If DWS is still measured only on:
- Course throughput  
- Pass rates  
- Training hours  

Then no one will care about innovation or all-domain warfare.

But if new success metrics include:

- Number of experiments conducted  
- New tactics validated  
- New capabilities integrated  
- Interoperability trials run  
- Lessons learned exported to the force  

Then the culture will change naturally.

### **(4) They design structures that force collaboration**

Great military reforms redesign structures so that cooperation is unavoidable.

Framework: **Structure drives behaviour more than leadership.**

Applied to DWS:

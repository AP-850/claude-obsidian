# SBTDWS CAPDEV Projects

The existing Capability Development portfolio at SBTDWS. These projects are the "multi-domain seeds" that make ADWC transformation viable — SBTDWS is already experimenting across land, air, information, and sustainment domains.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Three Development Thematics]] | [[ATI-Hub Concept]]

---

## Summary Assessment

> SBTDWS is approximately 70% of the way to an All Domain Warfare Centre. The CAPDEV portfolio is the evidence. Projects exist, but they currently run as separate initiatives rather than structured experiments with hypotheses, measures of effectiveness, and doctrine outputs.

**The transformation of CAPDEV:** Projects → Structured Experiments → Doctrine.

---

## Portfolio by Domain

### Air Domain

**Paramotor Capability**
- Roles: Reconnaissance, resupply, mobility
- Current state: Trial programme in progress
- Future experiment hypothesis: Paramotors extend reconnaissance reach by 5x over dismounted patrols
- Integration potential: ISR feed into Exercise EXCON; TTPs for collective exercises

**FPV Drone Program (Priority)**
- Roles: Strike, ISR, harassment, sensor delivery
- Target endstate: Ten 10-man FPV teams with organic production capability (0–100 units)
- FPV Schoolhouse to train operators, maintainers, and planners
- Living FPV TTP publication updated after each exercise/trial
- Reserve Brigade as the standing manpower pipeline

**Drone Resupply Concept**
- Roles: Autonomous aerial logistics, forward resupply
- Integration: Dispersed sustainment under extreme heat; links to water generation CAPDEV
- Doctrine output target: Autonomous logistics TTP for company-level operations

---

### Land Domain

**UGV (Unmanned Ground Vehicle)**
- Roles: Resupply, CASEVAC, overwatch
- Current state: Trial programme
- Future experiment: UGV reduces CASEVAC risk in defended areas; resupply reach in dismounted operations
- Integration: Feeds into Collective Training Wing exercises; links to Razor Ambulance

**Electric Motorcycles**
- Roles: Reconnaissance, mobility, signature reduction
- Advantage: Near-silent mobility; reduced thermal and acoustic signature
- Integration: Counter-detection and patrolling TTPs; links to camouflage CAPDEV

**Razor Ambulance Platform**
- Roles: CASEVAC in austere environments
- Future: UGV-CASEVAC integration; drone-assisted casualty extraction
- Links to: Medical Training (E&E Wing), UGV, drone resupply

**Vehicle Simulator**
- Roles: Training technology, driver training reduction, synthetic exercise support
- Integration: LVC architecture when connected to EXCON and BMS

---

### Information Domain

**OPFOR / Role Player Program**
- Roles: Realistic adversary simulation, hybrid threat scenarios
- Includes: FPV threats, EW operators, IW actors, deception elements
- Critical for: SAG 9/10 validation serials; hybrid threat realism in collective exercises

**Decoy Vehicles**
- Roles: Deception operations, sensor spoofing, concealment practice
- Integration: Counter-detection TTPs; camouflage CAPDEV

**Multi-Spectral Camouflage**
- Roles: Signature management against thermal, optical, and radar sensors
- Links to: Counter-UAS/UxS training; patrolling and concealment TTPs

---

### Sustainment Domain

**Water Generation (WaterGen)**
- Roles: Decentralised water supply in extreme heat
- Strategic significance: Removes a critical logistics constraint in deep desert operations
- Partner: WaterGen (commercial)

## Structured Experiment Model (Future State)

Instead of running projects, CAPDEV Cell will run **structured experiments** under FDC 5 governance:

**Before (current):**
- Trial the paramotor
- Report results informally

**After (target):**
- Define hypothesis (e.g., "Paramotors extend recon reach by 5x over dismounted")
- Run trial during a Collective Exercise
- Evaluation by TEX element / E&E Wing
- Produce doctrine output: TTP, equipment spec, training requirement
- Transition gate: does it go to unit adoption? prime contractor? shelved?

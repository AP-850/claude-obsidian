# Three Development Thematics

The three strategic thematics that define SBTDWS's transformation identity. Each connects to Comd PG's language and provides the content backbone for the 2028 thematic exercise year.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]] | [[SBTDWS CAPDEV Projects]]

---

## Overview

The three thematics were established in the Initial Assessment Brief (Slides 11–13) as the visible face of transformation. They are **not separate programs** — they are the lens through which all SBTDWS activity is presented to higher command and the force.

Each has a direct commander's phrase, a supporting CAPDEV base, and a clear SAG / FDC linkage.

---

## Thematic 1 — Experimentation to Adaptation

**Commander's phrase:** "Experimentation to Adaptation" (Comd PG's catchphrase)

**Core concept:** SBTDWS as the learning machine for PG. Every trial or exercise generates a tactical lesson. Every lesson feeds doctrine or equipment change. The loop: Experiment → Evaluate → Adapt → Redeploy.

**FDC linkage:** FDC 5 — Tactical Experimentation. This is SBTDWS's contribution to the PG Force Design Cycle.

**Execution node:** CAPDEV Cell (refocused as Makhlab 1 / FDC 5 execution arm under J3 WDC governance).

**Sub-concept: Makhlab 1**
The first governed experimentation sprint. Falcons Brigade / SOF as the partner force. Produces the first TTP output under FDC 5. Target: Q1 27.

**Capability development cycle (slide 11 framing):**
1. Problem Definition — operator problem statement, Problem Sponsor assigned
2. Sprint Event — 3–5 day rapid co-development with operators + engineers
3. Prototype Trial — 3–6 month prototype under TEX/EXCON control
4. Doctrine Output — TTP publication, DLOD/FIC package
5. Handover — to sponsor unit or prime manufacturer

**CAPDEV projects that feed this thematic:**
- Paramotor (reconnaissance reach)
- UGV (autonomous resupply)
- FPV Drones (strike and ISR)
- Electric Motorcycles (mobility and signature reduction)
- Drone Resupply (autonomous logistics)

## Thematic 2 — UxS Tactical Employment

**Commander's phrase:** "Training PG to fight as it would choose to"

**Core concept:** SBTDWS as the home of Unmanned System (UxS) tactical employment — not developing the systems, but developing the TTPs, training pipelines, and validation standards for how to use them in desert operations.

**Coverage (from slide 12):**

| Domain | Systems | Roles |
|--------|---------|-------|
| Ground | UGV (resupply, CASEVAC, overwatch) | Autonomous logistics, crew protection |
| Air | FPV drones, paramotors, drone resupply | ISR, strike, mobility, resupply |
| Maritime | Future (Hameem beach) | TBD |

**FPV Drone Program (priority effort):**
- Ten 10-man FPV teams as target endstate
- Organic production of airframes (0–100 units)
- FPV Schoolhouse under ATI-Hub / Innovation Command
- Living FPV TTP publication updated after every exercise and trial
- Reserve Brigade integration as the manpower pipeline

**Key principle:** Competitive element (leaderboards, Red vs Blue exercises) accelerates TTP development. SOF and FPV communities thrive on this model.

**DLOD/FIC requirement:** Every UxS capability requires a Fielding Dossier covering doctrine, organisation, training, materiel, infrastructure, personnel, sustainment, and C2/information.

## Thematic 3 — Irregular and Unconventional Warfare (IW/UW)

**Note:** This thematic was updated from "Information Warfare Integration" based on user feedback. IW/UW is more accurate to SBTDWS's SAG 9 authority and existing SERE/desert skills base.

**Core concept:** SBTDWS as the PG validation authority for Combined UW (SAG 9) and R&CB (SAG 10) operations. IW/UW is a crosscutting thematic — it runs through collective exercises, validation serials, and individual training.

**Why IW/UW is core to ADWC (slide 13 reasoning):**
- SAG 9 is SBTDWS's existing Special Reconnaissance and Observation (SRO) responsibility
- Existing SERE and desert survival skills are foundational UW competencies
- Hybrid threat demand is growing — ADWC must account for non-state actors, proxies, and unconventional tactics

**What ADWC must deliver:**
- UW training continuum (individual SERE → collective UW operations)
- SAG 9 validation framework (OT cadre, evaluation standards)
- Hybrid threat scenarios (OPFOR with FPV, EW, deception, IW)
- Partner and SOF integration (Falcons Brigade, international partners)

**PGI linkage:** PGI/IWTC teaches IW qualifications. SBTDWS practises and validates them. The pipeline: PGI teaches → SBTDWS validates. This requires a formal IW pipeline protocol.

## 2028 Thematic Showcase Year

All three thematics converge in 2028 as a public-facing showcase:
- Exercise(s) designed around each thematic
- International observer and partner invitations
- Doctrine outputs published
- Lessons captured and fed into Phase 3 planning

This is the "prove it" moment before FOC declaration in 2030.

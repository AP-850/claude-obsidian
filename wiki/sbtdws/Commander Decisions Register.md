# Commander Decisions Register

All pending command decisions required to unlock SBTDWS transformation. Consolidated from the O6 Command Brief, FIC Analysis, SAG 9/10 analysis, and transformation workstream.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ADWC Command Brief (5-Slide)]] | [[ADWC FIC Analysis]]

> [!key-insight] Read this register in tiers
> Tier 1 decisions unblock Year 1 and are either overdue or due Q2 26. Tier 2 decisions gate the IOC (Q1 29). Tier 3 decisions gate FOC (Q3 30). Decisions in Tier 1 cascade — a delay here is a direct delay to every downstream milestone.

> [!note] Consolidated for submission: [[RFI to PG HQ]] (2026-07-28)
> The Tier 2/3 decisions here that are genuinely PG HQ/Higher HQ questions (D-04 through D-11) — plus the untracked items from the 5-LOE slide and MDWC Six-Domain Study — have been consolidated into a single prioritised RFI document for the new PG 2030 advisor to submit upward. Tier 1 (D-01/02/03) is excluded there since those are SBTDWS's own O6 CoC actions, not PG HQ questions.

## Tier 1 — Unlock Year 1 (Required: Now to Q2 26)

---

### D-01: Endorse the Three-Wing Design
**Decision owner:** O6 CoC (Commandant)
**Status:** PENDING

**What must be decided:**
Formally endorse the ADWC three-wing structure — Desert Warfare Wing (feeder), Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal Wing (main effort), Tactical Experimentation and Innovation Wing (narrow) — as the design basis for SBTDWS transformation.

**What it gates:**
- Commandant authority to plan the structural reorganisation
- Ability to communicate the design to the force and J-Staff partners
- Phase 1 planning can begin

**What happens if not decided:**
Transformation work proceeds without organisational mandate. Staff resistance will be unmanaged. External partners (J7, J3 WDC) cannot be engaged with a clear institutional ask.

**Hard ask:**
None — this is conceptual endorsement, not a resource commitment. It should be the easiest of the three O6 decisions.

**Source:** [[ADWC Command Brief (5-Slide)]] Slide 5, Decision 1

---

### D-02: Direct Force Generation Pipeline ToR Engagement
**Decision owner:** O6 CoC (Commandant) directing to J3 and J7
**Status:** PENDING — OVERDUE (Q2 26 target)
**Target:** Q2 26

**What must be decided:**
Commandant directs SBTDWS to formally engage J3 and J7 to draft and sign the Force Generation Pipeline ToR — the document that defines the SBTDWS / J7 lane split for collective training and validation.

**What it gates:**
- Formalises SBTDWS's validation lane in the PG training architecture
- Unlocks access to J7 exercise programme resources
- Enables HICON / EXCON planning with J7
- Gives SBTDWS legitimate standing in the force generation system

**What happens if not decided:**
SBTDWS operates outside the formal training architecture. J7 has no obligation to support. Validation events cannot be integrated into the PG exercise programme.

**Hard ask:**
The Commandant must direct the J3 and J7 engagement — SBTDWS cannot self-initiate this at sufficient authority.

**Source:** [[ADWC Command Brief (5-Slide)]] Slide 4 and 5; [[SBTDWS 9 Directed Tasks]] Task 6

---

### D-03: Commit to Backing the First Adverse Assessment
**Decision owner:** O6 CoC (Commandant)
**Status:** PENDING

**What must be decided:**
Commandant commits — in advance — to visibly supporting the SBTDWS OT cadre's first adverse readiness assessment when a unit commander challenges it.

**What it gates:**
- The OT cadre's authority to fail is only real if command backing is certain before the first challenge
- Without this, OTs will self-censor and the entire certification function collapses
- Determines whether the validation function is real or advisory

**What happens if not decided:**
OTs conduct assessments but will not fail units they know command will not back. Inflated pass rates continue. SAG 9/10 validation becomes advisory.

**Hard ask:**
This is the hardest O6 decision. It asks the Commandant to pre-commit to absorbing political pressure from a subordinate unit's commander. The speaker note in the command brief states this directly: *"An OT cadre without command backing is an audience."*

**Source:** [[ADWC Command Brief (5-Slide)]] Slide 5, Decision 3; [[SBTDWS Barriers and Blockers]]

## Tier 2 — Unlock IOC (Required: Q3 26 to Q2 27)

---

### D-04: IW / UW Boundary Clarification
**Decision owner:** Higher HQ (above O6 CoC)
**Status:** PENDING — BLOCKS MULTIPLE DOWNSTREAM ITEMS
**Required by:** Q3 26 (before OT curriculum design can complete)

**What must be decided:**
Higher HQ must formally define the boundary between:
- **(a) Pl(-) Irregular Warfare capability** — being grown across all PG units through individual IW courses delivered by PGI / IWTC
- **(b) Unconventional Warfare capability** — being handed off from SOC as a force-generation product to specialist sub-units

**What it gates:**
- SAG 9 validation scope and standards (cannot write standards without knowing what is being validated)
- OT curriculum design for SAG 9 tasks
- PGI / IWTC scope and throughput requirements
- SOC handover plan and timelines
- SBTDWS collective validation design

**What happens if not decided:**
SBTDWS designs OT curriculum to an assumed scope that may be wrong. SAG 9 standards remain undefined. IW training pipeline (PGI / IWTC) may be misaligned to the actual requirement.

**Who raises it:** O6 CoC raises to PG HQ. SBTDWS cannot resolve this alone.

**Source:** [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]]; [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] Key Issue 1; [[Unconventional Warfare and Resilience]]

---

### D-05: Validation Authority
**Decision owner:** Higher HQ (PG HQ level)
**Status:** PENDING
**Required by:** Q4 26 (before first SAG validation event)

**What must be decided:**
Define the formal authority SBTDWS holds in readiness validation:
- Can SBTDWS **certify** (issue a formal readiness certificate)?
- Can SBTDWS **fail** (record an adverse assessment with command consequences)?
- Can SBTDWS **recommend remediation** (direct a unit to return for re-validation)?
- Or is SBTDWS **advisory only** (observes and reports, no formal consequence)?

**What it gates:**
- Whether validation events have operational consequence (forces allocation, deployment decisions, FE@R declarations)
- Whether OTs can report honestly without being overruled
- Whether SAG 9/10 validation means anything

**What happens if not decided:**
Default is advisory. OTs cannot fail units. Assessment results are recommendations, not verdicts. The entire validation architecture produces reports that commanders can ignore.

**Source:** [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] Gap 2; [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]]; [[ADWC Command Brief (5-Slide)]] Slide 5

---

### D-06: SAG 9 and SAG 10 Standards Ownership
**Decision owner:** PG HQ (above J3/J7/SBTDWS)
**Status:** PENDING
**Required by:** Q4 26

**What must be decided:**
Confirm who owns, writes, and endorses the validation standards for SAG 9 (Combined Unconventional Warfare) and SAG 10 (Resilience and Capacity Building). Options:
- J3 WDC drafts, PG HQ endorses, SBTDWS executes
- SBTDWS drafts, J7 endorses, PG HQ approves
- SAG owners (study group leads) write the standards and pass to SBTDWS for execution

**What it gates:**
- SAT Cell can only develop competency profiles (ADW v0.1) once the standard is defined
- OT curriculum cannot be finalised without validated standards
- IOC gate (Q2 27) requires standards to exist and be endorsed

**What happens if not decided:**
SAT Cell writes to assumed standards. Standards may not survive command review. All curriculum work from Q3 26 onward is at risk of being redone.

**Source:** [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]]; [[OC-T Cadre Development Plan]]

---

### D-07: J-Staff Support Model
**Decision owner:** PG HQ
**Status:** PENDING
**Required by:** Q4 26 (before structural reorganisation)

**What must be decided:**
Confirm how SBTDWS will be supported by J-Staff functions it does not currently have:
- **Option A:** Organic — SBTDWS grows its own J-Staff (J2, J6, J8 minimum)
- **Option B:** Allocated — PG HQ allocates J-Staff to SBTDWS on exercise cycle
- **Option C:** Liaison — SBTDWS embeds liaison officers into J-Staff cells

**What it gates:**
- Structural reorganisation (Q4 26) cannot be designed without knowing which J-functions SBTDWS will own vs borrow
- EXCON, HICON, and collective validation planning requires J3, J6, J7, and J2 support at minimum

**What happens if not decided:**
Structural design is incomplete. EXCON cannot be fully planned. Validation events will lack the J-function coverage required for realistic collective training.

**Source:** [[SBTDWS 9 Directed Tasks]] Task 7; [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] Dependency 1

**Status:** PENDING
**Required by:** Before structural reorganisation announcement (Q4 26)

**What must be decided:**
Accept or amend the "All Domain Warfare Centre" label. Options:
- **Accept ADWC** and actively manage the expectation gap through a communications plan
- **Recommend alternative** (e.g., "Saeed Bin Tahnoon Desert Warfare Centre") and brief PG HQ on the naming risk

**What it gates:**

**What happens if not decided:**
ADWC label is used by default. Expectation gap grows as partners and units expect capabilities SBTDWS cannot independently deliver. Naming risk becomes a credibility risk.

### D-11: J3 WDC Governance for Tactical Experimentation
**Decision owner:** J3 (PG); O6 CoC (SBTDWS)
**Status:** PENDING
**Required by:** Before TEIW experiments begin (Q2 27)

**What must be decided:**
Formalise the relationship between SBTDWS TEIW and the J3-led Warfare Development Centre:
- How are experimentation priorities set? (J3 direction vs SBTDWS proposal?)

### D-12: SBTDWS Core Focus — Course Portfolio Decision
**Decision owner:** PG Comd (per the source document — the first item in this register naming PG Comd specifically, rather than the generic "PG HQ")
**Status:** PENDING
**Required by:** COA 1 needs manpower/budget uplift approved before Q4 26; COA 3 needs standing review authority in place by MLOC Q4 27

**What must be decided:**
As SBTDWS develops the Multi Domain Training Centre (MDTC), does the current desert warfare course programme remain the main output, or does the MDTC become the main output with the course programme reduced to an agreed core? Three COAs on the table — see [[SBTDWS Core Focus — COA Decision]] for full detail:
- **COA 1 — Retain and Add:** keep full course programme, build MDTC alongside it. Needs full manpower/budget uplift before Q4 26.
- **COA 2 — Progressive Pivot:** MDTC becomes main output over time; course programme reduced to a core, remainder stopped or handed off. May require cover under the founding decree.
- **COA 3 — Hybrid** *(assessed most likely outcome)*: permanent core kept, remainder reduced step by step at each gate (MLOC Q4 27 / IOC Q4 28 / FOC Q4 30). Needs standing review-and-adjust authority at each gate.

**What it gates:**
- Whether the Phase 1 structural reorganisation plan (D-01) is designed around retained scope (COA 1) or a phased handover (COA 2/3)
- The manpower and budget bid SBTDWS submits for Q4 26
- Which PG units continue receiving which courses, and from whom, during the transition

**What happens if not decided:**
SBTDWS cannot finalise its structural reorganisation plan or manpower/budget bid — both currently depend on knowing which COA applies. Risk of drifting into a de facto COA 1 (retain everything) by default, which the brief itself flags as unachievable without a full uplift.

> [!key-insight] This is arguably the most foundational open decision in the register
> Every other Tier decision (D-01 three-wing design, D-07 J-Staff model, D-08 Hameem, D-09 CAPDEV/PGIC) implicitly assumes some answer to "how big is SBTDWS's remit." This decision makes that assumption explicit and forces a resourcing choice, not just a conceptual or naming one. Placed in Tier 2 here because the decision owner is PG Comd/PG HQ, not O6 CoC alone — but its Q4 26 COA 1 deadline is as urgent as anything in Tier 1.

**Source:** [[SBTDWS Core Focus — COA Decision]] (2026-08-05, J3/J7 combined meeting follow-up)

## What Requires O6 Action Now

Three decisions can be made or initiated this month without external authority:

1. **D-01** — Endorse the three-wing design. Conceptual. No resource commitment. No external dependency.
2. **D-02** — Direct the ToR engagement. One tasking to J3 and J7. Overdue.

Everything else requires PG HQ or Higher HQ, and SBTDWS must surface these through the command brief cycle.

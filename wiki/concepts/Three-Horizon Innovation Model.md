# Three-Horizon Innovation Model

A strategic innovation framework used to structure capability development across short, medium, and long-term horizons. Applied to the ATI-Hub and SBTDWS innovation strategy.

---

## The 70/20/10 Model

| Horizon | Focus | Resource allocation | Timeframe |
|---------|-------|---------------------|-----------|
| H1 — Extend core business | Improve and scale what already works | 70% | Now — 2 years |
| H2 — Emerging technology | Build next-generation capabilities | 20% | 2-5 years |
| H3 — New / transformative | Experiment with frontier ideas | 10% | 5+ years |

The 70/20/10 split is a guideline, not a rigid rule. Organisations in rapid transformation may shift toward more H2/H3 investment.

## Applied to SBTDWS / ATI-Hub

| Horizon | SBTDWS application |
|---------|-------------------|
| H1 | Improve existing courses; strengthen instructor standards; scale FPV program; mature existing CAPDEV projects |
| H2 | Develop autonomous resupply systems; build VTOL tactical mobility capability; stand up TEX-Wing experimentation programme |

## Key Questions for Applying the Model

The innovation strategy development work identified four diagnostic questions before building the framework:

1. **What problem are you solving?** CAPDEV, R&D, innovation, and S&T are different things with different architectures.
2. **What frameworks already exist?** Conforming to existing strategy gets more support than building from scratch.
3. **Is there a physical innovation space?** A dedicated facility that inspires a creative mindset is critical for an innovation culture (less so for a pure trials capability).
4. **Are the right people and culture in place?** Innovation requires intrapreneurs and innovation champions — not just a project management workforce.

---

## Distinction: Innovation vs Trials vs CAPDEV

| Function | What it does | Who leads |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Innovation | Creates new approaches; challenges assumptions; takes managed risk | Culture-driven; innovation champion |
| R&D | Systematic research into new technologies; scientific method | Scientists and engineers |
| Trials / T&E | Tests known systems against defined criteria | Technical assessors |
| CAPDEV | Manages fielding of existing capability across DLOD/FIC | Project managers |

The ATI-Hub must decide which of these it primarily is — and resist the tendency to claim all four.

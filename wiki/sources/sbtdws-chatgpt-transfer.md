## Key Synthesis Points

- SBTDWS is already ~70% of the way to an ADWC — CAPDEV portfolio provides multi-domain seeds across land, air, information, and sustainment domains
- The critical gap is the **integration layer**: no sensor-to-shooter linkage, no ISR fusion, no effects synchronisation across the three wings
- P1 laundry list is dominated by documents, decisions, and relationships — not kit. Capital comes later.
- The FPV Pilot proves the model before PGIC is established — critical for command credibility
- "Credibility before ambition" and "Truth before excellence" are the two non-negotiable design principles

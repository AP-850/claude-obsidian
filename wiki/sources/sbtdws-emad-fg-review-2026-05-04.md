## Slide 1 — SBTDWS Intent, Gaps, Linkages, Dependencies, Comments

States the SBTDWS → ADWC 2030 intent in near-final language (matches [[ADWC Transformation Concept]] §2030 Intent). Confirms, at this 2026-05-04 date, the same core findings later formalised elsewhere:

- **Gaps:** PG HQ J-Staff allocation; future PG UW unit concept/raising plan (decision point NLT Q4 26); J7-led OT capability in PG LVC architecture (IOC NLT Q2 27); SBTDWS HICON/EXCON nested in J7 LVC (by Q4 28); J3 WDC experimentation governance with Makhlab 1 as the FDC 5 operating model (first governed serial NLT Q1 27); all-domain digital enablers wholly dependent on the J6 Digital Backbone.
- **Linkages:** J7 (LVC nesting, shared tooling/standards), J3 WDC (experimentation trigger and governance), PGI/IWTC (individual vs collective RTSV split for SAG 9/10), STC/Innovation Command (T&E relationship via FDC 5/Makhlab 1), PG Units (shared exercises, SAG 9/10 SRO).
- **Dependencies:** J7 LVC architecture; J6 Digital Backbone; J3 WDC stand-up and governance; PG HQ J-Staff decision; SAG 9/10 standards endorsement; PGI/IWTC course alignment; Innovation Command stand-up timing; Hameem capital funding.
- **Comments/Issues/Concerns:** Pl(-) IW vs UW capability boundary needs Higher HQ clarification (→ [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-04); "All Domain Warfare Centre" name creates an expectation gap given near-term land/air-only delivery (→ [[ADWC Naming Analysis]], D-10); direction is flexible — don't over-commit; Ramadan/summer windows are a no-new-resource opportunity; SBTDWS holds a single point of failure (SAG 9/10 SRO + UW raising + FDC 5 lead + Hameem LVC delivery all on one unit).

This slide is the earliest dated confirmation in the wiki (2026-05-04) of content that [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] (ingested 2026-06-10) and [[Commander Decisions Register]] (2026-06-02) later formalised — no contradictions found, only earlier provenance.

## Slide 2 — SBTDWS Transformation Timeline

Presents the timeline as **4 LOEs across a year-by-stage band** (2026 Foundations → 2026 Decisions → 2027 Build → 2027 Endorse → 2028 Demonstrate → 2029–2030 FOC), distinct from the **PREP/Phase 1/2/3 by-quarter** structure used in [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]] and [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] (both later, 2026-06-10 framing). Same underlying milestones, different periodisation — the LOE/year view is the earlier (May) framing; the Phase/quarter view supersedes it for working-level planning.

**LOE 1: Foundations** — Course review begins; Instructor Development Programme (continuous); Standards & Assessment programme launch; Course review complete Q3 26; **Contractor HOTO NLT Q4 27** (new milestone — not previously captured in [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]]); Structural Reorg endorsed NLT Q4 26; J-Staff decision (PG HQ).

**LOE 2: Structure** — OT Team IOC Q2 27 (in J7 LVC); HICON/EXCON IOC Q4 28; ADWC FOC Q4 30.

**LOE 3: Validate & Raise** — UW UE concept Q4 26; first below-Bde validation serial Q2 27; OT trained to endorsed SAG 9/10 standards; continuous validation cycle (PGI/IWTC partner, Component Command customer); validation cadence at FOC.

**LOE 4: Experiment & Rehearse** — CAPDEV portfolio review Q3 26; Makhlab 1 launch (1st FDC 5 serial) Q1 27; Hameem dev concept endorsed Q1 27; STC/Innovation Command → J3 WDC governance → J7 LVC architecture; Makhlab 1 showcase + UxS exercise; Hameem Mission Rehearsal IOC Q4 28; Hameem FOC Q4 30.

> [!key-insight] New milestone
> "Contractor HOTO* — NLT Q4 27" (contractor hand-over-take-over) is not captured elsewhere in the wiki. It sits between the Phase 1 (Q2 27 OT IOC) and Phase 2 (2028 military instructor ownership) milestones in [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]] and should be added there as the explicit gating date for the contractor-to-military instructor transition.

## Slides 3–4 — Embedded Force Design Exhibit (External Source)

Slides 3 and 4 are screenshot exhibits, not native EMAD content — both carry "PRIVATE AND CONFIDENTIAL" / "FOXGLOVE" footer branding and page numbers (58 of 68), indicating they are lifted from a separate, larger 68-page PG 2030 Force Design document authored by an external party (see [[Foxglove]]). This is the first appearance of that parent document in the wiki. Full detail captured in [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]]:

- **Slide 3 (Figure 2, page 57):** Revised Unit & Staff Branch focus across 20 PG units/branches, each mapped to a numbered Force Design Concept (1–12 observed, partial set).
- **Slide 4 ("2. Unit Tactical Concepts", page 58):** Sustain vs Develop-by-2030 capability table for six PG manoeuvre/special units (Al Forsan, Falcons Brigade, Hamad Bin Easa, PG Special Guard, Special Operations Command, Specialist Reserve Brigade).

These exhibits place SBTDWS/ADWC inside a PG-wide force design programme for the first time — prior wiki content treated SBTDWS transformation in isolation from the other 19 PG units' parallel 2030 development paths.

# SBTDWS Domain

Saeed Bin Tahnoun Desert Warfare School (SBTDWS) transformation into the Presidential Guard's next-generation multi-domain warfare institution by 2030.

> [!note] Naming
> **SBTDWS is sometimes written "SBTS"** in other documents — same unit, alias only. Do not treat as a separate entity.
> The institution's own end-state name is still unresolved: "All Domain Warfare Centre" (ADWC, formal directive), "Multi Domain Warfare Centre" (MDWC, J7 working title), and **"Multi-Domain Training Center" (MDTC) — confirmed 2026-08-05 as the current working terminology**, pending a PG Comd decision on overall direction. ## What This Domain Covers

SBTDWS is a three-wing Desert Warfare School under the Presidential Guard Command (PGC). It has been directed to evolve into the PG All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC) — delivering training, validation, tactical experimentation, and mission rehearsal as the field-execution arm of PG readiness by 2030.

This domain captures the strategic concept, gap analysis, transformation roadmap, CAPDEV portfolio, key relationships, and all briefing products developed across multiple sessions.

## Key Design Principles

- **Credibility before ambition** — fix foundations before branding
- **Truth before excellence** — honest assessment drives transformation
- **Evolution, not replacement** — preserve the three wings; restructure, don't rebuild
- **Commander's language:** "Experimentation to Adaptation", "Training PG to fight as it would choose to"

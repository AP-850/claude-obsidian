# Gap and Issue Triage Register

Consolidated, ranked triage of every gap, blocker, and issue identified across the SBTDWS transformation analysis (FIC Analysis, Barriers paper, CTC Architecture, 9 Directed Tasks, Commander Decisions Register, WG2 Back Brief). Built to feed the upcoming J3/Commander brief: what we can fix ourselves, what we cannot resolve at our level, and what needs to change in Paper 2 before the OPORD is released to units.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Commander Decisions Register]] | [[SBTDWS Action Plan]]

> [!key-insight] How to read this register
> Items are triaged by **who can act** and **how urgent**, not by which source document they came from. Tier 0 is the Paper 2/OPORD timing risk — act before the OPORD is issued, not after. Tiers 1–4 mirror escalation level. Each item cites every source it's drawn from so nothing here is invented — it's all consolidation.

> [!key-insight] Independent confirmation of a Paper 2 gap, from J7's own endorsed register (2026-08-05)
> [[J7 Readiness Flow and Deliverables Register]] — briefed to J3 and endorsed today — separately flags "J7 as collective TRA not stated in Paper 2" as one of only 8 "NOT IN PLACE" gaps in its Readiness Flow model, and tables **A6 — J7 as Collective Training Requirements Authority** as a staff paper going to PG HQ (target Q4-26) specifically because "Paper 2 names a TRA for individual training only." This is a second, independent source landing on the same Paper 2 gap this register already tracks — not new territory, but real corroboration the same day.

## Tier 0 — Paper 2 / OPORD Window (Act Before OPORD Release)

This is the time-boxed opportunity the Commander has now. Two live discrepancies exist between the **written** Paper 2 §3.15 and the **verbal** J3 direction given at the WG2 back brief (2026-06-10). If the OPORD is cut from Paper 2 as currently worded, it will lock in language the J3 has already informally moved away from.

| # | Issue | Paper 2 (written) | J3 verbal direction (WG2, 2026-06-10) | Recommendation |
|---|-------|-------------------|----------------------------------------|-----------------|
| P2-1 | **Domain scope** | §3.15 Task 1: train/validate FEs to deliver synchronised effect across **all 6 domains** (air, sea, land, cyber, EM, space) | Prioritise **land and partial air** now; scope maritime/space/cyber/EMS "later if required" | Amend Task 1 language to a phased domain commitment (land + air now, remainder conditional) before OPORD — otherwise units are tasked against a 6-domain standard the Commander has already de-scoped verbally |
| P2-2 | **Naming** | §3.15 names the 2030 end-state "All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC)" | J3 referred to it as "Multi Domain Warfare Centre (MDWC)" at WG2 — no official decision made | Do not let the OPORD cement a name in dispute. Either hold ADWC formally pending D-10, or flag the name as "under review" in the OPORD so units aren't briefed a label that may change |
| P2-3 | **Main effort / supporting effort weighting** | §3.15 task ordering implies training/course delivery is foundational alongside validation and experimentation, with no explicit main/supporting split | J3 direction reprioritised: Main Effort = Collective Training/Validation/Mission Rehearsal + Tactical Experimentation (UxS); **Supporting Effort = Courses and Training** (demoted) | OPORD should explicitly carry this main/supporting split so units and resourcing decisions don't default to the old course-centric framing |
| P2-4 | **CAPDEV handoff timing (Task 2)** | §3.15 Task 2: "hand off the existing SBTDWS capability development cell to the new PG Innovation Command" — no date | D-09 (PGIC transition plan) is PENDING, required by Q1 27 | Recommend OPORD attach a trigger date or condition for handoff, not an open-ended instruction — prevents indefinite ambiguity over who owns CAPDEV |
| P2-5 | **J7 lane split (Task 6) has no document yet** | §3.15 implies SBTDWS supports the J7 exercise programme | Force Generation Pipeline ToR (the document that actually defines this) is OVERDUE (D-02, Q2 26 target missed) | Recommend OPORD either reference the ToR as a named deliverable with a hard date, or escalate D-02 now so the ToR exists before OPORD units start planning against Task 6 |
| P2-6 | **SAG 9/10 validation authority unstated (Tasks 7/8)** | §3.15 Tasks 7/8 direct SBTDWS to "exercise PG SRO responsibility for validating" SAG 9 and SAG 10 | No authority defined — D-05 (certify/fail/recommend remediation vs advisory-only) is PENDING | This is the single biggest credibility risk in the whole transformation (see Tier 1 below). Recommend OPORD not assume validation authority exists — flag it as a pre-condition for Tasks 7/8 to be executable |

**Why this is Tier 0, not folded into the decision register below:** these are not new decisions — they are existing Commander Decisions Register items (D-09, D-02, D-05, D-10) with a hard external deadline attached: **the OPORD drafting window**. Once the OPORD is cut, the cost of correcting these rises sharply (units will have planned against it).

## Tier 3 — Institutional / Cultural (Slow-Burn, Needs Command Attention, No Single Decision Fixes It)

These are the barriers documented separately and translated into command-safe language. They are not blocked by a single pending decision — they require sustained command tone-setting and are flagged here because the 2030 timeline assumes they're fixed faster than institutional culture typically changes.

| Barrier | Command-safe framing used in briefs | Risk if unaddressed |
|---------|--------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Inflated pass rates | "Strengthen assessment credibility" | Entire validation system remains theatre even if D-03/D-05 are decided favourably |
| Lack of constructive feedback | "Improve visibility of training outcomes" | OT cadre will repeat the pattern it's meant to replace |
| Weak instructor accountability | "Mature instructor standards" | Poor performers retained; standards erode under pressure |
| O5-level resistance | "Build institutional capacity" | Named as the primary political risk at the structural reorg inflection point |
| Contractor dependency | "Consolidate military ownership of core skills" | Unsustainable as a validation institution; 2028 military-ownership target at risk |
| Resource shortages | "Strengthen training system foundations" | Training continuity disrupted independent of any HQ decision |
| Bureaucratic delays | "Improve training continuity" | Schedule risk compounds on top of the Tier 1/2 decision delays |
| Hiding deficiencies | "Enhance readiness assurance" | Directly contradicts the "truth before excellence" design principle the whole transformation is built on |

> [!contradiction] Timeline tension worth stating plainly in the brief
> IOC is targeted for Q1/Q2 27–29 — roughly 1–3 years out. These are cultural/institutional barriers, which historically take longer to fix than capability or infrastructure gaps. There is a real risk the transformation timeline does not allow enough time for cultural change to precede the capability build it depends on.

## Suggested Brief Structure (Pulling From This Register)

1. **Open with Tier 0** — the OPORD timing window. This is the one part of the brief with a hard expiry: once the OPORD is cut, P2-1 through P2-6 become much more expensive to fix.
2. **Tier 1 as the "decisions we cannot make for you" section** — D-04, D-05, D-06, D-07, plus the single-point-of-failure risk stated explicitly.
3. **Tier 2 as proof of momentum** — show D-01/D-02/D-03 are already in motion or about to be actioned at Commandant level, so the brief isn't purely a list of asks.
4. **Tier 3 mentioned, not dwelt on** — the barriers paper already exists in command-safe language; reference it rather than re-litigate it in this brief.
5. **Tier 4 flagged as "on the radar, not today's ask."**

# Special Operations Command (SOC)

PG command responsible for clandestine/covert Special Operations, reorganising into 4x Special Operations Groups (SOGs) and carrying the largest single SAG portfolio of any PG unit (8 SAGs).

Navigation: [[entities/_index]] | [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Programme MAKHLAB]] | [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]]

> [!note] Distinguish from "PG SOC HQ" / NCTJC
> [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]] Figure 2 uses "PG J3/NCTC/HQ" for the 2030 role of "exercise 24/7/365 C2 of PG All Special Operations Forces from a new, permanently manned, PG SOC HQ" — that is the **command-and-control headquarters**, tracked separately as [[NCTJC]]. This page, Special Operations Command (SOC), is the **force-generating unit** that owns SAGs 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 12, 13, 20 and organises into SOGs 61/66/71/76. Do not conflate the two.

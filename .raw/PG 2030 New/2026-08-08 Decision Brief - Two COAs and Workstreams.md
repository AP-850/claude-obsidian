# Decision Brief — Two COAs Confirmed, Workstreams Opened

## Session context

Follow-up session after the combined **J3/J7 meeting**. J7 briefed their overall requirements; the school then covered its two Lines of Effort:

- **LOE 1** — development of the [[MDTC]] (Multi Domain Training Centre), building on the collective training / validation / mission rehearsal work already done.
- **LOE 2** — tactical experimentation and innovation (touched on briefly; links to [[Innovation Command]] and [[FDC 5]] — see Workstream 2 below).

The meeting surfaced the core unresolved question again: **PG HQ has not decided what SBTDWS becomes.** As the school develops into the MDTC, does the current desert warfare core focus continue, reduce, or end? The decision brief was built and then iterated across this session to answer that.

---

## Decision brief — current state

### Status
Drafted. Originally built with three options (retain-and-add / progressive pivot / hybrid). **Officer decision: collapse to two COAs.** COA 2 and COA 3 were blended into a single phased option, and COA 1 was rewritten so it no longer represents "continue as we are." The intent, confirmed this session, is that **both COAs now require change** — the status quo is not offered as a selectable option to the commander.

### Decision Required (revised framing)

> Authority is sought to transition SBTDWS to the Multi Domain Training Centre. The decision required is the extent of that change: full transition, with the desert warfare role ending by Q4 30, or transition with a defined desert warfare core retained permanently.

This replaces the earlier framing, which asked whether to *retain, pivot, or hybridise*. The revised version bundles two things deliberately: **authority to change the organisation**, and **direction on how much change**. This was a conscious choice — a commander who isn't ready to grant the authority still has the reframe surfaced in the room rather than assumed away, because the authority question is named explicitly rather than smuggled into the options.

### COA 1 — Full Transition

- MDTC becomes the sole output of the organisation.
- The desert warfare school role is run down on a planned schedule and **ceases by FOC Q4 30**.
- All remaining courses are handed off or stopped. No core is retained.
- End state: single-purpose organisation.
- Smallest manpower uplift of the two options — no dual output to sustain.
- Requires: named organisation to assume the desert warfare role (or PG HQ acceptance that it lapses), decree/mandate position confirmed as a change of institutional purpose, run-down schedule endorsed by Q4 26.

### COA 2 — Phased Transition with a Protected Core

*(blend of the former "progressive pivot" and "hybrid" options)*

- MDTC becomes the main output over the plan period.
- A **defined desert warfare core is agreed once and kept permanently** — not subject to later reduction.
- Everything outside that core is reviewable: reduced, stopped, or handed off in steps, matched to what the MDTC can absorb.
- Steps occur at the gates — **MLOC Q4 27 / IOC Q4 28 / FOC Q4 30** — not as a single up-front cut.
- Smaller, phased manpower uplift versus COA 1's dual-track predecessor.
- Requires: endorsed split of protected core vs. reviewable remainder by Q4 26, agreed reduction criteria applied at each gate (not renegotiated each time), named receiving organisation for handed-off courses, standing authority to adjust the reviewable portion at each gate, decree position confirmed.

### Risks (reduced to two for the brief)

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Output gap during transition** — course places and instructor expertise lost before MDTC can replace them | Nothing stops until the receiving organisation confirms capacity, or MDTC proves the equivalent output at the prior gate. Named cadre held against the protected core (COA 2 only). **Partial mitigation** — some throughput dip is unavoidable. |
| 2 | **Committing to a pivot that cannot be resourced or reversed** — phased manpower uplift is agreed but not yet delivered when the pivot begins; course/role handover is not reversible | **No mitigation available at our level.** Accept-and-escalate item. This is the trade both COAs make: reversibility for a smaller, phased manpower bid (COA 2) or for the smallest bid but total loss of the role (COA 1). |

Other risks considered and deliberately **not** carried into the brief (answers held in reserve if asked): decree cover (already RFI-1), institutional identity/heritage concerns (answered by the permanent core in COA 2 / accepted loss in COA 1), gate-by-gate renegotiation (answered by agreeing criteria once at Q4 26).

### Not Part of This Decision (revised)

- Whether SBTDWS transitions to the MDTC — both COAs transition. The decision is how the desert warfare role ends or endures.
- Retaining the current course programme *alongside* MDTC development was assessed and is not achievable within available manpower and timeline.
- The founding decree, institutional name, and heritage question — separate items already raised as [[RFI-1]].
- Either COA changes institutional purpose or scope; decree cover is required regardless of which is chosen.

### What We Need to Proceed (revised)

- Direction on whether a desert warfare core is retained permanently, or the role transfers out by Q4 30.
- Authority to build the implementation plan against a reducing course programme, with adjustment at each gate.
- A named organisation to receive the desert warfare role or any handed-off course — or PG HQ acceptance that it lapses.
- Confirmation that reduced course output during transition is understood and accepted at PG HQ level.

### Open naming question (unresolved, carried forward)

This session used **MDTC (Multi Domain Training Centre)** throughout, per direction. Prior sessions have also used **ADWC** and **MDWC**. This is still not settled and is already flagged in [[RFI-1]] — needs resolving before the brief and implementation plan are issued as final documents, so all references use one consistent name.

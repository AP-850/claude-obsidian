# Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept

The Hameem site (also referred to as Khaimah in the formal directive) is the physical heart of the future ADWC. It is not simply a training site — it is the mission rehearsal core that gives the ADWC its unique operational value.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]] | [[ADWC FIC Analysis]]

> [!key-insight] Strategic infrastructure, not facility upgrade
> The directive mandates a world-class mission simulation facility. This means Hameem must be designed around use cases — discrete pre-deployment rehearsal — not around conventional range infrastructure logic.

---

## What Hameem Must Become

The Hameem concept is built around **discrete, hyper-realistic, pre-deployment mission rehearsal**. Force elements that have trained and are ready to deploy use Hameem to rehearse the specific mission before commitment.

This is different from a training range (where forces learn) or an exercise area (where forces practice collective tasks). Hameem is where forces **rehearse a real mission**.

---

## Design Logic — Use Cases Drive the Concept

The facility must be designed around operational use cases:

- Mission rehearsal for special operations and partnered operations
- Low-signature and clandestine preparation activity
- Integrated task-force rehearsal (including combat support and service support)
- Digital and electromagnetic signature management during rehearsal
- Force packages that include logistics, medical, maintenance — not just tactical assaulters
- Realistic validation immediately prior to deployment or high-readiness tasking

## Key Issues

> [!gap] Dependency on external architecture
> Hameem's full capability depends on J6, J7, PG HQ, and infrastructure owners. SBTDWS cannot build this alone.

The FIC analysis identified **Data and Instrumentation Gap** as a major concern: Hameem and validation activity require data capture, tracking, AAR tools, and evidence-based reporting — none of which exist today.

Naming risk also applies: referring to the site as an "All Domain" facility before cyber, EMS, and space enablers exist creates expectation that SBTDWS cannot yet meet.

---

## Directive Language (Task 6)

> "Enhance the 'Khaimah' site to become a world-class training facility for mission simulation, enabling Presidential Guard units to conduct realistic and high-level training before deployment."

The directive is explicit: world-class, mission simulation, realistic, pre-deployment. These four words define the design standard.

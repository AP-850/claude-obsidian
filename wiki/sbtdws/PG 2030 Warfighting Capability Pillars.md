# PG 2030 Warfighting Capability Pillars

The top-level PG-wide logic chain connecting the 12 Force Design Concepts (FDCs) to the 21 SAG mission sets and the PG 2030 End State. First seen in [[sources/pg-2030-comd-briefing-pack]], page 2 ("PG 2030 Transformation Logic").

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]] | [[PG 2030 Mission Response Categories (MRC)]]

> [!key-insight] This resolves the Force Design Concepts gap
> [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]] previously captured only a partial FDC list (11 of an unknown total, with FDC 5 and a possible FDC 13 unaccounted for). This source gives the full, closed list: **12 Force Design Concepts, numbered 1–12, no FDC 13.** FDC 5 is **"Tactical Experimentation."** See that page's updated FDC table.

## The Logic Chain

```
12 Force Design Concepts
        ↓ enable
PG 2030 Warfighting Capability Pillars ("what PG must be able to do")
        ↓ produce
Mission-Ready All-Domain Force Packages
        ↓ task-organized against
21 SAG Mission Sets ← benchmarked against → MRC Readiness Benchmark (MRC 1–4)
        ↓ validated, delivering
PG 2030 End State
```

Flow labels on the source diagram (right-hand margin, bottom to top): **Task-Organize → Validate → Deliver Operational Effect.**

## PG 2030 End State

> Units capable of executing various specialized tasks with high flexibility and a rapid response level to counter current and emerging future threats in a decisive and multi-domain manner.

## Force Driving Mission Sets

The mission-type categories that the 21 SAGs task-organize against:

- Special Protection Ops
- Special Recce Strike Ops
- Forcible Theater Entry
- Irregular Warfare / Unconventional Warfare
- Special Military Intel Ops
- Special Military Ops
- Campaign Management

## The 6 Warfighting Capability Pillars

Each pillar is framed as a governing question ("what PG must be able to do") and mapped to the Force Design Concepts that enable it.

| Pillar | Governing Question | Enabled by (FDC #) |
|---|---|---|
| **Command & Integrate** | Can PG command, integrate and synchronize Mission-Specific Forces across all domains? | 1, 3, 6, 10 |
| **Understand & Decide** | Can PG see, understand, prioritize and make decisions faster than the adversary? | 3, 7, 9 |
| **Maneuver & Project** | Can PG place the right force, at the right scale, in the right place, under the required conditions? | 2, 6, 10, 11 |
| **Deliver Effects** | Can PG generate the kinetic and non-kinetic effects required to accomplish the mission? | 2, 7, 8, 9 |
| **Sustain & Protect** | Can PG preserve the force, sustain it at strategic reach and maintain operational tempo? | 8, 10, 11, 12 |
| **Generate, Adapt & Assure** | Can PG continuously build, test, validate and improve combat capability? | 4, 5, 10, 11, 12 |

> [!key-insight] FDC 10 and FDC 11 are the most load-bearing concepts
> FDC 10 (J3 Readiness, Integrated, J4 & JTF) enables 5 of 6 pillars; FDC 11 (Force Generation & Training) enables 4 of 6. Both sit outside SBTDWS's direct ownership (J3/J4-owned) — reinforcing why [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-07 (J-Staff Support Model) and the Force Generation Pipeline ToR (D-02) are structurally central, not just administrative blockers.

## FDC Grouping (as shown on the source diagram)

The source groups the 12 FDCs into two bands feeding the pillars:

**PG-Specific Concepts** (1–5):
1. PG Component Concept, Unifying Doctrine & C2
2. Unit Tactical Concepts
3. Digital Spine
4. Industry & Academia R&D Hub
5. Tactical Experimentation

**Joint / Integration Concepts** (6–12):
6. Integration
7. Multi-Domain Targeting
8. UxS & Lethality (MAKHLAB)
9. J2 Special Intelligence
10. J3 Readiness, Integrated, J4 & JTF
11. Force Generation & Training
12. PG Personnel

This grouping is new — [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]] lists the same 12 concepts (by name, matched 1:1 against this source) but without a PG-Specific/Joint-Integration split. See that page for the full unit/branch mapping table.

## 21 SAG Mission Sets and the MRC Readiness Benchmark

The 21 SAGs (see [[Programme MAKHLAB]], [[sources/updated-pg-2030-tasks-aug-2026]] for the per-unit SAG assignments already tracked) are benchmarked for readiness against four Mission Response Categories. Full definitions moved to [[PG 2030 Mission Response Categories (MRC)]] — this is the first wiki source to name and define MRC 1–4 explicitly; MRC 2, 3, and 4 were previously referenced on [[Specialist Reserve Brigade (SRB)]] and [[Programme MAKHLAB]] without definition.

## Relevance to SBTDWS

- SBTDWS's core functions (validate SAG 9/10, run tactical experimentation under FDC 5) map directly to the **Generate, Adapt & Assure** pillar (FDC 4, 5, 10, 11, 12) — the pillar with the most FDCs attached, consistent with SBTDWS's broad "readiness/validation/experimentation institution" framing already established on [[ADWC Transformation Concept]].
- FDC 5 = "Tactical Experimentation" resolves the previously-unknown identity of Force Design Concept 5, which SBTDWS's own [[TEIW Standing Charter]] and [[Makhlab 1 Project]] work already sits under by number without a confirmed title.

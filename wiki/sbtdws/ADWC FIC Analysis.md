# ADWC FIC Analysis

Functions, Institutions, Capabilities analysis for SBTDWS transformation. Produced May 2026. Identifies 10 key development areas, 10 dependencies, and 12 key relationships.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]] | [[SBTDWS Key Relationships]]

> [!key-insight] The core finding
> SBTDWS can build many parts of the future model, but it cannot deliver the full 2030 role alone. The main requirement is to connect the school to the right command authorities, J-Staff enablers, training architecture, and operational units.

---

## 1. Key Areas Requiring Development (Gaps)

### 1. Future Role and Operating Concept
SBTDWS needs a clear future operating concept explaining how it evolves from desert warfare school into a centre for training, validation, mission rehearsal, and tactical experimentation. This should also clarify whether "All Domain Warfare Centre" is the right name, or whether a title retaining the Desert Warfare identity is more credible near-term.

### 2. Command Authority and Validation Role
SBTDWS needs clear direction on what authority it will hold in validation activity — whether it will assess, certify, recommend remediation, or only advise. SAG 9 and SAG 10 validation is only meaningful if standards and reporting authority are endorsed by Higher HQ. See [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]].

### 3. Training Pathway and IW / UW Role
A clear pathway is needed linking current courses, future IW / UW requirements, collective training, Hameem mission rehearsal, and SAG 9 / SAG 10 validation. The IW/UW distinction (Pl(-) IW across units vs UW handoff from SOC) must be clarified by Higher HQ.

### 4. Observer-Trainer, HICON and EXCON Capability
SBTDWS must build an OT capability and HICON / EXCON function nested within J7 LVC architecture. Essential for collective training, mission rehearsal, and below-brigade SAG validation. See [[OC-T Cadre Development Plan]].

### 5. Tactical Experimentation Framework
SBTDWS can support tactical experimentation but requires a governed methodology under the J3 WDC — including how experiments are selected, approved, executed, reported, and transferred into TTPs or unit training.

### 6. Lessons Exploitation and Feedback Loop
A formal mechanism is needed to turn PG mission exploitation, unit feedback, operational trends, and exercise observations into updated course design, refined TTPs, and improved validation standards.

### 7. Hameem Mission Rehearsal Capability
Hameem must develop into a true mission rehearsal capability — not just a training site. See [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept]].

### 8. Digital, Cyber, EMS and All-Domain Enablers
SBTDWS cannot generate all-domain training alone. Cyber, EMS, space, secure C4I, data systems, digital AAR, and Hameem connectivity are dependent on the J6 digital backbone and specialist enablers outside SBTDWS.

### 9. Integrated Support and Sustainment
Future validation should include more than tactical execution. Logistics, medical, communications, maintenance, recovery, and sustainment must be embedded in mission rehearsal and SAG validation from the start.

### 10. Institutional Credibility and Capacity
The school's future role depends on strong standards, instructor development, honest feedback, military ownership of core skills, and sufficient manpower. SBTDWS must avoid overcommitting to outputs it cannot yet resource or control.

## Key Issues for Command Direction

From the officer-level analysis (May 2026):

1. **IW / UW clarification required.** Higher HQ must confirm the IW / UW distinction before SBTDWS can define its validation scope.
2. **Naming risk.** "All Domain Warfare Centre" may create an expectation gap — cyber, EMS, space are not independently deliverable near-term.
3. **Validation authority.** SAG 9 / SAG 10 validation requires endorsed standards and authority to report honestly.
4. **Do not over-commit.** The direction is flexible — SBTDWS should use that flexibility, not fill it with promises it cannot yet keep.
5. **Use low-tempo periods.** Ramadan and summer windows are development opportunities — instructor training, course redesign, simulation planning.
6. **Single point of failure risk.** SAG 9/10 SRO + UW raising mission + FDC 5 tactical experimentation lead + Hameem LVC delivery all currently sit on SBTDWS without sufficient resource. Under-resourcing puts all lines of effort at risk simultaneously.
7. **Define Hameem early.** Hameem must be planned as a mission rehearsal capability, not a training site — EXCON, LVC integration, data capture, scenario design from the start.
8. **Build feedback loops.** Mission lessons and unit feedback must feed directly into training design, TTP refinement, and validation standards.

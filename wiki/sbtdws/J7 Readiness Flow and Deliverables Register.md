# J7 Readiness Flow and Deliverables Register

J7's own brief, **briefed to J3 on 2026-08-05 and endorsed** — the most authoritative PG 2030 artifact ingested to date, produced at today's first-ever J3-chaired SBTDWS/J7 working group. Two parts: (1) "The Readiness Flow" — a one-page model of individual-training-to-mission-rehearsal, mapping responsibility across J1&J6, J3, SBT, J7, and Brigades/PGI at every stage, with 8 explicit "NOT IN PLACE" gaps; (2) the J7 Deliverables Register — roughly 60 numbered instruments (A1–J9) across 10 categories that J7 commits to write, issue, or seek approval for for, each with an approval owner, target date, and what it unlocks.

## The Readiness Flow

Eight-stage model: **Individual → Cadre and Standards → Unit Collective → Formation and CPX → Validation vs SAG → JTF C2 Certified → Declared Ready → Mission Rehearsal**

### Responsibility by owner

**J1 & J6 — Record, positions and network**
- Individual: J1 records, accredits MOS, MQC hosts
- Cadre and Standards: J1 establishes EXCON/OTC positions
- Formation and CPX: J6 networks, LVC feeds, accreditation

**J3 — Standards, ORC, Delivery declaration**
- Unit Collective: Owns the ORC, protects the rotation with J7
- Formation and CPX: Owns the Battlefield Management System
- Validation vs SAG: Issues OPS for each SAG
- JTF C2 Certified: Owns the C2 model
- Declared Ready: SRO validates, J3 declares "Ready" to MOD

**SBT — Delivery Arm**
- Cadre and Standards: Trains the OTC cadre to the J7 standard
- Unit Collective: Supports collective training
- Formation and CPX: Runs EXCON/HICON and OPFOR
- Validation vs SAG: Validates the FE — certify, fail, or remediate
- Mission Rehearsal: Executes at Hameem or appropriate FTA

**J7 — Standards, design, control & assurance**
- Individual: Owns the currency rules; delivers none of it (key client)
- Cadre and Standards: Publishes OTC accreditation standard, exercise design assessment method, AAR framework
- Unit Collective: Sets the collective requirement — proposed collective training TRA
- Formation and CPX: Designs and controls the simulation architecture and the CPX
- Validation vs SAG: Owns the assessment method; endorses SAG 9/10 standards
- JTF C2 Certified: Certifies JTF C2 with J3
- Declared Ready: Assures the ORC
- Mission Rehearsal: Plans, controls, and exploits the MRE

**BDEs and PGI — Requirement and individual delivery**
- Individual: Brigades set the requirement (TRA); PGI delivers the courses (key client)
- Unit Collective: Delivers unit collective training
- JTF C2 Certified: AF, HBE, SOC provide the 1-star HQ
- Mission Rehearsal: FEs and partners participate

> [!key-insight] SBT's role, made explicit and endorsed
> This is the clearest single statement yet of what SBT (SBTDWS) actually does across the readiness pipeline, now carrying J3/J7 endorsement: **trains the OT cadre to J7's standard, supports collective training, runs EXCON/HICON and OPFOR, holds the certify/fail/remediate validation function, and executes mission rehearsal at Hameem.** This matches and now formally confirms what was previously synthesised from multiple partial sources (OT cadre plan, D-05 validation authority, TEIW/EXCON role, Hameem execution) — see [[OC-T Cadre Development Plan]], [[J3 WDC]], [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept]], [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-05/D-06.

# PG 2030 Working Groups (12, FDC-Aligned)

A formal, bilingual "Force Design Concepts Working Groups: Presidential Guard Vision 2030" document establishing one Working Group per Force Design Concept — 12 WGs, each mapped 1:1 to a numbered entry in [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]]. Supersedes the earlier informal 8-WG draft at [[PG 2030 Working Groups]].

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]] | [[PG 2030 Working Groups]]

> [!key-insight] WG5 is SBTDWS's own transformation, now named as PG governance
> **WG5 — Multi-Domain Battle Center** (FDC 5, "Tactical Experimentation") describes the Presidency Guard Training Academy at Saif Bin Tahnoon standing up a Multi-Domain Training Center at "Hamim" (= [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept]], spelling variant) as "the primary headquarters for collective training command, multi-domain training design," with field-trial, readiness-assessment, and warfare-methodology development in support of manufacturing and innovation. This is SBTDWS's ADWC/MDTC transformation, now formally chartered as a PG Working Group under **J7 Training Department** lead — directly corroborating the J7-led WG finding from [[PG 2030 Working Groups]] WG7 ("Development of the PG Warfare Development Center"). Confirms J7 as the WG-formation lead while [[J3 WDC]] retains operational governance once the Centre stands up — the build-vs-run split flagged there is now stronger, not weaker.

## Purpose, Concept, Responsibilities

**Background/Purpose:** given the requirement for interoperability and alignment across the functional domains of the Force Design Concepts, and to ensure PG Vision 2030's desired end-state objectives are realised through a unified approach, Working Groups translate the Vision into actionable programmes and initiatives, aligning with the Vision, ensuring consistent outputs across all units, and fostering collaborative action plans under a unified approach. These groups support and monitor the implementation of each Force Design Concept.

**Concept of Working Groups:** WGs are the execution mechanism for PG Vision 2030 — they translate Force Design Concepts into executable and measurable action plans, and are responsible for monitoring progress, measuring outcomes, and submitting necessary recommendations to ensure the achievement of Vision objectives.

**Responsibilities (all WGs):**
- Analyze the pillars of the Vision
- Prepare the Action (Execution) Plan, including objectives, phases, priorities, timelines, and requirements
- Establish measurable Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Coordinate with other Working Groups to ensure integration
- Prepare periodic progress reports
- Prepare briefings on the status of Concept Development for the Commander of the Presidency Guard (quarterly)

## The 12 Working Groups

### WG1 — Presidential Guard Component, Unified Doctrine, and Command & Control (C2)
**FDC:** 1 — PG Component Concept, Unifying Doctrine & C2
**Lead:** J3 Operations and Plans Branch
**Members:** J7 Training Branch, Presidential Guard Command; S-3/Operations Chiefs (Joint Counter-Terrorism Command, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade, Special Operations Command, Cavalry Brigade, Falcons Brigade); J2 Intelligence
**Related FDCs:** Special Intelligence Concept; Operational Readiness Concept; Integration Concept; Digital Backbone Concept
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG possesses an integrated Command and Control (C2) model, enabling the Commanding General flexible command at the Joint Task Force / Land Component level, supported by secure communications.

### WG2 — Unit Tactical Concepts (*internal to each unit*)
**FDC:** 2 — Unit Tactical Concepts
**Lead:** Commanders of Presidential Guard Units
**Members:** All Force Design Concepts
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command possesses combat units capable of deployment and operational adaptation — supported by modern technologies, professional, flexible, and able to operationally adapt to the demands of modern warfare.

### WG3 — The Digital Spine
**FDC:** 3 — Digital Spine
**Lead:** J6 Communications and Information Technology Department
**Members:** S-6/Communications; J3 Capability Development Department; J3 Operations and Plans Department; J2 Intelligence Department; J1 Personnel — plus all Force Design Concept departments across all PG units
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG possesses a secure, resilient, Ministry of Defense-compatible "Digital Backbone" — data processing, unified planning tools, secure communications, and AI-enabled command and control systems.

### WG4 — Research and Development / Manufacturing and Innovation Center Concept
**FDC:** 4 — Industry & Academia R&D Hub
**Lead:** J8 Capabilities Development Directorate
**Members:** J3 Operations and Plans; Special Operations Command, Knights Brigade, Presidency Guard Institute; Saif Bin Tahnoon School; J4 Supply Directorate; J1 Human Resources Directorate
**Related FDCs:** Unmanned Systems Concept; Live Experiments Concept; Multi-Domain Targeting Concept
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG possesses an integrated multi-domain research and manufacturing/innovation institution, coordinating joint research and innovation activity with academic and industrial sectors, and conducting tests/experiments and limited production/prototyping/modification/repair to support operational requirements.

### WG5 — Multi-Domain Battle Center
**FDC:** 5 — Tactical Experimentation
**Lead:** J7 Training Department
**Members:** J3 Operations and Plans Department; Saif Bin Tahnoon Academy; J4 Supply Department; S7 Training Staff/Training Sections (Presidential Guard, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade, Special Operations Command, Horse Brigade, Falcons Brigade, Presidency Guard Institute)
**Related FDCs:** Force Readiness and Training Concept; Operational Readiness Concept; Multi-Domain Systems Concept; Unmanned Systems Concept; Manufacturing and Innovation Center Concept
**Desired End State:** By 2030, the Presidency Guard Training Academy at Saif Bin Tahnoon will possess a Multi-Domain Training Center at the Hamim location, serving as the primary headquarters for collective training command and multi-domain training design — in addition to supporting field-trial requirements, readiness assessment, and warfare-methodology development for manufacturing and innovation.

> [!key-insight] This is SBTDWS — see callout above.

### WG6 — Concept of Integration: The Future Character of Conflict
**FDC:** 6 — Integration
**Lead:** J3 Operations and Plans Branch
**Members:** J2 Intelligence Branch; S-2/Intelligence Chiefs (Joint Counter-Terrorism Command, Cavalry Brigade, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade); S-3/Operations Chiefs (Joint Counter-Terrorism Command, Cavalry Brigade, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade); Presidential Guard Institute
**Related FDCs:** Special Intelligence Concept; Maneuver Experiments Concept; PG Component/Unified Doctrine/C2 Concept; Unmanned Systems Concept
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command possesses an integrated force characterised by flexibility, speed, secure communications, and multi-domain capabilities — capable of meeting the requirements of modern warfare and analysing lessons learned from contemporary conflicts.

### WG7 — Multi-Domain Targeting Concept
**FDC:** 7 — Multi-Domain Targeting
**Lead:** J2 Intelligence Branch
**Members:** S-2/Intelligence Chiefs (Joint Counter-Terrorism Command, Cavalry Brigade, Special Operations Command, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade); J3 Operations and Plans; S-3/Operations Chiefs (Joint Counter-Terrorism Command, Cavalry Brigade, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade); J6 Communications and Information Technology Branch
**Related FDCs:** Special Intelligence Concept; Digital Backbone Concept; PG Component/Unified Doctrine/C2 Concept; Unmanned Systems and Enhanced Lethality Concept (MAKHLAB)
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command possesses an advanced multi-domain targeting capability underpinned by intelligence, sensor integration, data analytics, and rapid decision-making (RST) tools — capable of achieving desired effects at all levels of operation, surveillance, and targeting.

### WG8 — Unmanned Systems and Enhanced Lethality
**FDC:** 8 — UxS & Lethality (MAKHLAB)
**Lead:** Falcons Brigade
**Members (as extracted — see source-quality note):** J3 Operations and Plans Branch; S-7 (Personnel/Plans)/Capability Development Branches (Joint Inter-Agency Counter-Terrorism Command, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade, Special Operations Command, Knights Brigade, Presidency Guard Institute); J8 Capability Development Department; S-4 Supply Department (Joint Inter-Agency Counter-Terrorism Command, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade, Special Operations Command, Knights Brigade, Falcons Brigade)
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command is capable of executing tasks and duties within approved response categories with efficiency and tested operational readiness, enhanced by modern technologies, unified command, control, and communications.

> [!key-insight] Corroborates Falcons Brigade as MAKHLAB coordination lead
> Matches [[PG 2030 Working Groups]] WG5 (old numbering) exactly on Lead — Falcons Brigade. Confirms Falcons Brigade's cross-programme MAKHLAB coordination role first identified 2026-08-05 is stable across two independently-dated source documents.

### WG9 — Special Intelligence Concept
**FDC:** 9 — J2 Special Intelligence
**Lead:** J2 Intelligence Department
**Members (as extracted — see source-quality note):** Joint Intelligence Departments (Special Guard, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade, Falcons Brigade, Close Protection Group); J2 Intelligence Department; J3 Operations and Plans; Reserve Brigade; Presidential Guard Institute
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command possesses advanced, integrated collection, analysis, and fusion of intelligence from diverse sources, supporting all levels of decision-making.

### WG10 — Operational Readiness and Supply-Combat Force Integration Concept
**FDC:** 10 — J3 Readiness, Integrated, J4 & JTF
**Lead:** J3 Operations and Plans Department
**Members (as extracted — see source-quality note):** J4 Supply Department; S-3/Operations Chiefs and S-4/Supply Chiefs (Joint Inter-Agency Counter-Terrorism Command, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade, Special Operations Command, Knights Brigade, Falcons Brigade)
**Related FDCs:** Special Intelligence Concept; Force Readiness and Training Concept; PG Component/Unified Doctrine/C2 Concept; Special Combat Unit Concept
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command is capable of executing tasks and duties with efficiency and tested operational readiness, supported by an integrated combat-and-supply force model with unified command and control.

### WG11 — Force Readiness and Training Concept
**FDC:** 11 — Force Generation & Training
**Lead:** J7 Training Department
**Members:** J3 Operations and Plans Department; S7 Training Sections (Special Operations Command, Cavalry Brigade, Falcons Brigade, Saif Bin Tahnoon Academy, Protective Detail Group)
**Related FDCs:** Multi-Domain Battle Center Concept; Operational Readiness Concept; PG Component/Unified Doctrine/C2 Concept; Maneuver Unit Concepts
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command has a readiness concept comprising a series of dedicated readiness exercises to verify the operational readiness of units, supported by modern simulators and a multi-domain training center.

> [!gap] WG11 vs WG5 overlap — both reference "multi-domain training center"
> WG11's Desired End State references "a multi-domain training center" in support of readiness exercises, while WG5 is the WG that actually stands that center up. The source doesn't state whether WG11 is a downstream *consumer* of WG5's output (most likely reading — training readiness exercises use the center WG5 builds) or a partially duplicative body. Both share J7 Training Department as Lead, which reduces the risk of this being a genuine seam — same accountable owner either way.

### WG12 — Presidential Guard Personnel Qualification Concept
**FDC:** 12 — PG Personnel
**Lead:** J1 Human Resources Branch
**Members:** S1 Personnel Staff/Human Resources Branches (Special Guard, Hamad Bin Isa Brigade, Falcons Brigade, Protection Group, Presidency Guard Institute)
**Related FDCs:** Operational Readiness Concept; Digital Backbone Concept
**Desired End State:** By 2030, PG Command possesses an advanced Human Resource Management System capable of attracting and selecting competencies based on actual and future needs, supported by modern technologies for workforce data management, career progression, and performance.

## Cross-Cutting Observations

- **Every WG maps 1:1 to a numbered FDC** — this is the first source to close the FDC-to-governance gap flagged in [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]]. No FDC is without a named WG, and no WG is without a named FDC.
- **J3 Operations and Plans leads 3 of 12 WGs** (WG1, WG6, WG10) and appears as a member in most others — reinforcing J3's role as the overall PG 2030 programme owner, consistent with [[PG 2030 Working Groups]]'s finding that all 8 (old-numbering) WGs nested under J3.
- **J7 Training Department leads 2 of 12 WGs** (WG5, WG11) — both training/readiness-adjacent, both plausibly SBTDWS/[[J3 WDC]]-relevant execution partners.
- **Falcons Brigade is the only unit (not staff branch) to lead a WG** (WG8, UxS/MAKHLAB) — consistent with its cross-programme MAKHLAB coordination role.
- **Continuity with the old 8-WG draft:** WG1↔old WG3 (C2), WG3↔old WG6 (Digital Spine), WG5↔old WG7 (Warfare Development Center), WG7↔old WG1 (Multi-Domain Targeting), WG8↔old WG5 (MAKHLAB, Falcons Bde lead), WG10↔old WG2/WG4 (ORC + J4 Support, now merged). Old WG8 (Simulation Program) has no clean 1:1 successor — its content (simulators supporting readiness) now reads as folded into WG5 and/or WG11.

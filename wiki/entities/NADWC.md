# NADWC — National All-Domain Warfighting Center

Michigan National Guard's all-domain training and warfighting development centre. The only centre in the world explicitly branded as an "All-Domain Warfighting Center." Provides the most direct structural reference for the ADWC name and concept.

Navigation: [[Research: Global ADWC Equivalents]] | [[Combat Training Centre Model]] | [[ATI-Hub Concept]]

---

## Overview

NADWC operates across two anchor sites connected by shared command, making it a two-node model:

| Node | Location | What it provides |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Camp Grayling Joint Maneuver Training Center | Northern Michigan | ~200,000 acres maneuver space; ground training area; live fire |
| Alpena Combat Readiness Training Center (CRTC) | Northeast Michigan | 17,000+ sq miles military airspace over Lake Huron; aviation; C2 |

Supporting nodes: Battle Creek Air National Guard Base, Selfridge Air National Guard Base.

---

## Mission

Maximize Department of War readiness by providing unit-tailored, cost-effective, highly integrated Joint Fires and combined-arms live-fire training, with strong emphasis on interoperability among Joint and Coalition partners. Also supports other government agencies and interagency operations.

---

## UAS Designation (February 2026)

NADWC was designated by the US Department of War as a **National Range for Deep Uncrewed Aerial Systems (UAS) Training** — selected by the US Army and National Guard Bureau. The designation recognises NADWC's unmatched environment for drone development, testing, and training.

This is directly relevant to ADWC's TEIW and FPV program: NADWC's UAS range model (large open airspace, ground integration, live fire integration) is the reference for how to structure a drone experimentation and training range.

---

## Two-Node Architecture

The two-node model — maneuver space + C2/aviation space under one brand — is the practical architecture that makes NADWC work. Neither node alone is sufficient:
- Camp Grayling provides the ground and live fire environment
- Alpena CRTC provides the airspace and aviation integration
- Together they enable all-domain combined arms

**Relevance for ADWC:** The SBTDWS main site + Hameem Beach (future) follows exactly this two-node pattern. The desert ground training area + Hameem maritime/aviation node under one ADWC brand.

---

## Exercises

Northern Strike — one of the Department of Defense's largest reserve component readiness exercises — is hosted at NADWC. Northern Strike 24-2 ran August 2024 with participation from multiple reserve and National Guard components.

---

## What ADWC Can Learn

1. **The two-node model.** Don't try to replicate everything at one site. One node for ground manoeuvre; one node for aviation/maritime/C2. Connect them under one command and brand.
2. **UAS range design.** NADWC's UAS designation provides a template: large airspace, ground integration, live fire lanes, combined with manoeuvre space. ADWC's Hameem development should draw from this.
3. **"All-Domain" branding is achievable without owning all domains.** NADWC's name claims all domains; in practice it covers land and air through the two-node model. ADWC faces the same challenge — use the same solution.
4. **Joint and coalition interoperability as a core offering.** NADWC explicitly positions interoperability as a mission element. ADWC's partner force and foreign forces training is the equivalent.

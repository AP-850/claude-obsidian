# USAFWC — US Air Force Warfare Center

Direct reporting unit of Air Combat Command, headquartered at Nellis AFB, Nevada. The Air Force's premier proving ground for all-domain warfighting: test and evaluation, tactics development, and advanced training — combined under one command.

Navigation: [[Research: Global ADWC Equivalents]] | [[Combat Training Centre Model]]

---

## Mission

Conduct warfighter-centric live and virtual operational test and evaluation, tactics development, and advanced training to optimise Air Force capabilities for joint all-domain combat operations.

**Established:** 1 September 1966 as US Air Force Tactical Fighter Weapons Center. Renamed USAFWC October 2005 to reflect expanding all-domain role.

---

## Structure — Subordinate Wings

| Wing | Location | Function |
|------|----------|---------|
| 57th Wing | Nellis AFB, NV | Adversary tactics, advanced training |
| 53rd Wing | Eglin AFB, FL | Operational test and evaluation |
| 99th Air Base Wing | Nellis AFB, NV | Installation support |
| 350th Spectrum Warfare Wing | Eglin AFB, FL | EW tactics, testing, training |
| 505th Command and Control Wing | Hurlburt Field, FL | Operational-level C2 across air, space, cyberspace |
| Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR) | Nevada | Live-fly training area |

The 505th CCW is particularly relevant: dedicated to improving warfighter readiness through integrated training, tactics, and testing for operational-level C2 across air, space, and cyber — the "C2 integration engine" model.

---

## The T3 Model: Test, Tactics, Training — Integrated

USAFWC's defining feature is that test and evaluation, tactics development, and advanced training all happen under the same command. Lessons from test flow directly into tactics; tactics flow directly into training. The loop is closed institutionally, not through coordination.

This is distinct from the NTC/JRTC model, which focuses on collective training and validation of existing capability. USAFWC primarily develops and validates new tactics and systems.

## Facilities

**Joint Simulation Environment (JSE)**
- 52,000 sq ft facility at Nellis AFB, completed 2022, IOC 2023
- Accommodates up to 20 high-fidelity F-35 and F-22 simulators
- Enables multi-domain tactics development beyond open-air test limitations
- Can model threat environments that cannot be safely replicated in live fly

**Cyber Facility (activated 2023)**
- $8M facility at Nellis
- Secure high-speed networks for test data from F-15E, F-16
- Cyber range simulations and operational evaluations

**Nevada Test and Training Range (NTTR)**
- One of the world's largest air and ground training ranges
- Supports Red Flag exercise series

---

## Red Flag

USAFWC hosts Red Flag — the Air Force's premier air combat exercise. Multi-nation, multi-domain, high-end adversary simulation. Validates tactical competence of aircrews in a contested environment before deployment.

---

## What ADWC Can Learn

1. **The T3 model.** Integrating test, tactics, and training under one command closes the learning loop institutionally. ADWC's TEIW + CTVMR relationship should work the same way — TEIW generates new tactics, CTVMR trains and validates them.
2. **505th CCW — the C2 integration engine.** A dedicated C2 integration wing that owns how effects move across domains is the institutional form of "closing the integration gap." ADWC's future All-Domain Integration Cell follows this pattern.
3. **Virtual + live-fly combined.** The JSE enables tactics development in a synthetic environment before going to the range. ADWC's LVC architecture should do the same — simulate at Hameem before executing live.
4. **Spectrum warfare as a distinct function.** The 350th Spectrum Warfare Wing owns EW tactics and training. ADWC's IW/UW thematic should eventually have the same dedicated EW and EM competency.

# Rapid Innovation FPV Pilot

A concept brief demonstrating rapid innovation using existing resources, funding, and authorities — no new organisations required. The FPV Drone pilot is the first proof of concept for the broader ATI-Hub and PGIC model.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ATI-Hub Concept]] | [[SBTDWS CAPDEV Projects]]

---

## Strategic Message

> Rapid Innovation = Resources + Funding + Authorities
> No new organisations required. Existing capabilities can deliver rapid operational innovation.

This is the "quick win" argument. If SBTDWS can demonstrate a rapid innovation cycle before PGIC is established, it builds the credibility needed to make the larger case for ATI-Hub and PGIC.

---

## The Three-Pillar Framework

### Pillar 1 — Resources

**Science and Technology Centre (STC):**
- 3D printing facilities
- Technical support for prototype development
- Access to innovation infrastructure

**SBTDWS:**
- FPV Subject Matter Expertise
- Tactical experimentation support
- Operator training and testing

**Combined outcome:** Rapid prototyping and experimentation capability, already in place.

---

### Pillar 2 — Funding

**Hameem Fund:**
- Procurement of FPV drone components
- Batteries, electronics, and payloads
- Rapid procurement without lengthy approval cycles

**Strategic point:** Small investments generate rapid capability outcomes. This demonstrates the model before any capital is committed to ATI-Hub infrastructure.

---

### Pillar 3 — Authorities

**Granted by:**
- STC Command — resource allocation, innovation support, funding approval
- SBT Command — trial approval, SME allocation, operational testing authority

**Effect:** Removes administrative friction. Enables rapid decision-making without a new governance structure.

| Stakeholder | Role |
|------------|------|
| Science and Technology Centre | Resources and funding provider |
| SBTDWS | FPV SME and experimentation execution |
| STC Command | Innovation authority |
| SBT Command | Operational authority |

## Outputs

**Short-term (within the pilot):**
- Demonstrate rapid innovation process
- Develop FPV drone prototypes
- Establish innovation collaboration model between STC and SBTDWS

**Long-term (beyond the pilot):**
- Template for rapid capability development across PG
- Expansion into other technologies (UGV, autonomous resupply, sensor networks)
- Support for the broader PG Innovation Capability (ATI-Hub and PGIC)

## Relationship to ATI-Hub

The FPV Pilot is the proof-of-concept for the ATI-Hub model:

| FPV Pilot | ATI-Hub Scale |
|-----------|--------------|
| STC + SBTDWS | RDW + TEX-Wing + UxS/FPV Wing |
| Hameem Fund | Dedicated innovation budget |
| STC Comd + SBT Comd | PGIC governance and delegated authorities |
| Ad hoc | Structured 7-phase pipeline |
| One technology | Portfolio of UxS capabilities |

Run the FPV Pilot well, and PGIC approval becomes significantly easier.

---

## FPV Program Vision (Full ATI-Hub Scale)

From the ATI-Hub concept, the FPV program at full scale:
- Ten 10-man FPV teams
- Organic airframe production (0–100 units)
- FPV Schoolhouse under Innovation Command
- Regular FPV "Red vs Blue" exercises embedded in DWS courses
- Competitive leaderboards to accelerate TTP development
- Living FPV TTP publication updated after every exercise and trial
- Reserve Brigade as the standing manpower and production pipeline

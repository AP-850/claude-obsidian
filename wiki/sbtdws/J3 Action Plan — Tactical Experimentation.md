## 2. Defining Phased Objectives

### Phase One – 2026/2027

1. **Establish the J3 WDC experimentation governance framework.** Trigger, approval, reporting, and TTP transfer process defined by **Q3 26**; SBTDWS executes, J3 WDC owns priorities and outputs.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-11 (PENDING)
   > Formal J3 WDC governance for tactical experimentation is not yet decided. Without it, Makhlab 1 and follow-on serials proceed informally and outputs are not institutionalised. See [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-11.

2. **Establish the SBTDWS experimentation cell.** 3–5 personnel in role and operational by **Q4 26**.
3. **Deliver the first governed Makhlab 1 PoC serial.** UxS focus, data captured, AAR conducted, TTP recommendations drafted, by **Q1 27**, in partnership with [[Falcons Brigade]].
4. **Complete the first TTP transfer test.** Makhlab 1 recommendations reviewed, refined, and formally handed to J3 WDC for unit adoption by **Q2 27**.
5. **Finalise the TEIW Standing Charter** (v0.2, FPV as Priority 1) and field the UxS portfolio (land and air) by **Q2 27**.

### Phase Two – 2028

6. **Deliver the thematic UxS Tactical Employment exercise.** Large-scale exercise integrating experimentation and validation, across 2028.
7. **Stand up the formal lessons exploitation mechanism.** Quarterly cycle: exercise observations and operational lessons → course review → TTP refinement → validation criteria update, operational by **2028**.
8. **Establish the Makhlab follow-on serial cadence.** Annual or bi-annual governed experimentation serials, expanding UxS scope, from **FY28**.
9. **Assess maritime UxS scope and feasibility**, 2028.

### Phase Three – 2029/2030

10. **Declare the experimentation programme routine and self-sustaining.** TEIW operating at scale across land, air, and (if assessed viable) maritime; TTP transfer to units embedded as standard cycle, by **2030**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-09 (PENDING)
   > The CAPDEV → PGIC transition plan (what stays with SBTDWS under J3 WDC governance vs transfers to PG Innovation Command) is not yet decided. TEIW's long-term scope cannot be finalised until this boundary is confirmed. See [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-09, [[PGIC Concept]].

## 3. Identifying Requirements for Achieving Objectives

- Formalise the J3 WDC experimentation governance framework (trigger, approval, reporting, TTP transfer) in writing — not assumed from the PG 2030 Directive alone.
- Confirm SBTDWS's mandate as the PG's field execution arm for tactical experimentation, in writing.
- Stand up and resource the experimentation cell (3–5 personnel with serial design, data capture, and TTP writing capacity).
- Field the UxS portfolio: FPV drones (Priority 1 per TEIW charter), VTOL UxS, land UxS, data capture systems, simulation capacity.
- Negotiate and sign the Makhlab 1 Memorandum of Agreement between J3 WDC and SBTDWS.
- Define and confirm the CAPDEV → PG Innovation Command (PGIC) transition boundary.
- Design and operationalise the lessons exploitation mechanism (J7 / J2 input into course design, TTP refinement, validation criteria).

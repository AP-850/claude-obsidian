# SBTDWS Core Focus — Decision Brief (Two COAs, Revised)

The current, authoritative version of the SBTDWS core-focus decision brief (supersedes [[SBTDWS Core Focus — COA Decision]], which is retained for the historical record but marked superseded). Produced in a follow-up working session after the combined J3/J7 meeting. **Officer decision this session: collapse the original three COAs to two**, and rewrite the decision itself so both remaining options require change — the status quo is no longer offered.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Commander Decisions Register]] | [[SBTDWS Core Focus — COA Decision]]

> [!contradiction] Supersedes the three-COA original — COA numbering means something different now
> See [[sources/pg-2030-decision-brief-two-coas-workstreams]] for the full note. In short: the old COA 1 ("Retain and Add") was the closest thing to status quo. The new COA 1 ("Full Transition") is the most disruptive option — desert warfare training ends entirely by FOC Q4 30. Do not read "COA 1" from any pre-2026-08-08 source as describing the same thing as "COA 1" here.

## Session Context

Follow-up after the combined J3/J7 meeting. J7 briefed overall requirements; SBTDWS covered its two Lines of Effort:

- **LOE 1** — development of the MDTC (Multi Domain Training Centre), building on collective training / validation / mission rehearsal work already done.
- **LOE 2** — tactical experimentation and innovation (touched on briefly; links to [[PGIC Concept]] and FDC 5 — see [[PG 2030 Workstreams (2026-08-08)]] Workstream 2).

The meeting surfaced the core unresolved question again: **PG HQ has not decided what SBTDWS becomes.** As the school develops into the MDTC, does the current desert warfare core focus continue, reduce, or end?

---

## Decision Required (revised framing)

> Authority is sought to transition SBTDWS to the Multi Domain Training Centre. The decision required is the extent of that change: full transition, with the desert warfare role ending by Q4 30, or transition with a defined desert warfare core retained permanently.

This deliberately bundles two things: **authority to change the organisation**, and **direction on how much change**. A commander not yet ready to grant the authority still sees the reframe named explicitly in the room, rather than it being assumed away inside softer-sounding options.

---

## COA 1 — Full Transition

- MDTC becomes the sole output of the organisation.
- The desert warfare school role is run down on a planned schedule and **ceases by FOC Q4 30**.
- All remaining courses are handed off or stopped. No core is retained.
- End state: single-purpose organisation.
- Smallest manpower uplift of the two options — no dual output to sustain.
- **Requires:** named organisation to assume the desert warfare role (or PG HQ acceptance that it lapses); decree/mandate position confirmed as a change of institutional purpose; run-down schedule endorsed by Q4 26.

## COA 2 — Phased Transition with a Protected Core

*(blend of the original brief's "progressive pivot" and "hybrid" options)*

- MDTC becomes the main output over the plan period.
- A **defined desert warfare core is agreed once and kept permanently** — not subject to later reduction.
- Everything outside that core is reviewable: reduced, stopped, or handed off in steps, matched to what the MDTC can absorb.
- Steps occur at the gates — **MLOC Q4 27 / IOC Q4 28 / FOC Q4 30** — not as a single up-front cut.
- Smaller, phased manpower uplift versus COA 1's dual-track predecessor (the original COA 1 "Retain and Add").
- **Requires:** endorsed split of protected core vs. reviewable remainder by Q4 26; agreed reduction criteria applied at each gate (not renegotiated each time); named receiving organisation for handed-off courses; standing authority to adjust the reviewable portion at each gate; decree position confirmed.

> [!key-insight] Gate dates unchanged from the original brief
> MLOC Q4 27 / IOC Q4 28 / FOC Q4 30 carry over unchanged from [[SBTDWS Core Focus — COA Decision]] and remain consistent with [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]]. Only the COA structure and decision framing changed, not the gate calendar.

## Risks (reduced to two for the brief)

| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Output gap during transition** — course places and instructor expertise lost before MDTC can replace them | Nothing stops until the receiving organisation confirms capacity, or MDTC proves equivalent output at the prior gate. Named cadre held against the protected core (COA 2 only). **Partial mitigation** — some throughput dip is unavoidable. |
| 2 | **Committing to a pivot that cannot be resourced or reversed** — phased manpower uplift is agreed but not yet delivered when the pivot begins; course/role handover is not reversible | **No mitigation available at our level.** Accept-and-escalate item. This is the trade both COAs make: reversibility for a smaller, phased manpower bid (COA 2), or the smallest bid but total loss of the role (COA 1). |

Other risks considered and deliberately **not** carried into the brief (answers held in reserve if asked): decree cover (already RFI-1, see [[RFI to PG HQ]]), institutional identity/heritage concerns (answered by the permanent core in COA 2 / accepted loss in COA 1), gate-by-gate renegotiation (answered by agreeing criteria once at Q4 26).

> [!key-insight] Clarified by the user, 2026-08-08: desert warfare identity is not what's actually at stake under either COA
> The original three-COA brief stated plainly, under its own "Not Part of This Decision": "Desert warfare remains a permanent core competency of the institution. It is not removed under any option." This revised brief's language below — "how the desert warfare role ends or endures" — reads, on its own, as if COA 1 could end desert warfare as an institutional identity. **It does not.** Per the user directly: regardless of COA 1 or COA 2, SBTDWS will still be the PG's centre of excellence for desert warfare — the same training area, the same accumulated expertise, the same people. What the two COAs actually vary is the **delivery mechanism**: whether that expertise continues as a dedicated course wing and standalone programme (closer to COA 2), or is embedded within the MDTC construct in some other form once the formal course catalogue winds down (COA 1) — not whether the organisation stops being desert warfare specialists. "Role ends or endures" in this brief's own wording refers to the formal school/training-delivery function, not the institution's underlying identity. This nuance did not carry through earlier analysis on this wiki cleanly and needed explicit correction — see [[Workstream 4 — Task 1 Deep Dive (COA Overlays)]] for where it was previously stated too starkly.

## Not Part of This Decision (revised)

- Whether SBTDWS transitions to the MDTC — both COAs transition. The decision is how the desert warfare role ends or endures.
- Retaining the current course programme *alongside* MDTC development — assessed and confirmed **not achievable** within available manpower and timeline (this rules out what the original COA 1 "Retain and Add" proposed).
- The founding decree, institutional name, and heritage question — separate items already raised as RFI-1, see [[RFI to PG HQ]].
- Either COA changes institutional purpose or scope; decree cover is required regardless of which is chosen.

---

## What We Need to Proceed (revised)

- Direction on whether a desert warfare core is retained permanently, or the role transfers out by Q4 30.
- Authority to build the implementation plan against a reducing course programme, with adjustment at each gate.
- A named organisation to receive the desert warfare role or any handed-off course — or PG HQ acceptance that it lapses.
- Confirmation that reduced course output during transition is understood and accepted at PG HQ level.

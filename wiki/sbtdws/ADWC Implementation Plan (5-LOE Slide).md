One-page black-and-white brief for the next J3 / EMAD Committee submission: 5 Lines of Effort down the left, 4 phases across the top, `Q3 26 – Q4 30`. Deliverable file: `SBTDWS_ADWC_Implementation_Plan.pptx`. Full reasoning and working notes: [[sources/sbtdws-to-adwc-implementation-plan]].

## The 9 Indicative Tasks (PG 2030, para 3.15b)

| # | Task |
|---|---|
| T1 | Train and validate PG FEs for All Domain operations (synchronised effect across air, sea, land, cyber, EMS, space) |
| T2 | Hand off the SBTDWS capability development cell to [[PGIC Concept\|Innovation Command]] |
| T3 | Act as PG's lead for tactical experimentation under [[J3 WDC]] |
| T4 | Integrate operational trends and lessons learned into training and course design |
| T5 | Support PG 2030 / AI EMAD proof-of-concept events, starting with [[Makhlab 1 Project\|Makhlab 1]] |
| T6 | Enhance [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept\|Hameem]] into a world-class mission rehearsal facility |
| T7 | Exercise SRO responsibility for validating [[SAG 9 and SAG 10\|SAG 9]] (Combined UW) by 2030 |
| T8 | Exercise SRO responsibility for validating [[SAG 9 and SAG 10\|SAG 10]] (Resilience and Capacity Building) by 2030 |
| T9 | Ensure personnel attend IW training delivered by [[PGI]] for partnered/combined SF operations |

> [!note] T1 appears in two LOEs
> T1 reads "train **and** validate" — two different capabilities requiring two different cadres. Deliberately split across LOE 2 (train) and LOE 3 (validate). Footnoted on the slide, not an error.

## 5 Lines of Effort

This grouping **supersedes the 4 LOEs in the EMAD review deck** (Foundations / Structure / Validate & Raise / Experiment & Rehearse).

| LOE | Tasks covered | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Authority & Agreements | Supports T1–T9 | Owns no single task — every other LOE is gated by decisions and agreements above SBTDWS |
| 2. People & Teams | T1 (train), T9 | Observer Trainers, instructors, scenario designers, HICON/EXCON, specialists |
| 3. Standards & Validation | T1 (validate), T7, T8 | What "good" looks like, and proving units meet it — carries the SAG 9/10 SRO responsibility |
| 4. Experiment & Innovate | T2, T3, T4, T5 | FDC 5 experimentation, CAPDEV handover, Makhlab 1, lessons-to-course-design loop |
| 5. Facilities & Rehearsal | T6 | Hameem estate, ranges, simulation, networks, systems |

**Mapping from the EMAD deck's old LOEs:**

| EMAD deck LOE | Now |
|---|---|
| LOE 1: Foundations | Split across new LOE 1 (authorities) and LOE 2 (course review, reorg) |
| LOE 2: Structure | New LOE 2 — People & Teams |
| LOE 3: Validate & Raise | New LOE 3 — Standards & Validation (UW raising element dropped — see below) |
| LOE 4: Experiment & Rehearse | Split into new LOE 4 (experiment) and LOE 5 (rehearse/facilities) |

> [!gap] Third taxonomy, not yet reconciled
> This is now the **third** framing of the same 9 tasks in the wiki: the EMAD deck's 4 LOEs (explicitly superseded by this document), the [[SBTDWS Action Plan]]'s 3 Objectives + CAPDEV Handoff (still the operative detailed-planning structure, not superseded by this slide), and this document's 5 LOEs (built specifically for the J3/EMAD one-page brief). The three do not map 1:1 — e.g. Objective 1 (T1,6,7,8) here splits across LOE 2, LOE 3 and LOE 5; Objective 2 (T3,5) and the deferred CAPDEV Handoff (T2) both fold into LOE 4 along with T4. Nothing forces reconciliation yet since they serve different audiences (detailed internal plan vs one-page command brief vs the still-pending J3 3-part Action Plan format), but a reader moving between [[SBTDWS Action Plan]], the [[J3 Action Plan — SBTDWS Transformation (Combined)]], and this slide should not assume the LOE/Objective boundaries line up.

## Phase model

| Phase | Window | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Decide & Agree | Q3 26 – Q4 26 | Decisions made; reorganisation endorsed; course review complete |
| Phase 2 — Create Teams & Standards | Q1 27 – Q4 27 | MLOC — Q4 27 |
| Phase 3 — Build Facilities & Systems | Q1 28 – Q4 28 | IOC — Q4 28 |
| Phase 4 — Sustain & Expand | Q1 29 – Q4 30 | FOC — Q4 30 |

**Critical path:** `Decisions Q4 26` → `OT team ready Q2 27` → `First below-Bde serial Q2 27` → `HICON/EXCON + Hameem MR IOC Q4 28` → `FOC Q4 30`. Each gate must clear before the next phase begins; no cadre, infrastructure or systems spend is committed until Phase 1 clears.

> [!note] Phase boundaries differ slightly from [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]]
> This document uses Phase 1 = Q3 26–Q4 26 (folding what the Timeline page calls PREP into Phase 1). [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]] and [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] keep PREP (Now–Q3 26) separate from Phase 1 (Q4 26–Q3 27). Same end dates (FOC Q4 30) and no conflicting milestones found — just a coarser phase cut for the one-page slide.

## LOE × Phase detail

### LOE 1 — Authority & Agreements (supports T1–T9)

| Phase | Objective | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Get the authority to act | Power to pass or fail a unit · Units must attend validation, not volunteer · Decide who approves the standards · Scope and J-Staff decisions |
| P2 | Agree roles with other headquarters | Written agreement with J7 and PGI · Written agreement with J3 WDC and J8 · Component Command agrees to send units |
| P3 | Standards approved and in use | SAG 9, SAG 10 and ADW standards published · Independent checking of results begins · *ADWC name confirmed* |
| P4 | Work with partner nations | *Agreements with partner nations* · Our trainers recognised by allied programmes · Review authority and standards each year |

### LOE 2 — People & Teams (T1, T9)

| Phase | Objective | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Review courses and reorganise | Check every course against 6GW — Q3 26 · New SBTDWS structure approved — Q4 26 · Bid for Observer Trainer and instructor posts |
| P2 | Create the training teams | Observer Trainer team ready — Q2 27 · Military instructors replace contractors — Q4 27 · Scenario design team created |
| P3 | Create the exercise control teams | HICON and EXCON teams ready — Q4 28 · White Cell, AAR analysts, enemy force team · Joint Fires, cyber and EW specialists |
| P4 | Keep the teams trained and staffed | Career path for validators in place · Instructor training continues every year · *Maritime specialists — if in scope* |

### LOE 3 — Standards & Validation (T1, T7, T8)

| Phase | Objective | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Write the standards | Draft the ADW validation framework · Send SAG 9 and SAG 10 standards for approval · List required skills for each force element |
| P2 | Test the method below Brigade | Run first validation below Brigade — Q2 27 · Publish the Collective Training framework · Train Observer Trainers to approved standard |
| P3 | Validate against SAG 9 and SAG 10 | Run a full SAG 9 and SAG 10 validation · Set Joint Fires and counter-drone standards · Publish AAR template and rehearsal framework |
| P4 | Validate units every year | Yearly validation programme — full by Q4 30 · Send lessons to J3 WDC and PGI · Review the standards every year |

### LOE 4 — Experiment & Innovate (T2, T3, T4, T5)

| Phase | Objective | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Review projects and plan handover | Review all CAPDEV projects — Q3 26 · Agree handover plan to Innovation Command · Design the Makhlab 1 trial framework |
| P2 | Begin experiments under J3 WDC | Run first Makhlab 1 experiment — Q1 27 · J3 WDC rules for experiments in use · CAPDEV cell handed to Innovation Command |
| P3 | Demonstrate results and pass on lessons | Makhlab 1 demonstration and drone exercise · Support PG 2030 and AI EMAD trial events · Proven way to pass new TTPs to PG units |
| P4 | Keep improving from experience | Run one experiment every year · Add operational lessons to course design · *Innovation Command fully working* |

### LOE 5 — Facilities & Rehearsal (T6)

| Phase | Objective | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Plan and fund Hameem | Hameem plan — what, when and how much · J7 approves the design · Hameem plan approved — Q1 27 |
| P2 | Build the simulation and network | Simulation system linked to J7 network · EXCON building and secure network at Hameem · Start building ranges and command post |
| P3 | Open the mission rehearsal facility | Hameem rehearsal facility ready — Q4 28 · Urban village, patrol base, cyber and EW ranges · Tracking equipment and AAR analysis tools |
| P4 | Finish building the site | Hameem fully complete — Q4 30 · AAR rooms, accommodation, remaining buildings · *Maritime training site — if in scope* |

## Dependencies — not SBTDWS-owned

The core resourcing argument. Most of the critical path sits outside SBTDWS control; the full working note frames this as a ribbon across the bottom of the slide (condensed to 5 items on the slide itself; full table below).

| Dependency | Owner | What fails without it |
|---|---|---|
| [[J7 LVC Architecture]] | J7 | OT, HICON, EXCON and Hameem mission-rehearsal builds cannot nest coherently |
| [[J6 Digital Backbone]] | J6 | Cyber/EMS/space training overlays, C4I integration, LVC connectivity at Hameem cannot be self-generated by SBTDWS |
| [[J3 WDC]] governance | J3 | FDC 5 experimentation has no governance framework, trigger mechanism or feedback loop |
| PG HQ J-Staff decision | PG HQ | Structural dependency gating every other LOE |
| SAG 9/10 standards endorsement | Higher HQ / Readiness Governance | Validation is meaningless without command-endorsed standards and reporting authority |
| [[PGI]] (IWTC) course alignment | PGI | Collective validation has no individual-training foundation |
| [[PGIC Concept\|Innovation Command]] stand-up timing | PG HQ | Long-term SBTDWS–STC governance relationship unplannable |
| [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept\|Hameem]] capital funding & approvals | PG capital planning (J7 as LVC architectural authority) | Infrastructure investment exceeds SBTDWS budget authority |

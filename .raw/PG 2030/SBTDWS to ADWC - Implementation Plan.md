## 2. Intent (PG 2030, para 3.15a)

[[SBTDWS]] will extend its scope from training PG force elements to operate in the desert environment, to enabling them to prosecute All Domain Warfare. Focus is on training and validating PG units' ability to conduct effective operations synchronising effects across all six domains — air, sea, land, cyber, electromagnetic and space — so they are capable of waging Sixth-generation Warfare ([[6GW]]).

To reflect the expanded remit, SBTDWS will be referred to as the PG **All Domain Warfare Centre** ([[ADWC]]) by 2030.

## 4. Line of Effort grouping

The 9 tasks were regrouped into **5 LOEs**. This supersedes the 4 LOEs in the EMAD review deck (Foundations / Structure / Validate & Raise / Experiment & Rehearse).

| LOE | Tasks covered | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Authority & Agreements** | Supports T1–T9 | Owns no single task. Every other LOE is gated by decisions and agreements that sit above SBTDWS. Made explicit because *"get the authorities"* is absent from the task list. |
| **2. People & Teams** | T1 *(train)*, T9 | The workforce: Observer Trainers, instructors, scenario designers, HICON/EXCON, specialists. |
| **3. Standards & Validation** | T1 *(validate)*, T7, T8 | What "good" looks like and proving units meet it. Carries the SAG 9/10 SRO responsibility. |
| **4. Experiment & Innovate** | T2, T3, T4, T5 | FDC 5 experimentation, CAPDEV handover, Makhlab 1, and the lessons-to-course-design loop. |
| **5. Facilities & Rehearsal** | T6 | Hameem estate, ranges, simulation, networks and systems. |

> [!note] Why T1 appears twice
> T1 reads *"train **and** validate."* These are two different capabilities requiring two different cadres, so T1 is deliberately split across LOE 2 (train) and LOE 3 (validate). This is a mapping decision, not an error — it is footnoted on the slide.

## 6. Decisions taken in this session

> [!important] Read this before reusing the slide
> These are working decisions made while building the brief. They are **not** all endorsed.

1. **UW unit raising removed.** SBTDWS is no longer raising a UW unit. The `UW UE concept – Q4 26` milestone and the `UW unit decision point NLT Q4 26` have been deleted. LOE 3 was consequently renamed from *"Validate & Raise"* to *"Standards & Validation."*
2. **Single vocabulary adopted.** The EMAD deck's time bands (2026 Foundations / 2026 Decisions / 2027 Build / 2027 Endorse / 2028 Demonstrate / 2029–30 FOC) were dropped in favour of the transformation brief's 4 phases. One naming scheme across the brief.
3. **5 LOEs, not 4.** Authorities were separated out as their own LOE, and Experiment was split from Rehearse.
4. **Owner / resource column removed** from the slide at J3's preference; replaced with task-mapping chips.
5. **Language simplified for Arabic translation.** English idiom was replaced with literal, plain phrasing throughout. All dates, acronyms, gates and milestones unchanged. See [[#9. Translation notes]].
6. **Format:** black and white only, plain bordered boxes with dot points, no colour coding. Italic + underline is the single convention for undecided or conditional items.

## 9. Translation notes

The brief is translated into Arabic. English idiom was the main failure point — literal translation produces nonsense, and interpretive translation loses precision.

| Original | Replaced with |
|---|---|
| Build the spine | Build the simulation and network |
| Stand up the cadres | Create the training teams |
| Lock the relationships | Agree roles with other headquarters |
| Triage & hand off | Review projects and plan handover |
| Define what 'good' looks like | Write the standards |
| Standing annual rhythm | Validate units every year |
| Complete the footprint | Finish building the site |
| Operationalise | Build facilities & systems |
| Governance in force | Standards approved and in use |
| Extend reach | Work with partner nations |

> [!danger] Highest-risk phrase
> **"Authority to fail"** is an English military idiom meaning *the power to declare a unit not competent*. Translated literally it reads as permission to be unsuccessful — the opposite meaning. Now rendered as **"Power to pass or fail a unit."** Check this line specifically with the translator; it is the most important authority being requested.

Decision items were also reworded as direct questions ("Is maritime in scope?") rather than compressed English noun phrases ("Maritime in / out of scope"), which translate poorly.

**Outstanding:** a bilingual glossary for retained acronyms — SAG, MLOC, IOC, FOC, LVC, HICON, EXCON, CAPDEV, AAR, TTP, ADW — agreed once so the translator does not produce multiple Arabic renderings of the same term.

## 10. Open questions and actions

- [ ] **Confirm the MLOC date.** Assumed Q4 27. The EMAD deck implies the first below-Bde serial (Q2 27) is the milestone, but the transformation brief's Phase 2 gate also requires trained OTs, Makhlab 1 launched *and* contractor HOTO (Q4 27). One-cell change if J3 has been briefed Q2 27.
- [ ] **Clarify residual raising responsibility.** With the UW unit removed, does SBTDWS retain *any* raising role, or is that now purely PGI / Component Command? This changes what SAG 9 SRO actually covers.
- [ ] **Resolve Pl(-) IW vs UW capability.** Each unit is directed to grow a Pl(-) IW capability. Working assumption is that this is separate from the UW capability handed off from SOC — needs explicit clarification from Higher HQ. Direct implications for RTSV responsibilities and for what PGI's IWTC delivers individually.
- [ ] **ADWC naming.** The name creates an expectation gap — SBTDWS has land and air access only in the near term; cyber, EMS and space remain dependent on the J6 Digital Backbone. Recommend a name that does not promise what cannot yet be delivered. Currently marked as an open decision on the slide.
- [ ] **Produce the resourcing backing slide.** The "single point of failure" argument was removed from the main slide with the owner/resource column. SAG 9 & 10 SRO, FDC 5 tactical experimentation lead, and Hameem LVC site delivery all sit on one unit — under-resourcing puts all of them at risk simultaneously.
- [ ] **Agree the bilingual acronym glossary** before translation.

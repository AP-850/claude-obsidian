# RFI to PG HQ — SBTDWS / ADWC-MDWC Transformation

Consolidated Request for Information for PG Headquarters, built for the new PG 2030 project lead/advisor. Drawn from everything on record to date: [[Commander Decisions Register]], [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)]], [[MDWC Six-Domain Study]], [[ADWC FIC Analysis]], [[Force Generation Pipeline ToR]], and [[SBTDWS Barriers and Blockers]]. Purpose: surface exactly what SBTDWS needs PG HQ to answer before planning and implementation can proceed with confidence — not a re-statement of everything open, but the subset that is (a) genuinely a PG HQ / Higher HQ question, not an internal SBTDWS action, and (b) answerable.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Commander Decisions Register]] | [[MDWC Six-Domain Study]]

> [!note] How this document was built
> Every wiki page tracking a pending decision, dependency, or gap was reviewed. Items already owned by SBTDWS's own O6 CoC (D-01, D-02, D-03 in [[Commander Decisions Register]] — endorsing the three-wing design, directing the ToR engagement, committing to back the first adverse assessment) are **excluded** from the RFI proper and listed in the appendix instead, since they are not questions for PG HQ. Everything below requires PG HQ or Higher HQ action, direction, or confirmation.

## A. Institutional Identity & Scope

> [!key-insight] Confirmed circulating and actively referenced (2026-08-05)
> [[SBTDWS Core Focus — COA Decision]], a J3/J7 combined meeting follow-up brief, explicitly defers its own naming/heritage/decree question by pointing to "RFI-1" by that exact label — matching this item's number precisely. First direct evidence the RFI has reached J3/J7 and is being used as a live reference point in decision-making, not just held by the advisor.

### RFI-1 — Institutional name and source-of-truth document

**Question:** Is "All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC)" still the confirmed 2030 end-state name, or has it been superseded by "Multi Domain Warfare Centre (MDWC)" as used in the J7-authored Six-Domain Study (dated 2026-07-28)? If MDWC is now the working title, is there a single PG HQ-endorsed source-of-truth document we should be planning against going forward?

**Why we're asking:** Two products dated the same day — SBTDWS's own [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)|Implementation Plan]] and J7's [[MDWC Six-Domain Study]] — use two different institutional names, with the J7 document stating outright that ADWC is superseded. Neither is a formal decision. This is a live, observed example of two authorities planning against different names in parallel.

**What's blocked without an answer:** External communications, the structural reorganisation announcement, the Arabic translation glossary work already underway, and every future briefing product risk using the wrong name.

**Priority:** P1 — cheap to answer, high cost of continued ambiguity.

**Source:** [[ADWC Naming Analysis]], [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-10, [[MDWC Six-Domain Study]]

### RFI-2 — Domain scope: maritime, cyber, space, EMS

**Question (three parts):**
1. Does SBTDWS have a maritime UxS Test & Evaluation role under Force Design Concept Five (FDC5), or is maritime out of scope?
2. Which organisation holds training authority for the cyber and space domains, and what coordination mechanism (not ownership) should SBTDWS establish with them?
3. Can SBTDWS begin EMS capability planning for Hameem now — portable threat emulation systems, spectrum management plan, EW/EMS SME cadre — on the basis that EMS is a near-term MDWC/PG HQ shared responsibility, not a claim to full EMS warfare capability?

**Why we're asking:** Land and air are clear and already being resourced. The other four domains have never received explicit HQ confirmation, and two independent analyses — the 5-LOE slide's decisions box and the Six-Domain Study — flagged the identical three gaps from different angles the same week.

**What's blocked without an answer:** Domain-scope planning generally; specifically, UxS T&E credibility is compromised without realistic EMS threat representation, which directly undermines every MAKHLAB/Makhlab 1 validation outcome.

**Priority:** P2 — doesn't block current Phase 1 land/air work, but EMS in particular "cannot be deferred" per the Six-Domain Study's own framing.

**Source:** [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)]], [[MDWC Six-Domain Study]]

## B. Authority & Standards

### RFI-3 — Validation authority

**Question:** Can SBTDWS certify (issue a formal readiness certificate), fail (record an adverse assessment with command consequences), and recommend remediation for units assessed under SAG 9/10 and DMT validation events — or is the function advisory only?

**Why we're asking:** Everything downstream of standing up an Observer Trainer cadre assumes real consequence. Without an answer, the default is advisory, and OTs will self-censor rather than fail units they know command cannot back.

**What's blocked without an answer:** OT cadre authority and credibility, the entire adverse-assessment reporting chain drafted in the [[Force Generation Pipeline ToR]] §6, and the institutional-culture fix (honest assessment) that has to precede higher validation functions.

**Priority:** P1 — required before the first validation serial (targeted Q2 27); the OT pipeline has zero schedule slack.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-05, [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] Key Issue 3, [[Force Generation Pipeline ToR]] §9

### RFI-4 — SAG 9 / SAG 10 standards ownership

**Question:** Who drafts, endorses, and owns the validation standards for SAG 9 (Combined Unconventional Warfare) and SAG 10 (Resilience and Capacity Building)? Three options on the table: SBTDWS drafts and J7 endorses; J3 WDC drafts and PG HQ endorses; or the SAG-lead study group owners write the standard and hand it to SBTDWS for execution.

**Why we're asking:** The SAT Cell cannot finalise ADW competency profiles against an undefined standard, and this is already a named critical-path item.

**What's blocked without an answer:** OT curriculum design; the Phase 1 IOC gate, currently targeted Q2 27.

**Priority:** P1.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-06

### RFI-5 — IW / UW capability boundary

**Question:** What is the formal boundary between (a) the Pl(-) Irregular Warfare capability being grown across PG units through individual courses delivered by PGI/IWTC, and (b) the Unconventional Warfare capability being handed off from SOC as a force-generation product to specialist sub-units?

**Why we're asking:** This is the single most cited blocking dependency in the wiki — it appears in the Commander Decisions Register, the FIC Analysis, and the 5-LOE slide's PGI dispute question all independently. SBTDWS cannot resolve it alone; it requires Higher HQ, above PG HQ level.

**What's blocked without an answer:** SAG 9 validation scope and standards, OT curriculum design for SAG 9 tasks, PGI/IWTC scope and throughput requirements, and the SOC handover plan.

**Priority:** P1 — required by Q3 26 per the current plan, before OT curriculum design can complete.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-04, [[Unconventional Warfare and Resilience]]

## C. Structure & Resourcing

### RFI-6 — J-Staff support model

**Question:** Will SBTDWS receive organic J-Staff (its own J2/J6/J8 minimum), PG HQ-allocated J-Staff on an exercise cycle, or embedded liaison officers into PG HQ J-Staff cells?

**Why we're asking:** The structural reorganisation plan (targeted Q4 26) cannot be finalised without knowing which J-functions SBTDWS will own outright versus borrow.

**What's blocked without an answer:** Structural design; EXCON and HICON planning, both of which require J2/J6/J7 support at minimum.

**Priority:** P1.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-07

### RFI-7 — Hameem concept of use and capital commitment

**Question:** Does PG HQ endorse developing Hameem as a mission rehearsal capability (EXCON, LVC integration, instrumentation, security, digital connectivity) rather than a conventional training-site upgrade, and can FY27–30 capital planning begin on that basis?

**Why we're asking:** Capital planning has long lead times; PG HQ endorsement of the concept of use is the precondition for starting it, not a parallel-track item.

**What's blocked without an answer:** Phase 2 (MLOC), formation-level mission rehearsal capability, LVC integration with J7.

**Priority:** P2 — required by Q2 27 (capital planning must begin before the FY27 budget cycle), not immediate, but the runway is not long.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-08, [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept]]

### RFI-8 — Resourcing commitment given concurrent single-point-of-failure risk

**Question:** SAG 9/10 SRO responsibility, UW-adjacent validation, FDC 5 tactical experimentation lead, and Hameem LVC delivery currently all sit on SBTDWS at once. Will PG HQ resource the school to carry all four functions concurrently at the scale implied (an estimated 30–45 additional personnel at FOC), or should PG HQ direct a phased sequencing that protects whichever functions it considers highest priority?

**Why we're asking:** This is the resourcing question SBTDWS cannot answer for itself — it needs an explicit ambition-versus-resourcing steer before the WG8 consolidated resource ask is finalised and submitted.

**What's blocked without an answer:** The credibility of the resource submission; risk that all four functions are simultaneously under-resourced if the ask is not backed.

**Priority:** P2.

**Source:** [[ADWC FIC Analysis]] Key Issue 6, [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] §7.4

## D. Governance & Relationships

### RFI-9 — Force Generation Pipeline ToR: engagement and J7 delivery capacity

**Question:** Can J7 and J3 confirm willingness to engage on the draft Force Generation Pipeline ToR (target signature was Q2 26; currently overdue)? Separately, can J7 confirm capacity to deliver the resource provisions listed in the draft ToR §5 — OPFOR elements, instrumentation, LVC simulation access, a designated liaison officer — before the remaining TBD process timelines (advance-request windows, FGPC meeting frequency, lessons-report turnaround) are finalised?

**Why we're asking:** SBTDWS has no formal standing in the PG force generation system without this ToR. The draft is complete and waiting on direction to table it; the Commandant-side decision to direct the engagement (D-02) is SBTDWS's own action, but J7/J3's willingness and capacity to actually deliver against it is a genuine open question, not assumed.

**What's blocked without an answer:** Everything in the ToR's scope — collective training integration into the J7 exercise programme, EXCON/HICON nesting inside J7 LVC architecture, formal recognition as a validation node.

**Priority:** P1 — already overdue.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-02, [[Force Generation Pipeline ToR]]

### RFI-10 — J3 WDC experimentation governance

**Question:** Can J3 formalise the SBTDWS TEIW / J3 WDC relationship — experimentation trigger, approval process, findings and TTP ownership, and the governing MoA/MoU — before the first Makhlab 1 serial, currently targeted Q1 27?

**Why we're asking:** Without formal governance, TEIW experiments run informally: outputs get demonstrated but not institutionalised, and lessons aren't transferred to units in a governed way.

**What's blocked without an answer:** The Makhlab 1 PoC serial and everything that depends on it being a governed, not informal, first sprint.

**Priority:** P1 — Q1 27 is close.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-11, [[J3 WDC]], [[TEIW Standing Charter]]

### RFI-11 — PGI dispute resolution mechanism

**Question:** In the absence of a formal PGI–SBTDWS agreement, what is the mechanism for resolving disagreements between the two institutions — for example over course alignment, attendance tracking, or the individual-to-collective handover?

**Why we're asking:** The draft Force Generation Pipeline ToR's dispute-resolution clause (§7.2) covers only J7/SBTDWS/J3. PGI is not a party to it, and no equivalent mechanism has ever been proposed. This item first surfaced as an unanswered line on the 5-LOE slide's decisions-needed box, with no existing owner.

**What's blocked without an answer:** Nothing actively yet — no dispute has occurred — but this is a documented process gap PG HQ should be aware exists before one does.

**Priority:** P3.

**Source:** [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)]], [[PGI]]

### RFI-12 — CAPDEV → PGIC transition plan

**Question:** What is the scope and timeline for transferring the CAPDEV cell to PG Innovation Command — specifically, what capabilities/personnel/projects transfer, what tactical experimentation capability stays with SBTDWS under J3 WDC governance, when the transfer occurs, and whether a transition or dual-hat period is planned?

**Why we're asking:** Without this, TEIW cannot be scoped correctly and SBTDWS staff don't know whether to develop CAPDEV skills or hand them off.

**What's blocked without an answer:** TEIW scoping; risk of duplication and confusion between ATI-Hub (innovation) and TEIW (tactical experimentation).

**Priority:** P2.

**Source:** [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-09, [[PGIC Concept]]

## E. For the advisor's awareness (not a formal RFI item)

**Transformation timeline versus institutional floor.** [[SBTDWS Barriers and Blockers]] documents real, currently-briefed-in-command-safe-language institutional issues (assessment credibility, instructor accountability, contractor dependency) that are cultural and take longer to fix than capability or infrastructure gaps. The current IOC target (Q2 27) assumes these can be fixed on the same clock as the capability build. Worth a soft, separately-framed check with PG HQ on whether the IOC date is firm or has flexibility if foundational work needs longer — using the command-safe framing already established (e.g. "strengthen assessment credibility," not "our pass rates are inflated"), not folded into this RFI as a formal numbered question.

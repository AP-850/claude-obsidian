# Source: Commercial Innovation Management Platform Landscape

Navigation: [[sources/_index]] | [[Research: Defense Innovation Governance Tools]]

Overview of the commercial innovation management software market as of 2024-2026, with analysis of why existing platforms fail small organizations.

---

## Leading Platforms

| Platform | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Brightidea | 4.4/5 stars; strong governance; enterprise program management | "Steep learning curve"; complex for smaller teams |
| HYPE Innovation | 4.6/5 stars; end-to-end from idea to portfolio; 20+ years methodology depth | Enterprise-scale; not suited to smaller organizations |
| Planview (Spigit) | Strong enterprise integration; idea evaluation and project tracking | "Can be complex to fully utilise" |
| IdeaScale | Lower tiers; good for idea capture | "Limited customization in lower tiers" |

---

## Common Features Across All Leading Platforms

- Idea submission with voting and ranking
- Collaboration and feedback tools
- Workflow automation (structured pathways from ideation to implementation)
- Analytics dashboards
- Integration with enterprise systems (CRM, ERP)
- Role-based access

---

## Why Platforms Fail to Be Adopted

Five consistent failure patterns:
1. Steep learning curve — onboarding friction kills participation
2. Demand process restructuring — require organisations to abandon established workflows
3. Inadequate support during implementation
4. Limited customisation — one-size-fits-all imposed on diverse organisations
5. Poor UX — complex interfaces deter participation, especially non-technical teams

---

## Market Gap

No commercial platform targets small/medium defense organizations running tactical experimentation. The existing market assumes:
- Large enterprise (1,000+ users)
- Civilian or commercial context
- Idea management focus (not experiment execution and governance)
- Stable network connectivity
- No classification requirements

TACTIX, positioned as a small-unit CAPDEV governance tool (5-50 users, offline-capable, classification-aware, experiment-lifecycle focus), has no direct commercial competitor.

---

## Relevance to TACTIX

The market gap is confirmed. TACTIX does not need to compete with Brightidea or HYPE — it serves a segment those platforms cannot reach. The design failure modes identified (learning curve, process restructuring, poor UX) are exactly the risks in the current 16-section TACTIX prototype.

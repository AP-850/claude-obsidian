A PG-wide (not SBTDWS-specific) planning standard authored by [[Frank]], lead advisor to J3 on PG 2030, shared with the user for review. Establishes the common method every PG2030 Working Group, Unit, and Staff Directorate must use to convert approved tasks into an Integrated Development Plan (IDP) — a phased, measurable plan running current state → IOC → FOC by 2030.

A methodology/template standard ("Product 3A" — the numbering implies a wider Product series, not yet otherwise documented in this wiki). Not itself a plan — it's the required *shape* every PG2030 plan must take, whatever its owner or subject.

## Action Plan — Specialized Technical Skills and Unmanned Systems Transition

Falcons Brigade's J3-submitted Action Plan is the worked example used in [[J3 Action Plan Template]] to teach other PG units the required 3-part format. Summary:

- **Phase One (2027):** Individuals' qualification on Specialized Technical Skills — approve the training course programme, conduct the first course.
- **Phase Two (2028):** Reorganisation of units and transition to systems upon IOC — define roles/responsibilities per unit, identify required unmanned capabilities.
- **Phase Three (FOC):** Transition to unmanned systems.
- **Requirements:** updating doctrines, developing current training courses, qualifying and training instructors.

See [[J3 Action Plan Template]] for the full worked example and template structure.

## Relationship to SBTDWS — Makhlab 1 PoC and SAG 9/10

Falcons Brigade is the natural partner unit for SBTDWS's first tactical experimentation sprint and a validation customer:

# NCTJC — National Counter Terrorism Joint Command HQ

The permanently manned, 24/7/365 PG headquarters providing C2 for both independent PG-led operations and Joint Force operations, and leading PG's Multi-Domain Targeting process alongside J2 and J3.

Navigation: [[entities/_index]] | [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Special Operations Command (SOC)]]

> [!key-insight] Likely the settled name for "PG J3/NCTC/HQ" in Force Design Concepts
> [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]] Figure 2 lists a row "PG J3/NCTC/HQ" with 2030 role "Exercise 24/7/365 C2 of PG All Special Operations Forces from a new, permanently manned, PG SOC HQ. Lead PG MD Targeting." [[sources/updated-pg-2030-tasks-aug-2026]] gives a full, detailed NCTJC task list that matches this role point-for-point (24/7/365 C2, SCIF, Multi-Domain Targeting with J2/J3). Treated as the same entity — "NCTJC" appears to be the settled name for what the earlier Force Design source called "NCTC"/"PG SOC HQ." Do not conflate with [[Special Operations Command (SOC)]], the force-generating unit NCTJC has C2 authority over.

---

## Tasks (Aug 2026 register)

1. Provide the backbone of the PG C2 framework for Operations, both independent and those subordinate to the JOC
   - A. Develop organizational structures and manning requirements to sustain enduring 24/7/365 operating cycles — including an LNO network to support integration with Joint Forces
   - B. Improve connectivity and interoperability with MOD JOC, Joint Force partners and domestic intelligence agencies (SIA, SSD, NISA)
   - C. Maintain a Sensitive, Compartmented, Information Facility (SCIF) to ensure access to TOP SECRET intelligence feeds — includes CT but wider application against hostile state actors; must fuse information from lower classification into the Common Operating Picture
   - D. Develop HQ Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to enable effective C2 for both independent PG-led operations and Joint Force operations
   - E. Identify and implement systems/technology to enable persistent Situational Awareness in the HQ, negating the need to develop products to support standard Battle Rhythm events
   - F. Implement a robust, secure information management system for rapid archive and retrieval of info/data collected on operational deployments
   - G. Develop systems and connectivity to provide reach-back support for forward-deployed PG JTFs, focusing on PG Digital Spine use to connect to lower tactical levels
2. Deliver a PG Multi-Domain targeting process in conjunction with PG J2 and J3

## 1. Phases of Implementation

| Phase | Period | Capability Gate |
|-------|--------|-----------------|
| **Phase One** | Now — Q3 2027 | Foundations: course portfolio reviewed and consolidated, instructor development underway, PGI/IWTC engagement initiated |
| **Phase Two** | Q4 2027 — Q1 2029 | Scale: PGI/IWTC pipeline formalised, military instructor ownership achieved, lessons integration cycle operational |
| **Phase Three** | Q2 2029 — Q3 2030 | Consolidate: leaner, credible, military-owned course portfolio sustained as steady state, feeding Objective 1 |

*Phase One combines the PREP and Phase 1 stages of the detailed plan ([[SBTDWS Action Plan]] §6.3). This objective is a Supporting Effort — net direction is to free instructor capacity for Objective 1 (Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal), not to expand the course portfolio.*

---

## 2. Defining Phased Objectives

### Phase One – 2026/2027

1. **Complete the 6GW training review.** Full audit of the course portfolio — retain, consolidate, or close — mapped against ADW requirements, by **Q3 26**.
2. **Implement the course consolidation plan.** Courses restructured or closed per the PREP-phase review; freed capacity redirected to Objective 1, by **Q4 26**.
3. **Launch the instructor development programme.** Structured programme running in low-tempo (Ramadan/summer) windows, military instructors improving in priority skill areas, from **Q4 26**.
4. **Initiate PGI/IWTC engagement.** Define the split — PGI delivers individual IW training, SBTDWS delivers collective application and validation — from **Q1 27**.

### Phase Two – 2028

5. **Formalise the PGI/IWTC pipeline.** MoU or equivalent covering attendance tracking, unit release, collective consolidation at SBTDWS, and return-to-unit utilisation, by **2028**.

   > [!gap] Depends on D-04 (PENDING)
   > The IW/UW boundary must be defined by Higher HQ before the PGI/SBTDWS split can be finalised — without it, this pipeline is being designed against an undefined standard. See [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-04, [[Unconventional Warfare and Resilience]].

6. **Achieve military instructor ownership** of all core military skills across priority courses, by **2028**.
7. **Operationalise the lessons integration cycle.** Quarterly review — exercise AARs and operational lessons feeding course updates and validation criteria review, by **2028**.

### Phase Three – 2029/2030

8. **Sustain the consolidated, credible course portfolio as steady state**, feeding instructor and capacity gains directly into Objective 1, through **2030**.
9. **Embed honest assessment culture.** Pass rates reflect genuine standards, not social pressure — sustained as a standing Commandant directive.

---

## 3. Identifying Requirements for Achieving Objectives

- Complete the 6GW training review and course consolidation plan (retain / consolidate / close decisions per course).
- Map the current military vs. contractor instructor delivery ratio; identify priority skills for military ownership.
- Design and run the instructor development programme using low-tempo (Ramadan/summer) windows.
- Negotiate and formalise the PGI/IWTC pipeline split (individual IW training vs. collective application and validation).
- Issue a Commandant directive establishing honest assessment culture — pass rates reflecting standards, not pressure.
- Design and operationalise the lessons integration cycle (exercise AARs and operational lessons feeding course review).

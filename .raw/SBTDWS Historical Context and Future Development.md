## Part 1 — Initial Thoughts (Brainstorming Output)

### Key Text Extracted from User Document

**SBTDWS can achieve the directed tasks and overall outcome.** We have the broad foundations and will develop these to meet the intent of developing SBTDWS into the PG All Domain Warfare Centre.

### SBTDWS Areas for Development

- **Collective Training Wing** (Company level Collective Training) — This will form the key backbone to develop the readiness, rehearsal, and validation functions

- **Exercise and Evaluation Wing** (Foundation Skills Test) — Evaluators will reroll, retrain, and form part of the initial Observer Trainer team in support of Collective Wing in achieving the readiness and evaluation responsibility
  - **Recommendation:** Phase out FST and transfer squad readiness responsibility to units as part of their functions

- **SBTDWS CAPDEV Cell** — Has developed and mature projects that can be integrated into the current training continuum. The cell will refocus its remit towards tactical experimentation and pilot capabilities ICW the J3, STC, and CDD until Innovation Command is established
  - **Recommendation (Commander Decision Point):** SBTDWS retain its CAPDEV Cell to develop FDC 5: Tactical Experimentation for PGC

- **ADWC Naming Issue** — The term All Domain Warfare Centre is misleading. It implies ADWC should deliver All Domain Training whilst only having access to Land and Air domains. There are options to expand to include the sea domain; other three domains can be exercised notionally with external support.
  - **Recommendation:** Explore Hameem Beach (ZMU University) as option for SBTDWS Training Area. Cyber, Space, and Electromagnetic domains not achievable with current remit.
  - **Recommendation:** Change the name of ADWC to something more broad. Not essential to identify immediately; core focus will still remain All Domain Warfare.

- **Conduct a thorough, honest review** of current training and courses delivered at SBTDWS against PG's ability to deliver and achieve 6GW.

- **Complete review and overhaul** of functions required. SBTDWS does not have J2, J3, J5, J6, J8 departments.

- **Map all 9 directed tasks** to what we're currently doing and identify how we may be able to achieve this.

- **Develop Thematics:**
  - "Experimentation to Adaptation" — A concept for the ADWC based on Comd PG's catchphrase
  - "Training PG to fight as it would choose to" — Another Comd catchphrase to develop as a concept
  - Thematic to showcase tactical employment of UxS — Not coming up with the systems, but looking at employment
  - IW as a crosscutting thematic

# PGI — Presidential Guard Institute

Delivers individual Irregular Warfare (IW) training through its Irregular Warfare Training Centre (IWTC). PGI owns the individual-training half of the IW pipeline; SBTDWS owns collective application and validation.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]] | [[Commander Decisions Register]]

---

## Role and split with SBTDWS

Per [[PG 2030 Directive]] Task 9, personnel must attend IW training courses delivered by PGI to become deployment-ready for special, joint or partnered operations. The intended split:

- **PGI (individual):** delivers IW training courses through IWTC, selects and develops candidates
- **SBTDWS (collective):** consolidates and validates the collective application of IW-trained personnel, tracks employment, feeds SAG 9 validation

This split is not yet formalised — see D-04 and D-09 dependencies below.

## Open dependencies

- **PGI/IWTC course alignment** is listed as a direct dependency in the [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)|5-LOE implementation slide]]: "Collective validation has no individual-training foundation to build on" without it.
- **D-04 (IW/UW boundary)** in [[Commander Decisions Register]] gates PGI/IWTC scope and throughput requirements — Higher HQ must define the boundary between Pl(-) IW capability (PGI/IWTC delivered) and UW capability (handed off from SOC) before PGI's individual-training scope can be finalised.
- The [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] (Objective 3, Phase 1) targets initiating PGI engagement Q1 27, with a formal MoU-equivalent pipeline agreement targeted for 2028.
- The 5-LOE slide adds a decision item not yet in the formal register: **who settles PGI disputes?** — distinct from D-04, currently untracked.

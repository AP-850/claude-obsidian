# SBTDWS Core Focus — Course Portfolio Decision

A one-page Course of Action (COA) decision brief, produced as a **J3/J7 combined meeting follow-up** (DRAFT). It asks PG Comd to decide whether SBTDWS's current desert warfare course programme remains the main output as the Multi Domain Training Centre (MDTC) is developed, or whether MDTC becomes the main output with the course programme reduced to an agreed core.

## Decision Required

As SBTDWS develops the MDTC, does the current desert warfare course programme remain the main output, or does the MDTC become the main output with the course programme reduced to an agreed core?

---

## COA 1 — Retain and Add

Keep the current focus. Build the MDTC alongside it.

- **What it means:** SBTDWS keeps the full desert warfare course programme as its main output. MDTC is developed as a second, separate output of the same organisation.
- **What it costs:** Additional manpower on top of current establishment; new expertise required (Observer Trainers, scenario design, EXCON/HICON, LVC); a second budget line and additional facility capacity.
- **Main risk:** Two outputs delivered by one organisation. If the manpower bid is not met in full, both outputs are weakened and the MLOC/IOC/FOC dates are not achievable.
- **Required before adoption:** Full manpower and budget uplift approved before **Q4 26**.

## COA 2 — Progressive Pivot

The MDTC becomes the main output over time.

- **What it means:** MDTC becomes the main output; the course programme is reduced to an agreed core. Remaining courses are either stopped or handed to another organisation.
- **What it costs:** Fewer new posts — existing staff, facilities and budget are re-tasked to the MDTC. Course throughput to PG units falls during the change.
- **Main risk:** PG units lose course places before MDTC is able to replace that output. A change of this scope may require cover under the founding decree.
- **Required before adoption:** Agreed list of courses to keep; a named organisation to receive handed-off courses; decree position confirmed.

## COA 3 — Hybrid *(assessed most likely outcome)*

Keep a defined core. Reduce the remainder at each gate.

- **What it means:** A defined desert warfare core is kept permanently. The remainder of the course programme is reduced step by step, matched to what MDTC can absorb at each gate.
- **What it costs:** Manpower uplift smaller than COA 1, phased across the plan. Requires a review and re-scoping activity at each gate.
- **Main risk:** Without agreed criteria for what is kept, each reduction becomes a separate argument rather than one endorsed plan.
- **Required before adoption:** Standing authority to review and adjust the course programme at **MLOC Q4 27, IOC Q4 28, and FOC Q4 30**.

> [!key-insight] Gate dates match and sharpen the existing timeline
> MLOC Q4 27, IOC Q4 28, and FOC Q4 30 are consistent with [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]]'s existing phase windows (Phase 1 gate MLOC previously flagged as an unconfirmed Q4 27 *assumption*; Phase 2 IOC window was Q4 27–Q1 29 with no single confirmed date; Phase 3 FOC window was Q2 29–Q3 30, with "2030" as the only firm year). This brief is the first source giving single, specific dates for all three gates in one place, and they land inside the existing windows rather than contradicting them. Recommend treating MLOC Q4 27 / IOC Q4 28 / FOC Q4 30 as the current working dates on [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]].

## Not Part of This Decision

- Desert warfare remains a permanent core competency of the institution. It is not removed under any option.
- The founding decree, the institutional name, and the heritage question are separate items **already raised as RFI-1** — see [[RFI to PG HQ]].
- What is being decided is how much of the wider course programme is retained, not whether desert warfare training continues.

> [!key-insight] Confirms RFI-1 has reached J3/J7 and is being actively referenced
> This brief explicitly defers the naming/heritage/decree question to "RFI-1" by that exact label — matching [[RFI to PG HQ]]'s own RFI-1 ("Institutional name and source-of-truth document") item number precisely. This is the first direct evidence that the RFI document is circulating and being used as a reference point by whoever is running the J3/J7 combined meeting process, not just sitting with the advisor. It also confirms this Core Focus decision and the D-10 naming decision are being deliberately kept as two separate decision threads, not merged.

## What We Need to Proceed

- Direction on which courses are protected and which may be reduced, stopped, or handed over.
- Authority to build the implementation plan against a reduced course programme after MLOC, subject to review at each gate.
- A named organisation to receive any course handed off.
- Confirmation that reduced course output during the change is understood and accepted at PG HQ level.

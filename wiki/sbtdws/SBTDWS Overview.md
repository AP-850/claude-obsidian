# SBTDWS — Current State Overview

Saeed Bin Tahnoun Desert Warfare School (SBTDWS). A three-wing desert warfare training school under the Presidential Guard Command. It has been directed to evolve into the PG All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC) by 2030.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]] | [[SBTDWS CAPDEV Projects]]

---

## Mission (Current)

Deliver individual and collective desert warfare training up to company level, run evaluation and certification, and develop tactical equipment and doctrine. The school operates in a full desert environment and has international training reach.

Core aim: "World-class center of excellence for Desert Warfare" supporting joint and international forces.

---

## Structure — Three Wings

### Individual Skills Wing

Delivers individual niche Desert Warfare training.

Courses include:
- Desert Driving
- Desert Integration
- Desert Skills (navigation, survival, SERE)
- Medical
- Patrolling fundamentals
- Camels

**Assessment:** Mature individual pipeline. 70%+ of the ADWC foundation already exists here.

---

### Exercise and Evaluation Wing (E&E Wing)

Responsible for assessment and certification.

Courses include:
- Foundation Skills Test (FST) — squad-level evaluation
- Medical Training

**Key issue:** FST currently only covers squad level. No trained Observer Trainer (OT) cadre for SAG 9 / SAG 10 validation. Recommendation: phase out FST and transfer squad readiness to units; retrain E&E Wing as OT cadre for collective validation.

---

### Collective Training Wing (CTW)

Responsible for company-level collective training. Identified as the **Main Effort** for transformation.

Activities:
- Company collective training (patrolling, attacks, navigation, CASEVAC, leadership)
- Navigation and camels
- Foreign forces training
- After Action Reviews
- Exercise support (Observer Trainer role)

**Assessment:** Already running battle-lab-style exercises. The addition of CAPDEV systems (UxS, deception assets, dispersed logistics) transforms exercises into multi-domain testbeds with minimal extra workload.

---

## Supporting Cells

### CAPDEV Cell

Responsible for all CAPDEV projects and trials. See [[SBTDWS CAPDEV Projects]] for full portfolio.

Current projects include: Paramotor, Electric Motorcycles, UGV, FPV Drones, Multi-Spectral Camouflage, Razor Ambulance, Water Generation, Vehicle Simulator, Drone Resupply.

**Recommendation:** CAPDEV Cell retains its remit and refocuses toward FDC 5: Tactical Experimentation (Makhlab 1 PoC) under J3 WDC governance until PG Innovation Command is established.

---

### Systems Approach to Training (SAT) Cell

Responsible for course design and quality control of all courses.

**Future role:** Doctrine and Training Authority — manages training standards, OT accreditation, ADW competency profiles, and standards publication.

---

## J-Staff Gap

SBTDWS does not currently have J2, J3, J5, J6, or J8 departments. This is a critical gap for the ADWC function. Resolution requires a PG HQ J-Staff support decision (required before Q4 26 structural reorganisation).

---

## Key Strengths (What Makes Transformation Viable)

Most militaries building an ADWC lack one or more of these. SBTDWS already has all of them:

| Capability | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Full desert training area | Existing |
| Individual-to-collective training pipeline | Existing |
| CAPDEV portfolio (multi-domain seeds) | Existing |
| Industry access (Marakeb, IGG, Polaris, WaterGen) | Existing |
| International training reach | Existing |
| Exercises run regularly | Existing |

The ADWC transformation is therefore **evolution, not replacement**.

---

## ADWC Naming Issue

"All Domain Warfare Centre" implies six domains (land, air, maritime, cyber, space, EM). SBTDWS currently has direct access to only two (land, air). Maritime and other three can be exercised notionally with external support.

**Recommendation:** Revisit the ADWC name before the 2028 public-facing showcase year. Core mission remains All Domain Warfare regardless of name.

Hameem Beach (ZMU University) is under exploration as an option to expand to a sea domain node.

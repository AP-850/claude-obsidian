# Force Generation Pipeline WG — Internal Read-Ahead

**For:** PG 2030 Lead (LTCOL) — agenda review ahead of the working group
**Prepared:** 2026-08-03
**Meeting:** Chaired by PG J3, SBTDWS and PG J7 attending (date subject to change)

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[Force Generation Pipeline ToR]] | [[Commander Decisions Register]]

## Why this meeting matters

This is the first working group of its kind — chaired by J3, bringing SBTDWS and J7 into the same room to discuss integration of validation, collective training and mission rehearsal functions. Nothing on this topic has been discussed outside SBTDWS before now.

> [!key-insight] Agreeing this agenda closes D-02
> Agreeing the approach below effectively satisfies [[Commander Decisions Register]] **D-02** — the Commandant's decision to direct SBTDWS's formal engagement with J3 and J7 on the Force Generation Pipeline ToR. This isn't a separate signature step later; sign-off here, acting for the Commandant, is the act that closes it.

The [[Force Generation Pipeline ToR]] (v0.1, drafted 2026-06-02) is the working document behind this discussion. It is still a draft — present it Wednesday as a document to shape jointly with J3/J7, not one tabled for signature. Expect it to change after this discussion.

---

## Recommended opening move

Propose, rather than assume, this WG's own remit: that it exists to develop the lane split, resourcing, and governance sections of the ToR under J3's chairing authority. Confirm or adjust with J3 live — since even we're not certain what J3 intends the forum's scope to be, better to surface that question first than build the whole session on an assumption.

Rather than pre-trimming to "what we think matters most," raise the full landscape below and let J3 (chair) decide what gets worked now vs escalated vs parked.

- **ACT** — this room can just agree it
- **SHAPE** — J7's present, so start the joint work even if final sign-off sits above this room
- **ESCALATE** — name it for awareness; this room's job is to register it, not resolve it

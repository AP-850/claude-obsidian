## Key Analytical Outputs

1. Three-wing structure: DWW (JTW analog), CTVMR Wing (CTC-L analog, main effort), TEIW (novel, no Australian equivalent)
2. CTVMR's three non-negotiable pillars: OPFOR, OC/T cadre, AAR process — interdependent
3. Mission Rehearsal exceeds the Australian model — closer to NTC/JRTC pre-deployment MRX construct
4. TEIW risk: scope creep absorbing CTVMR resources before main effort matures — narrow charter required
5. Key finding: OC/T cadre has the longest lead time and is the determining factor for CTVMR quality

# PGIC — PG Innovation Command

PG Innovation Command (PGIC) is the enterprise-level command layer above the ATI-Hub. It sets priorities, governs the innovation portfolio, allocates resources, and authorises transitions to CAPDEV/acquisition.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ATI-Hub Concept]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]]

---

## Purpose

PGIC is pathfinding, not procurement. It de-risks options and hands validated solutions to established acquisition mechanisms.

**PGIC (Enterprise Command):** Sets priorities, governs portfolio, allocates resources, authorises transitions.
**ATI-Hub (Execution Node):** Designs, prototypes, tests, validates rapidly; develops TTPs and training integration; enables limited organic production (0–100 units).

---

## What PGC Is Being Asked to Approve

1. Establish PG Innovation Command (PGIC) under PGC
2. Authorise ATI-Hub as a PGIC execution component (Desert Warfare node)
3. Approve a 24-month phased implementation (pathfinding → system build → scale)
4. Endorse delegated authorities for rapid prototyping, testing, and transition gates
5. Direct alignment with existing CAPDEV and acquisition frameworks

## Operating Model

**Problem-led:** Command-endorsed problem statements with named sponsors and operator champions.

**Three Horizons (70/20/10):**
- 70% — improving current capability and training
- 20% — emerging technologies with near-term impact
- 10% — disruptive concepts for future advantage

**End-to-End Pipeline:** Ideation → rapid prototyping → tactical evaluation → transition gate → organic pilot production → handover to CAPDEV/prime.

**People-centric:** Operators embedded with engineers; early end-user input at speed.

**Ecosystem-enabled:** Off-camp facility and controlled commercial language to enable industry and academic participation.

## Why This Works

| Factor | How it helps |
|--------|-------------|
| Command authority | Clear governance under PGC prevents isolation; accelerates transition |
| Speed with control | Flat execution within ATI-Hub; vertical oversight through PGIC |
| Reduced risk | Early trials, kill/continue gates, full DLOD/FIC fielding packages |
| Sovereignty | Organic pilot production supports resilience and non-attribution |
| Early wins | FPV and UxS programs deliver visible outcomes within 6–12 months |

| System Build | 6–18 months | Full pipeline operational, TEX-Wing active, FPV Schoolhouse established, Hameem planning |

## Success Measures

- Reduced time from problem to field trial
- Number of capabilities transitioned with full DLOD/FIC packages
- Operator adoption and satisfaction
- Cost and resilience benefits versus imported solutions

## Relationship to SBTDWS CAPDEV Cell

PGIC is the long-term home for the CAPDEV Cell's portfolio. However, timing matters:

- CAPDEV Cell should NOT hand over until Makhlab 1 PoC is complete and PGIC is stood up with governance
- Interim: CAPDEV Cell refocuses toward FDC 5 (Q1 27) under J3 WDC governance
- PGIC handover protocol to be defined early to avoid duplication and capability gap

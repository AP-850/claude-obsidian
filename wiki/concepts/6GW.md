# 6GW — Sixth-Generation Warfare

The doctrinal target state behind the SBTDWS → [[ADWC Transformation Concept|ADWC]] transformation: PG force elements able to synchronise effects across all six domains — air, sea, land, cyber, electromagnetic (EMS), and space — rather than the desert-warfare-only training SBTDWS currently delivers.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[PG 2030 Directive]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]]

---

## Source

[[PG 2030 Directive]] purpose statement (para 3.15): SBTDWS is to "verify [PG units'] readiness to execute joint operations across the six domains... ensuring their preparedness to fight sixth-generation warfare." The [[ADWC Implementation Plan (5-LOE Slide)|5-LOE implementation slide]] restates this as the Intent line: training and validating PG units' ability to synchronise effects across all six domains "so they are capable of waging Sixth-generation Warfare (6GW)."

## How it drives the plan

- **6GW training review** is the first gating action in both [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] (PREP phase) and the 5-LOE slide (LOE 2, Phase 1): every current SBTDWS course is audited against 6GW requirements before any structural reorganisation proceeds — target NLT Q3 26.
- **Domain scope is not yet fully resolved.** [[SBTDWS Action Plan]] framing guidance sets domain priority as land and air, with maritime, space, cyber and EMS scoped "progressively if required" — i.e. 6GW is the doctrinal end-state, but which domains SBTDWS itself trains/validates (versus depends on J6/J7 for) is still an open PG HQ decision. The 5-LOE slide's "Decisions needed from PG HQ by Q4 26" box makes this explicit: *is maritime in scope?* and *who owns cyber, EMS and space?* are both unresolved.

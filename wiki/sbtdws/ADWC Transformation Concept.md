# ADWC Transformation Concept

The strategic concept for evolving SBTDWS into the Presidential Guard All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC) by 2030.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[SBTDWS Overview]] | [[SBTDWS 2030 Timeline]] | [[Three Development Thematics]]

---

## 2030 Intent (EMAD-Approved Language)

> By 2030, SBTDWS will have evolved into the All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC), delivering training, validation, tactical experimentation, and mission rehearsal as the field-execution arm of PG readiness. SBTDWS will contribute routinely to FE@R declarations to MOD via the J7 exercise programme, and drive PG capability adaptation through governed FDC 5 experimentation under J3 WDC.

> SBTDWS will extend its scope from training PG force elements to operate in the desert environment to enabling them to prosecute All Domain Warfare — training and validating PG units' ability to conduct effective operations which synchronise effects in all 6 domains, ensuring they are capable of successfully waging Sixth Generation Warfare (6GW).

## Design Principles

These are the governing principles for all transformation work. They must not be violated to maintain credibility with the O6 CoC and the PG Transformation Team.

1. **Credibility before ambition** — fix the foundations before building the brand
2. **Truth before excellence** — an honest assessment drives transformation; optimistic briefing does not
3. **Evolution, not replacement** — preserve existing wings; restructure for efficiency, don't rebuild
4. **Commander's language:**
   - "Experimentation to Adaptation"
   - "Training PG to fight as it would choose to"

## Five ADWC Functions (Endstate)

The five linked roles SBTDWS must fulfil as ADWC:

1. **Collective Training** — Multi-domain training up to formation level; the readiness function
2. **Validation** — SAG 9 / SAG 10 validation authority; Observer Trainer cadre; authority to fail
3. **Mission Rehearsal** — EXCON, HICON, scenario design; feeds J7 exercise programme
4. **Tactical Experimentation** — FDC 5 under J3 WDC governance; Makhlab 1 as the first sprint
5. **Doctrine and Standards** — Training design, OT accreditation, ADW competency profiles

## Structural Transformation

The transformation does not replace wings. It restructures their roles:

| Current | Transformed Role |
|---------|-----------------|
| Individual Skills Wing | Desert Warfare Training Wing (unchanged) |
| Collective Training Wing | Multi-Domain Collective Training (Main Effort) |
| E&E Wing | Validation and Evaluation Wing (OT cadre, SAG 9/10) |
| CAPDEV Cell | FDC 5 Tactical Experimentation (Makhlab 1) |
| SAT Cell | Doctrine and Training Authority |
| (New) | All-Domain Integration Cell (ISR, fires, EW, data fusion) |

The new All-Domain Integration Cell is the heart of transformation — the element that synchronises effects across domains during exercises and experiments.

## Archetypes Referenced

Three global archetypes for ADWC-equivalent organisations:

- **Archetype A: Training / Exercise / Scenario Centre** — JWC (NATO), NADWC (US). Rehearses multi-domain ops, validates HQs, generates lessons. This is SBTDWS's nearest-term role.
- **Archetype B: Operational Integration / Effects Hub** — MDTF pattern (US Army). Synchronises sensors-to-shooters across domains. The longer-term aspiration.
- **Archetype C: Experimentation / Rapid Capability Development** — NIWC-style (US Navy). Connects industry/tech to operators, runs integration events.

SBTDWS should blend A and C first, moving toward B as Hameem infrastructure matures.

## SBTDWS's Unique Strategic Position

Most ADWC equivalents are temperate, air-centric, or HQ-centric. None specialise in:
- Austere desert logistics
- Extreme heat operations
- Desert manoeuvre
- Dispersed operations in complex terrain

This makes SBTDWS a globally unique "Desert Multi-Domain Warfare Centre" — a positioning that is both strategically differentiated and entirely grounded in existing capability.

## Key Risks

1. **ADWC name vs domain access** — name promises six domains; currently have two. Must revisit before 2028 public-facing showcase year.
2. **O5 mid-management resistance** — critical inflection point; deliberate engagement required alongside O6 CoC briefing.
3. **Contractor dependency** — several courses are contractor-dependent. Unsustainable as ADWC. Military ownership required by 2028.
4. **Ramadan/summer windows** — 4-month training gaps annually. Treat as opportunity for instructor dev, planning, experimentation prep.
5. **Sustained leadership** — must be embedded in PG governance to outlive command tours.

# Source: Atlantic Council — Defense Innovation Adoption (2023)

Navigation: [[sources/_index]] | [[Research: Defense Innovation Governance Tools]]

The Commission's final report on why the DoD fails to adopt innovation and what governance models actually work. High confidence: institutional primary source with extensive evidence base.

---

## 8 Structural Barriers to Defense Innovation Adoption

1. Outdated R&D model — DoD's processes designed when it funded most global R&D; federal share now below 20%
2. Long timelines — systems deliver to requirements defined 10+ years earlier
3. Shrinking industrial base — down 40% over the past decade
4. Valley of death — billions fund prototypes; few transition to production
5. Program-centric acquisition — prevents interoperability, locks in requirements
6. Workforce bureaucratic culture — excessive compliance, discourages creative problem-solving
7. Limited technology understanding — requirements personnel lack expertise in AI, quantum, biotech
8. Cumbersome reporting — budget justifications spanning tens of thousands of pages

Note: all 8 barriers are structural/governance failures, not technology failures.

---

## Task Force 59 Model (Best Practice at Unit Level)

The report identifies TF 59 as the strongest example of successful unit-level innovation governance:
- Problem-centric organisation: structure built around actual operational challenges, not bureaucratic requirements
- Meritocracy of ideas: "the best ideas prevailed" regardless of rank
- Team of teams execution: collaborative networks across government, academia, and industry
- Capability as a service: pivot from traditional procurement to data/services models

---

## What Works: Recommended Governance Framework

- Capability portfolio model (5 PEOs with consolidated budgets)
- Rapid requirements process replacing JCIDS (which "locks down all requirements up front")
- Bridge funding ($250M annually) to accelerate from demonstration to production
- Six-page program overviews replacing tens-of-thousands-of-pages justifications
- Technology transition target: demonstrated capabilities reach warfighters within 12-24 months (vs. 2-4 year historical lag)

---

## Relevance to TACTIX

The 8 barriers describe exactly the environment TACTIX operates in. A tool that generates a "six-page program overview equivalent" (command decision brief) from structured trial data directly addresses barrier 8. The TF 59 model — flat hierarchy, problem-centric, meritocracy — is the design philosophy TACTIX should adopt for its UI.

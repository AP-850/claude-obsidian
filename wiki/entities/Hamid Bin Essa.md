# Hamid Bin Essa (HBE)

PG unit converting to an Airborne/Air Assault role (FOC Dec 27), responsible for Medium Scale Airborne Forcible Entry (SAG 15). Building toward a deployable 1-star JTF HQ, receiving Air Assault knowledge transfer from [[Al Forsan]].

## Tasks (Aug 2026 register)

- Responsible PG unit for Medium Scale Airborne Forcible Entry (SAG 15) — OPS approval from J3, pass-off to J7 for training
- Develop a deployable 1-star JTF HQ capable of integrating PG and joint force enablers
- MAKHLAB 2 — develop and integrate AI-enabled Uncrewed Systems (UxS)
- Transfer Air Assault knowledge and expertise from Al Forsan to support HBE's conversion transition
- Act as Training Requirements Authority (TRA) for all HBE training courses delivered by [[PGI]]
- Be prepared to prosecute targets allocated by PGI's Multi-Domain Targeting Cell
- Ensure appropriate personnel attend IW training courses delivered by PGI

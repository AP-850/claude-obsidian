## The Template (the three headings)

J3-format Action Plans require three numbered sections, each building on the last. These headings are the template — every unit's Action Plan must use this structure, regardless of subject matter:

1. **Phases of Implementation**
2. **Defining Phased Objectives**
3. **Identifying Requirements for Achieving Objectives**

---

## The Worked Example: Falcons Brigade — Specialized Technical Skills and Unmanned Systems Transition

This is [[Falcons Brigade]]'s actual Action Plan content, consistent with their established role as the PG's UxS/FPV talent pipeline and Makhlab 1 partner force (see [[Makhlab 1 Project]], [[PG 2030 Force Design Concepts]]).

### 1. Phases of Implementation

- **Phase One** — Individuals' qualification on "Specialized Technical Skills".
- **Phase Two** — Reorganisation of units and transition to systems, upon reaching Initial Operational Capability (IOC).
- **Phase Three** — Transition to unmanned systems, upon achieving Full Operational Capability (FOC).

### 2. Defining Phased Objectives

For each phase above, objectives that translate the vision into tangible outcomes, with a timeline for achieving each:

- **Phase One – 2027:** (1) Approval of the Specialized Technical Skills Training Course programme. (2) Conducting the first training course on Specialized Technical Skills.
- **Phase Two – 2028:** (1) Defining roles and responsibilities for each unit. (2) Identifying required unmanned capabilities.

### 3. Identifying Requirements for Achieving Objectives

Initiatives, projects, and programmes needed to achieve each objective:

- Updating doctrines.
- Developing current training courses.
- Qualifying and training instructors.

## Relevance to SBTDWS

This is the formatting standard against which J3 will assess SBTDWS-originated action plans — demonstrated here against a real unit (Falcons Brigade), not a hypothetical.

**Applied (2026-06-23):** [[SBTDWS Action Plan]]'s three objectives have each been reformatted to this template, as three separate documents — matching the template's single-capability-line grain rather than forcing all three objectives into one document:

- [[J3 Action Plan — Collective Training, Validation and Mission Rehearsal]] (Main Effort)
- [[J3 Action Plan — Tactical Experimentation]] (Main Effort)
- [[J3 Action Plan — Training and Course Management]] (Supporting Effort)

Each compresses the detailed plan down to phases tied to capability gates (MLOC/IOC/FOC-equivalent), 2–4 dated objectives per phase, and a flat requirements list — and flags every objective that depends on a still-PENDING [[Commander Decisions Register]] item with an explicit callout, rather than silently assuming the decision.

[[Force Generation Pipeline ToR]] is a ToR rather than an Action Plan — not subject to this template directly, but any Action Plan that follows from the signed ToR should use this structure.

> [!key-insight] Combined version produced (2026-06-23)
> The three separate plans above have been combined into [[J3 Action Plan — SBTDWS Transformation (Combined)]] — same three-heading structure, phases defined once (since all three objectives share the same calendar), objectives as sub-sections under each phase. Combining surfaced one finding the separate documents didn't show: Objective 2's "lessons exploitation mechanism" and Objective 3's "lessons integration cycle" are the same machinery described twice — recommend building one shared lessons cycle, not two. Both the three-document and combined formats remain available; neither supersedes the other.

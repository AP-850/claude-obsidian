## 1. **Milrem Robotics - Type-X RCV** (Estonia)

  - Up to 4 100 kg mission module.
  - 25–50 mm autocannons (e.g., Cockerill CPWS II), 7.62 mm MG, ATGMs (Javelin/Spike), and multi-canister loitering-munitions racks .
- **Sensors & Autonomy**:
  - Visual, IR, and LIDAR arrays with deep-learning terrain mapping.
  - “Follow-me” convoy autonomy.
  - AI-assisted navigation; humans retain engagement control ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type-X_%28unmanned_ground_vehicle%29?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

  - Telescopic mast with 40 km datalink.
  - STANAG 4569 hardened Ethernet/comms ([milremrobotics.com](https://milremrobotics.com/type-x-2/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
- **Armor**:
  - STANAG Level 4 ballistic and Level 1 mine protection ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type-X_%28unmanned_ground_vehicle%29?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 🛠 Program Status & Procurements

- In production since 2020; available to NATO allies ([en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type-X_%28unmanned_ground_vehicle%29?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 🧠 Autonomy & C2 Architecture

- Combines LIDAR + AI to create terrain maps and execute autonomous maneuvers.
- Human-in-the-loop command: oversight via remote console; lethal engagements commanded by humans.
- Comm architecture enables platoon-like C2 from a central node.

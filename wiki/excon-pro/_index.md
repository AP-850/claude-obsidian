# EXCON Pro — Expert Feedback Programme

EXCON Pro is an AI-powered exercise planning system developed by a colleague. User role: supporting contributor — focused on pushing the product to trusted parties and experts for initial feedback.

Navigation: [[index]] | [[Outreach Framework]] | [[Expert Shortlist]]

---

## Product Summary

**Tagline:** The world's first AI-powered exercise planning system, built for military training establishments, emergency services, and joint operations.

**Core positioning:** Built for the Operational Planner.

**Key capabilities:**
- Live Intelligence Integration (ACLED, GDELT, ReliefWeb) — real-world grounded scenarios
- 21-agent QA Pipeline — doctrine compliance, inject logic, safety, training objective coverage
- 24-Document Package — General Idea to Role Player Briefs in ~30 minutes vs weeks manually
- Lessons Library — semantically embedded, compounding value across exercises
- Legal and ROE Module — NATO standard, LOAC/IHL, use-of-force decision trees
- Task Force Builder — full NATO ORBAT, 38+ nations
- GEOINT Mapper (v2.3) — MGRS mapping, NAIs/AOAs/Key Terrain, sensitivity-aware

3. What is the pricing model — per exercise, per seat, per organisation?
4. Who signs the contract — J7, training establishment CO, procurement?
5. Is there an NDA required before a demo, or is it open at concept brief level?

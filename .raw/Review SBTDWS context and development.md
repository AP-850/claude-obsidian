## Part 2 — Updated EMAD Content (User-Provided Final Version)

### SBTDWS 2030 INTENT

By 2030, SBTDWS will have evolved into the All Domain Warfare Centre (ADWC), delivering training, validation, tactical experimentation, and mission rehearsal as the field-execution arm of PG readiness. SBTDWS will contribute routinely to FE@R declarations to MOD via the J7 exercise programme, and drive PG capability adaptation through governed FDC 5 experimentation under J3 WDC.

SBTDWS will extend its scope from training PG force elements to operate in the desert environment to enabling them to prosecute All Domain Warfare. This will focus on training and validating PG units' ability to conduct effective operations which synchronise effects in all 6 domains — to ensure they are capable of successfully waging Sixth Generation Warfare (6GW). To reflect this expanded remit, SBTDWS will be referred to as the PG 'All Domain Warfare Centre' (ADWC) by 2030.

### GAPS
- J-Staff functions (J2/J3/J5/J6/J8) — establish or allocate from PG HQ
- Observer Trainer cadre — to validate against SAG 9 (Combined UW) & SAG 10 (R&CB) OPS
- Experimentation governance framework — J3 WDC methodology and feedback loop
- Hameem mission rehearsal infrastructure — capital build FY27–FY30
- Instructor development programme — replace contractor delivery for core military skills
- All-domain training enablers — cyber/EM/space via J6, R&D Hub, coalition partners

### LINKAGES
- PG J7 — defining linkage; SBTDWS provides EXCON, OTs, scenarios, validation, J-function support to J7 exercise programme
- J3 WDC — experimentation governance; SBTDWS as field execution arm
- PG Innovation Cmd — CAPDEV cell handover when stood up

- Falcons Bde / SOF — Makhlab 1 PoC, SAG 9 & 10 partners
- Operational Units — formalise how units feed requirements into exercise and validation

### DEPENDENCIES

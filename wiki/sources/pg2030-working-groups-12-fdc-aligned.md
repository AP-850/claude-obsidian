# New PG 2030 WGs (14 Aug 26) — 12 FDC-Aligned Working Groups

A 6-page bilingual (EN/AR) formal document, "Force Design Concepts Working Groups: Presidential Guard Vision 2030," establishing one Working Group (WG) per Force Design Concept (FDC) — 12 WGs total, each with a Concept statement, Working Group Lead, Working Group Members, Related Force Design Concepts, and a Desired End State.

## What This Document Is

The formal execution mechanism for PG Vision 2030: each Working Group translates one Force Design Concept into an executable, measurable action plan (objectives, phases, priorities, timelines, KPIs), monitors progress, and reports quarterly to the Commander of the Presidency Guard through its designated Lead. WG2 (Unit Tactical Concepts) is explicitly marked "INTERNAL to Each Unit" rather than a cross-branch body like the other 11.

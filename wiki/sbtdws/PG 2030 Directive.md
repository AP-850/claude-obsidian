# PG 2030 Directive — SBTDWS Guiding Tasks

The formal PG 2030 Vision Directive issued to SBTDWS. This is the authoritative source document — Section 3.15 of the PG 2030 Directive.

## Directive Text — Section 3.15

**Unit:** Saeed bin Huzn Desert Warfare School

### Purpose Statement

> By the year 2030, the Saeed bin Huzn Desert Warfare School will expand its scope to train personnel and enhance the capabilities of the Presidential Guard to operate in desert environments, enabling them to conduct multi-domain warfare. This will be achieved by focusing on training Presidential Guard units and verifying their readiness to execute joint operations across the six domains (land, sea, air, cyber, electromagnetic, and space), ensuring their preparedness to fight sixth-generation warfare. To support this direction, the school will be developed into a center of excellence for multi-domain warfare for the Presidential Guard by 2030.

## The 9 Guiding Tasks

### Task 1 — Train and validate for multi-domain operations
Develop the capability and expertise for training and validating Presidential Guard units in conducting multi-domain operations that create synchronized effects across air, land, sea, cyber, electromagnetic, and space domains.

### Task 2 — Transform capabilities cell into innovation leadership cell
Transform the existing capabilities cell into an innovation leadership cell within the Presidential Guard, ensuring the school focuses on multi-domain warfare tasks.

*Note: Subsequent guidance clarified the CAPDEV cell transfers to PG Innovation Command (PGIC). SBTDWS retains tactical experimentation under J3 WDC governance — see [[PGIC Concept]].*

### Task 3 — Act as leading experimental authority
Based on the establishment of a warfare development center within the Presidential Guard under Operations Command, adopt the role of the leading experimental authority by testing operational concepts and developing doctrines and experimental procedures before transferring them to Presidential Guard units.

### Task 4 — Integrate operational trends and lessons learned
Integrate emerging operational trends and lessons learned from practical application into training and course design to ensure the Presidential Guard units achieve operational excellence in the battlefield.

### Task 5 — Launch Makhlab 1 project
Launch the "Makhlab 1" project to support activities that reinforce the Presidential Guard 2030 vision ("Excellence"), highlighting key elements in the transformation agenda of the Presidential Guard. See [[Makhlab 1 Project]].

> **Name correction:** Source documents from April 2026 used the name "Nukhbah 1." The correct project name is "Makhlab 1" — a UxS Tactical Drones PoC, not a broad transformation umbrella.

### Task 6 — Enhance Khaimah site into world-class mission simulation facility
Enhance the "Khaimah" site to become a world-class training facility for mission simulation, enabling Presidential Guard units to conduct realistic and high-level training before deployment. See [[Hameem Mission Rehearsal Concept]].

### Task 7 — SAG 9: Irregular Warfare (Joint)
Develop the capability, knowledge, and expertise to fulfill the responsibility of "higher command" within the Presidential Guard, ensuring units can execute tasks related to Study and Evaluation Group No. 9 "Irregular Warfare (Joint)" by 2030. See [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]].

### Task 8 — SAG 10: Vital Capabilities Development
Develop the capability, knowledge, and expertise to fulfill the responsibility of "higher command" within the Presidential Guard, ensuring units can execute tasks related to Study and Evaluation Group No. 10 "Vital Capabilities Development" by 2030. See [[SAG 9 and SAG 10]].

### Task 9 — Recruit suitable personnel into irregular warfare courses
Ensure the recruitment of suitable personnel into irregular warfare training courses conducted by the Presidential Guard Training Authority, enabling them to qualify as deployment-ready cadres capable of executing special, joint, or partnered operations.

## Corrected Guidance (April 2026)

The directive was initially interpreted broadly. A corrected reading confirmed:

1. **ADWC is the named end-state** — not a generic "expansion" but a specific institutional designation.
2. **Desert warfare remains the context** — all-domain warfare becomes the purpose, not a replacement.
3. **CAPDEV transfers to PGIC** — SBTDWS retains tactical experimentation under J3 governance only.
4. **Five core functions** emerge from the tasks: Train, Validate, Experiment, Rehearse, Enable Command.

**Commander's summary line:** "SBTDWS will become the Presidential Guard's centre of excellence for desert-based multi-domain warfare, responsible for preparing, validating, and evolving forces for joint operations across the six domains through training, experimentation, innovation, and mission simulation."

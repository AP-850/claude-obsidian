# PG 2030 Working Groups

A bilingual (EN/AR, CamScanner-scanned) 4-page proposal, "suggested from J3 advisor," establishing a formal Working Group (WG) governance structure for PG 2030 — 8 WGs, each with a Task Lead, an Endstate, and a defined membership list drawn from PG staff branches and units.

Kept here for continuity and cross-referencing — several Leads and roles below (Falcons Bde/MAKHLAB, J6/Digital Spine, J7/Warfare Development Center) are corroborated, not contradicted, by the new structure. See the Cross-Cutting Observations section on the new page for the WG-to-WG mapping.

## Background, Purpose, Concept

**Background:** Due to interoperability requirements, cross-functional area work, and the complexity of many PG 2030 tasks, formal Working Groups are required to produce a coherent and synchronised PG output — output that would NOT be expected to emerge from normal Unit and Staff Directorate work alone.

**Purpose:** Working Groups give Units and Staff Directorates direct input into the development, planning, and execution of each task. Permanent (physical or virtual) group membership eliminates "stove piping" of tasks and issues and speeds up development.

**Concept:** Task Leads develop the timeline, framework, intermediate objectives, and KPIs for their task and update PG J3 (the PG 2030 Lead) as appropriate. Units must provide WG members able to maintain membership for a minimum of 12 months.

**Task Lead requirements include, but are not limited to:**
- Establishing a roadmap and timeline for programme completion
- Serving as Task Lead for a minimum of 12 months
- Establishing meeting frequency and location (physical or virtual)
- Determining and securing appropriate Working Group membership from Units
- Establishing meeting agendas
- Producing PG 2030 outputs for briefings, letters, etc.
- Dividing work requirements as appropriate and necessary
- Being prepared to brief PG and MOD senior leaders on task status as directed

## The 8 Working Groups

### WG1 — Development of Multi-Domain Targeting Capability
**Lead:** J2
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses a persistent, intelligence-driven Multi-Domain Targeting capability integrating sensors, analysts, decision-makers, and effectors across all domains, riding on the PG Digital Spine. Replaces separate "Sensors-Analysts-Shooters" functions with one integrated targeting capability, trained, exercised, and validated against relevant SAG mission requirements.
**Members:** J1, J2, J4, J6, J7, NCTJC, Falcon, AF, HBE, SOC, PSG, Innovation Command

> [!key-insight] Corroborates the Multi-Domain Targeting tasking already tracked
> Matches J2 Task 3, J3 Task 6, and [[NCTJC]] Task 2 from [[sources/updated-pg-2030-tasks-aug-2026]] — this WG is the formal governance vehicle for that same targeting capability, now with a defined cross-branch membership.

### WG2 — Development of the PG Operational Readiness Cycle (ORC)
**Lead:** J3
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses an integrated ORC that synchronises unit training, joint enablers, personnel, equipment, logistics, and readiness reporting; protects designated preparation and readiness periods; and gives the Commander an accurate, timely assessment of PG's ability to "fight tonight," validated against approved Operational Performance Standards across all 21 SAG mission sets.
**Members:** J1, J2, J4, J6, J7, HBE, SOC, PSG, SBTS, PGI

### WG3 — Development of PG Command & Control Models
**Lead:** J3
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses an integrated and scalable C2 Model enabling deployable 1-star JTF HQs (AF, HBE, SOC) capable of integrating PG forces, joint force enablers, and MOD Joint Operations, plus a permanent, scalable 2-star PG HQ.
**Members:** J1, J2, J4, J6, J7, Med, NCTJC, Falcon, J.Fires, AF, HBE, SOC, Innovation Command. Includes deployable J2 cells for both 2-star and 1-star HQs.

### WG4 — Development of the J4 Concept of Support
**Lead:** J4
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses an integrated Concept of Support enabling rapid deployment, sustainment, protection, and recovery of mission-ready PG force packages aligned to the ORC and the 21 SAG mission sets — scalable expeditionary logistics, contractor integration, medical support, pre-positioned material, resilient supply chains, and strategic reach.
**Members:** J1, J2, J3, J6, J7, Medical, Falcon, AF, HBE, SOC, PSG

### WG5 — Coordination, Synchronisation, and Maximisation of the PG MAKHLAB Program
**Lead:** Falcons Brigade
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses a coherent MAKHLAB programme that eliminates duplicated or isolated unit UxS solutions; establishes common technical, data, training, and support standards; delivers an interoperable family of uncrewed, autonomous, and digital capabilities; and accelerates successful capabilities from experimentation to fielding across the force.
**Members:** J1, J2, J3, J6, J7, Falcon, AF, HBE, SOC, PSG, SBTS

> [!key-insight] Falcons Brigade coordinates the WHOLE MAKHLAB family, not just MAKHLAB 1
> This is new and clarifies the earlier MAKHLAB 1 ownership question (see [[Programme MAKHLAB]], [[Makhlab 1 Project]]). Falcons Brigade is the Task Lead for cross-unit MAKHLAB coordination across all serials (1/2/3/4/8, spanning Al Forsan, HBE, SOC, PSG, SRB, J4) — not only the owner/operator of its own MAKHLAB 1 serial. That coordination-lead role plausibly explains why Falcons Brigade appears tasked so directly and broadly around MAKHLAB elsewhere in the task register: it is doing double duty as both a MAKHLAB 1 line unit and the cross-programme coordinating authority. This doesn't fully resolve the SBTDWS/J3 WDC FDC 5 question but adds real structure to why Falcons Brigade shows up so prominently.

### WG6 — Design and Delivery of the PG Digital Spine
**Lead:** J6
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses a secure, resilient, and interoperable Digital Spine connecting HQs, units, sensors, and PED capabilities, able to plug into MOD and partner UAE force systems. Supports the PG C2 model, Multi-Domain Targeting, the ORC, and sensor-to-effect/decision-to-effect integration with common operational picture, trusted data, and standardised planning/C2 tools.
**Members:** J1, J2, J3, J4, J7, NCTJC, Falcon, Joint Fires, AF, HBE, SOC, PSG, SBTS

> [!gap] Digital Spine vs Digital Backbone still unreconciled
> As with [[sources/updated-pg-2030-tasks-aug-2026]], this WG's "PG Digital Spine" (J6-led) is described in terms that overlap heavily with the wiki's existing [[J6 Digital Backbone]] entity. This source doesn't resolve whether they're the same initiative under two names or genuinely distinct — still open.

### WG7 — Development of the PG Warfare Development Center
**Lead:** J7 ("Includes PG Warfare Development Center (J3 Lead)" as a sub-note)
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses a Multi-Domain Training Center at the SBTDWS Warfare Development Command, serving as the command's central hub for turning new concepts, technologies, structures, and TTPs into operational use — supported by an experimentation capability, Observer-Controller teams evaluating PG exercises/forces, and SBTDWS/Hameem exercise capability.
**Members:** J1, J2, J3, J4, J6, J7, Falcon, AF, HBE, SOC, PSG, PGI

> [!contradiction] J7-led WG vs J3-led Warfare Development Centre — plausible non-conflict, needs confirmation
> [[J3 WDC]] describes the PG Warfare Development Centre as J3-owned governance ("SBTDWS is the field execution arm; J3 WDC owns governance, priorities and outputs"). This WG names **J7** as the Task Lead for "Development of the PG Warfare Development Center," with a sub-note "Includes PG Warfare Development Center (J3 Lead)." The most likely reading: J7 leads the *working group that stands up* the Centre (consistent with J7 owning PG's training/exercise architecture generally — see [[J7 LVC Architecture]]), while J3 retains *operational governance* of the Centre once established — a build-vs-run split, not a contradiction. But this is inference, not confirmed. Also note: this document spells it **"Multi-Domain Training Center"** (American "Center") — yet another spelling variant alongside the COA brief's "Multi Domain Training Centre" (British "Centre"). Treat as the same referent; spelling is inconsistent across authors, not a fourth distinct name. See [[ADWC Naming Analysis]].

### WG8 — Development of the PG Simulation Program
**Lead:** J7 (SBTS/SBTDWS prominent among members; a proponent-unit role for simulator upkeep/maintenance/validation is also assigned, not clearly attributed to a single unit in the scan)
**Endstate:** By 2030, PG possesses an integrated Simulation Plan using individual, crew, team, unit, and HQ simulators — combined with Live, Virtual, Constructive (LVC) capabilities — to build, sustain, and objectively assess readiness against Operational Performance Standards for the 21 SAG mission sets. Connects simulator performance to the ORC and currency reporting; assigns a proponent unit responsible for simulator upkeep, maintenance, and validation.
**Members:** J2, J3, J4, J6, NCTJC, Falcon, AF, HBE, SOC, PSG, SBTS, PGI

## Cross-Cutting Observations

- **All 8 WGs nest under PG J3** as the PG 2030 Lead who Task Leads report to — reinforcing J3's overall PG 2030 programme-management role above the individual WG leads.
- **SBTDWS/SBTS appears as a member (not lead) in 6 of 8 WGs** (WG2, WG5, WG6, WG7, WG8, and implicitly WG3 via C2 model work) — confirming SBTDWS's role as a cross-cutting execution partner across nearly the whole PG 2030 programme, consistent with its FDC 5 / experimentation / validation remit.
- **NCTJC appears in WG1, WG3, WG6, WG8** — consistent with its C2 and Multi-Domain Targeting role already tracked on [[NCTJC]].
- **Falcons Brigade leads one WG (MAKHLAB coordination) and is a member of 7 of the other 8** — the single most cross-referenced unit in this document, reflecting its UxS/targeting-web centrality to PG 2030.

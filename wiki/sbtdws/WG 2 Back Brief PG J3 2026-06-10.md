# WG 2 Back Brief PG J3 — 2026-06-10

**Date:** 2026-06-10
**Chaired by:** J3 (PG 2030 Lead)

---

## General Direction (All PG Units)

- Each unit to focus on their specific PG 2030 objectives.
- Each unit to develop a detailed Action Plan for each objective, ready for implementation.

---

## SBTDWS Specific Direction

- Desert warfare remains the core focus.
- Develop the school slowly, in line with the broader PG units tempo.
- Not required to develop all 6 domains now. Prioritize land and partial air.
- Scope viability and feasibility of integrating remaining domains (maritime, space, cyber, EMS) later if required.

---

## Naming

- ADWC is now being referred to as the Multi Domain Warfare Centre (MDWC).
- "Multi Domain" is considered more flexible than "All Domain" in military terminology.
- No official name decision has been made.
- See [[ADWC Naming Analysis]] for background on the naming discussion.

---

## MOD Body

- A push exists to nest within a wider MOD body focused on lessons learned, capability development, and training enhancement.
- Relationship is informal only: for advice and ideas, not an official or structural link.

---

## SBTDWS Initial Analysis

Three lines of effort emerging from the J3 direction:

| Priority | Line of Effort | Notes |
|----------|---------------|-------|
| Main Effort | Collective Training, Validation, and Development | Encompasses mission rehearsal and collective validation against standards |
| Main Effort | Tactical Experimentation (UxS) | Air, land, maritime; TEIW-led |
| Supporting Effort | Courses and Training | Review and consolidate; demoted from previous main effort status |

# Updated PG 2030 Framework — PG Comd Briefing Pack

A 3-page PG-wide briefing pack (CamScanner-scanned, rotated landscape slides) laying out the top-level PG 2030 transformation logic: Vision, the Warfighting Capability Pillars / Force Design Concept / 21 SAG Mission Set architecture, and a PG-wide Phase 1–3 timeline. This is the first wiki source to show how the 12 Force Design Concepts, the 21 SAGs, and the MRC readiness benchmark connect into one end-to-end logic chain, and the first to define **MRC 1–4** explicitly.

## Page 1 — Vision

> The Presidential Guard exists to conduct special missions in support of UAE National Policies and Objectives. These missions are inherently complex, require Rapid Execution, are no-fail, and of the utmost Strategic Importance. The PG operates across the full Operational Spectrum, and is capable of fighting independently or integrated within a Joint or Multinational Force.
>
> To effectively address the changing operational environment, the nature of modern threats, and technological acceleration, the PG must undergo continuous development to remain a force capable of operational adaptation and achieving decisive results. Through the PG2030 initiative, we will forge a force structured to be **adaptive** by nature, **innovative** by design, backed by modern technology, and **constantly ready** to deliver Strategic and Operational success.

This confirms the three strategic themes already tracked on [[PG 2030 Three Themes]] (Adaptive by Nature, Innovative by Design, Constantly Ready) as verbatim vision language, not a paraphrase.

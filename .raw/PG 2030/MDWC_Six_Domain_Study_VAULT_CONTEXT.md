## 2. Strategic Context

### 2.1 PG 2030 Direction

The Presidential Guard's SBT 2030 transformation initiative is driving the evolution of SBTDWS into the Multi Domain Warfare Centre. The J7 has identified six operational domains across which the MDWC must develop training and validation capability. This study is the first analytical product scoping what that means in practice.

### 2.2 Force Design Concept Five (FDC5)

FDC5 is the operative design reference. It introduces cross-domain experimentation and UXS testing requirements that directly shape which domains the MDWC must integrate — particularly maritime UXS T&E and EMS threat emulation for UXS validation.

### 2.3 Study Purpose

This study was initiated to:
1. Analyse all six domains against a consistent framework (requirements → current capability → gaps → what's needed → institutional positioning)
2. Determine which domains sit within MDWC, which require HQ decisions, and which sit elsewhere
3. Produce a Command Level brief to socialise findings with PG Headquarters
4. Build the analytical foundation for resourcing and institutional development planning

## 4. Domain Analysis Summaries

### 4.1 Land — CORE DOMAIN

**Current State:** Active live training at Hameem Proving Ground. Established collective training programme at sub-unit and unit level.

**Key Gaps:**
- No formal collective training validation framework
- No structured lesson capture / TTP codification pipeline
- Limited synthetic/constructive training capability
- No UGV / ground UXS training infrastructure
- Limited OPFOR capability

**What's Needed:**
- Formalised validation framework (standards, metrics, assessor cadre)
- Tactical Adaptation Platform concept → implementation (ORABATV cycle)
- LVC integration — initial increment
- UGV training lanes and SOPs
- OPFOR doctrine and trained element

**Position:** All investments within MDWC authority. Immediate priority.

---

### 4.2 Air — CORE DOMAIN / MAJOR GROWTH REQUIREMENT

**Current State:** Nascent UAS training activity only. No dedicated infrastructure, programme, qualification framework, or T&E framework.

**Key Gaps:**
- No UAS operator training programme or qualification framework
- No UAS training infrastructure at Hameem (airspace, RF management, safety)
- No C-UAS training capability
- No UAS T&E framework aligned to MAKHLAB projects
- JTAC training not institutionalised or accredited
- No synthetic air domain representation

**What's Needed:**
- UAS training range at Hameem (restricted airspace, RF spectrum management, safety protocols)
- UAS operator qualification programme (syllabus, standards, assessors)
- C-UAS training programme
- JTAC training and accreditation pathway
- UAS T&E SOPs aligned to FDC5 and MAKHLAB project offices
- Synthetic air domain representation in collective exercises

**Position:** Highest urgency development area. MDWC authority. Immediate priority.

---

### 4.3 Maritime (C/MAR) — CONDITIONAL

**Current State:** No maritime capability exists within MDWC. Hameem has no maritime infrastructure.

**Key Gaps:** All gaps are conditional pending HQ direction.

**Position:** MDWC does not assess a standing maritime training requirement. FDC5 *may* create a maritime UXS T&E requirement — this cannot be determined at MDWC level. Decision required from PG HQ.

> **HQ Action Required:** Determine whether MDWC has a maritime UXS T&E role under FDC5 and assign authority and resourcing accordingly.

---

### 4.4 Space — EXTERNAL

**Current State:** No capability. Not an MDWC requirement.

**Position:** Space domain training sits with a TBC organisation within the UAE defence establishment. MDWC should acknowledge space as an enabling domain in exercise design (e.g. GPS-denied scenario injects) without assuming institutional ownership.

> **HQ Action Required:** Confirm which organisation carries space domain training authority. Establish coordination mechanism with MDWC.

---

### 4.5 Cyber — EXTERNAL

**Current State:** No capability. Not an MDWC requirement.

**Position:** Cyber domain training sits with a TBC organisation within the UAE defence establishment. Basic cyber awareness should be embedded in MDWC staff preparation. Formal cyber training is not an MDWC responsibility.

> **HQ Action Required:** Confirm which organisation carries cyber domain training authority. Establish coordination mechanism with MDWC.

---

### 4.6 EMS — FUTURE INTEGRATION REQUIREMENT

**Current State:** No EMS training capability. No EW systems or threat emitters at Hameem. No spectrum management framework. UXS platforms currently tested without EMS threat representation.

**Key Gaps:**
- No EMS threat emulation capability (jammers, GPS spoofers) for UXS T&E
- No EMS training programme or awareness curriculum
- No spectrum management plan for Hameem
- No EW planning integration into exercise design
- MAKHLAB UXS platforms not tested against EMS threats
- No EW/EMS SME cadre within MDWC

**What's Needed:**
- Portable EMS threat emulation systems (jammers, GPS spoofers — safety-assured)
- Spectrum management plan and frequency deconfliction process for Hameem
- EMS awareness training module in all MDWC programmes
- EW/EMS SME cadre (organic or via support agreement)
- UXS T&E SOPs incorporating EMS threat scenarios
- EW standard injects in collective exercise design
- Coordination with MAKHLAB project offices

**Position:** Near-term planning priority. UXS T&E without realistic EMS threats is operationally unassured — this directly undermines the credibility of all MAKHLAB project validation outcomes. Cannot be deferred.

## 7. Findings & Recommendations

### Finding 1 — Land: Formalise What Exists

Land is the anchor domain and the MDWC's established core. Gaps are in formalisation, not foundational capability. The Tactical Adaptation Platform concept addresses several gaps and must be moved to implementation.

**Recommendation:** Formalise the collective training validation framework. Advance the Tactical Adaptation Platform concept to implementation as the institutional standard.

### Finding 2 — Air: Most Urgent Growth Area

UAS and C-UAS represent the most significant gap between current capability and future requirement. MAKHLAB project T&E demands make this the highest-priority investment area.

**Recommendation:** Develop a dedicated UAS training and T&E programme. Establish restricted airspace, RF spectrum management, and safety protocols at Hameem immediately. Align T&E framework with MAKHLAB project offices.

### Finding 3 — EMS: Cannot Be Deferred

Testing UXS platforms without realistic EMS threat environments produces results that are operationally unassured. This is a T&E credibility issue, not an aspiration.

**Recommendation:** Initiate EMS capability planning for Hameem now. Acquire portable threat emulation systems as priority. Embed EMS into all UXS T&E SOPs.

### Finding 4 — Maritime: Requires HQ Decision

No standing requirement assessed. FDC5 may create one. MDWC cannot make this determination.

**Recommendation:** Refer to PG HQ for decision. MDWC to prepare options brief if directed.

### Finding 5 — Space & Cyber: Sit Outside MDWC

Both domains are responsibilities of other organisations (TBC). MDWC should acknowledge them in exercise design without assuming ownership.

**Recommendation:** PG HQ to confirm institutional authority and establish coordination mechanisms with MDWC.

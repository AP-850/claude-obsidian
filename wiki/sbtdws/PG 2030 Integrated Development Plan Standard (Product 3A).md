# PG 2030 Integrated Development Plan Standard (Product 3A)

**PG-wide planning standard, not an SBTDWS document.** Authored by [[Frank]] (lead advisor to J3), shared for review 2026-08-09. Defines the common method every PG2030 Working Group, Unit, and Staff Directorate must use to turn approved tasks into a phased, measurable Integrated Development Plan (IDP) — current state → IOC → FOC by 2030.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[sources/pg2030-product-3a-integrated-development-plan]] | [[Frank]]

---

## The 9-Step Standard Planning Logic

Sequential — complete in order, then continuously update as decisions, dependencies, and evidence mature.

1. **Confirm assigned tasks** — concur or recommend changes; PG J3 approval required. *No organisation may independently change its LOEs without PG J3 approval.*
2. **Establish approved LOEs** — approved tasks become the plan's Lines of Effort.
3. **Baseline each LOE** — current state + principal gap. Concise; a planning reference, not a history.
4. **Define FOC** — the end-of-Phase-3 destination: capability, scale/echelon, operational effect, purpose, conditions/integration, sustainability.
5. **Define IOC** — work backward from FOC. The *minimum usable and demonstrable* capability at end of Phase 2 — not a percentage of completion.
6. **Develop sub-tasks** — directed + implied work to close baseline → IOC → FOC; brief PG J3.
7. **Set KPIs by phase** — measurable indicators for Phase 1/2/3.
8. **Build the roadmap** — milestones, contributors, dependencies, constraints, decisions through 2029.
9. **Execute and review** — track performance, update risks/issues, request decisions, refine.

**Two approval gates:** Gate 1 — PG J3 approves the authoritative LOEs. Gate 2 — PG J3 approves the sub-task structure beneath them.

## FOC / IOC Definition Method

**FOC** answers: *what must PG be capable of doing, at what scale, to create what effect, for what purpose, under what conditions, by end of Phase 3?* Minimum content: Capability/Function, Scale/Echelon, Operational Effect, Purpose, Conditions/Integration, Sustainability/Institutionalisation.

> **Weak:** "Complete development of the deployable HQ." (describes an activity as complete)
> **Strong:** "PG possesses a trained, equipped and validated deployable 1-star JTF HQ capable of commanding a mission-specific force package, integrating required PG and Joint Force enablers, and sustaining continuous operational C2 at the required notice to move." (defines capability, echelon, effect, purpose, integration, usable end condition)

**IOC** uses the same logic at the initial threshold — worked backward from FOC, not forward from today. Example progression: IOC = *"One deployable HQ cadre can plan and command a representative mission during a controlled exercise using an initial communications and augmentation package"* → FOC = *"The required headquarters are fully manned, trained, interoperable, sustainable and validated for their assigned mission sets and readiness requirements."*

## KPI Discipline

A KPI needs: a specific measure, a target/threshold, a due date/phase, an accountable owner, an evidence source, and a status method (On Track/At Risk/Off Track, or similar). **Rule: if a KPI can't be objectively assessed, it's probably too vague.**

> **Weak:** "Improve manning." → **Measurable:** "By end of Phase 1, 100% of critical billets have approved SQEP criteria and a baseline of authorized, assigned and qualified personnel; by IOC, at least 90% of designated critical billets are filled by qualified personnel." (uses a denominator, separates baseline from performance)

Minimum KPI table columns: KPI | Target/Threshold | Phase/Due Date | Responsible Owner | Evidence/Data Source | Status.

## SRO / Plan Lead Accountability Model

- **SRO (Senior Responsible Officer):** accountable for delivery. Sets/protects end state and priorities, approves plan direction before it goes upward, owns major risks and cross-organisational dependencies, engages senior stakeholders, holds the Plan Lead accountable.
- **Plan Lead / OIC:** day-to-day integration and orchestration on the SRO's behalf. Maintains the plan, tracks KPIs/milestones/dependencies, elevates issues early. **Continuity expectation: minimum 12 months in post.**
- Every plan names an SRO, a Plan Lead/OIC, and a deputy/alternate where appropriate.

## Risk / Issue / Dependency / Decision Taxonomy

A clean four-way distinction, worth comparing against SBTDWS's own tracking conventions:

| Category | Key Question | Required Response |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge / Gap | What condition is making delivery harder? | Describe impact and mitigation/assistance required |
| Risk | What might happen? | Assess, mitigate, monitor, assign an owner |
| Issue | What is already happening? | Act now, assign an owner, escalate if required |
| Dependency | What do I need from another owner, by when? | Specify provider, output, date, impact if late |
| Decision | What senior choice is required? | State the decision needed, by whom, by when |

> [!note] SBTDWS's own registers use a looser version of this
> [[Commander Decisions Register]] and [[Gap and Issue Triage Register]] track decisions and gaps but don't consistently separate Risk (uncertain future) from Issue (already happening) from Dependency (someone else's output) the way this taxonomy does. Worth adopting this exact four-way split once Product 3A is formally in force, to avoid SBTDWS's own tracking reading as inconsistent against the PG-wide standard at review time.

## Review & Recommendations

Prepared for the user to feed back to Frank, per his request for input. Finalised 2026-08-09 — user reviewed and scoped down; see decisions below.

**Strengths, worth acknowledging directly:**
- Genuinely well-built capability-planning discipline — LOE → baseline → IOC/FOC → KPI → SRO accountability maps cleanly onto established frameworks (DOTMLPF, JCIDS/CBA, NATO NDPP) the user has already researched in depth for TACTIX/CPT — see [[CPT Framework Research]], [[CPT Reference Frameworks]]. This is a real point of connection worth naming to Frank: SBTDWS/TACTIX work already has comparable planning-framework research that could sanity-check or extend Product 3A, and vice versa.
- The weak-vs-strong FOC and KPI examples are excellent — concrete, teachable, and exactly the kind of thing that actually changes how people write plans, not just what they're told to include.
- The Risk/Issue/Dependency/Decision taxonomy is sharper than SBTDWS's own current practice (see above) — a genuine methodology upgrade opportunity, not just new paperwork.

**Gaps and questions to raise with Frank (final list):**

1. **No stated mechanism for cross-organisational LOEs with no natural single SRO.** The document's SRO/Plan Lead model assumes one accountable owner per LOE — but PG2030 already has LOEs that genuinely span multiple organisations (Multi-Domain Targeting across J2/J3/NCTJC/units; the Digital Spine across J6 and every consuming unit; PG innovation governance itself — see [[PG Innovation Governance Framework (Deep Dive)]]'s "own the idea, not the work" finding). Worth asking Frank directly how Product 3A expects genuinely cross-cutting LOEs to name a single SRO without recreating the same stovepipe risk already flagged elsewhere.
2. **No described mechanism for rolling up KPIs across dozens of independent IDPs into one PG-wide picture.** Each plan's KPI table is well-specified individually, but nothing here addresses how PG J3 aggregates or compares across WGs/Units/Staff Directorates consistently. Worth asking whether that's a separate Product (3B, or similar) or an intentional gap for now.
3. **Section 12's "TBD" allowance for validation method has a known failure mode.** Reasonable for early planning — but SBTDWS's own experience with SAG 9/10 validation authority (still unresolved, see [[Commander Decisions Register]] D-05) shows "TBD" can persist well past when it should have converted into an active Decision item. Suggest Product 3A add an explicit TBD-aging rule — e.g., a validation method still marked TBD by the Phase 2 IOC gate automatically becomes a required Decision item, not something that can silently ride to FOC.
4. **"Product 3A" implies a series** — simple query to Frank on what else is coming (3B, or a 1/2/3 sequence).

**Dropped from the feedback (user decision, 2026-08-09):**
- *FOC/IOC date conflict* — not being raised with Frank. Treated as planning guidance he set himself (likely derived from AJ3), not a drafting error. Working assumption is now FOC = 31 Dec 2029 unless later direction changes it — see the resolved note above.
- *SBTDWS retrofitting question* — out of scope. Product 3A is a PG-wide document flagging issues likely universal across all units, not something requiring an SBTDWS-specific compliance answer from Frank right now.

# CTC-L and JTW — Australian Reference Model

The Australian Combined Training Centre - Land (CTC-L) and its relationship to the Joint Training Wing (JTW) is the primary reference model for ADWC transformation. The Australian construct solved almost exactly the same structural problem that SBTDWS faces.

Navigation: [[sbtdws/_index]] | [[ADWC Three Wing Structure]] | [[ADWC Transformation Concept]]

---

## Why This Model Matters

The Australian CTC solved the same problem ADWC faces: an enterprise whose centre of gravity had been a specialised environmental wing (JTW) that needed to be repositioned as a feeder while a collective training and certification capability became the main effort.

That transition is the roadmap.

---

## The Australian Structure

### Joint Training Wing (JTW)

The specialised environmental training wing — the equivalent of what SBTDWS does today as a Desert Warfare school.

JTW's role in the Australian model:
- Delivers specialist, environment-specific individual and small-team training
- Feeds personnel into the collective training pipeline
- Positioned as a **feeder**, not the main effort

**Key distinction from SBTDWS:** JTW was always geographically and institutionally separate from CTC-L. It never had to manage its own identity subordination within the same enterprise. This makes the SBTDWS cultural challenge harder — the Desert Warfare Wing is transitioning *within* the same institution, not as a separate entity.

---

### CTC-L — Combined Training Centre - Land

The main effort. The collective training and certification function. The Australian answer to "how do forces get validated as ready?"

CTC-L's three non-negotiable pillars (all interdependent):

| Pillar | What it is | Why it is non-negotiable |
|--------|-----------|--------------------------|
| OPFOR | Dedicated opposing force capability | Without credible OPFOR, collective exercises don't generate learning — they generate confirmation bias |
| OC/T Cadre | Observer/Controller/Trainer cadre | The human element that translates exercise events into learning; determines whether training delivers understanding or just activity |
| AAR Process | Structured After Action Review | The mechanism that converts observed behaviour into corrective insight; must be rigorous and non-punitive |

These three pillars are interdependent — a weakness in any one degrades the other two.

---

### CTC 2025 Concept — Experimentation Gesture

The Australians acknowledge a tactical experimentation function in their CTC 2025 Concept but have not formalised it as a wing. This is a gap in their model that ADWC can deliberately exceed.

---

## Key Lesson for SBTDWS

> The JTW → CTC-L transition is not a demotion of the environmental wing. It is a recognition that individual specialist training is the *input* to collective readiness, not the *output*. CTC-L measures outputs: can this formation fight?

The Australian model proves this transition is achievable and credible — it does not require dismantling existing capability, only repositioning it.
